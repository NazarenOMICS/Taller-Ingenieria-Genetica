[105]. The proteasome system also plays a role in regulating FTO expression. Specifically, FTO undergoes post-translational ubiquitination at the Lys-216 position. Introducing a ubiquitin-deficient K216R mutation at this site slows down FTO turnover in HeLa cells. This leads to increased FTO protein levels and decreased nuclear FTO levels, ultimately abolishing FTO nuclear translocation. These findings suggest that the proteasome system regulates FTO stability and intracellular localization by controlling FTO ubiquitination, thereby influencing its function and expression levels within the cell [106].In addition, members of cellular signaling pathways such as the Wnt signaling pathway and factors like STAT3 are also involved in regulating FTO expression.
