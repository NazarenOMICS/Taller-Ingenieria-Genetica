# Q01 - According to these sources, in which non-granulosa tissues has oxidative stress been reported to reduce FTO expression?

Based on the provided sources, **heart tissue (myocardium and cardiomyocytes)** is the primary non-granulosa tissue where oxidative stress has been reported to reduce FTO expression.

The sources provide the following specific evidence regarding this downregulation:

*   **Sepsis and Endotoxemia:** In animal models of endotoxemia and sepsis, as well as in cardiomyocyte cellular experiments, oxidative stress is associated with **reduced FTO expression in cardiomyocytes**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q20-fto-otros-tejidos-cromatina/Sources/chen_2026_m_lt_sup_gt_6_lt_sup_gt.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q20-fto-otros-tejidos-cromatina/Sources/chen_2026_m_lt_sup_gt_6_lt_sup_gt.pdf#Passage 2|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q20-fto-otros-tejidos-cromatina/Sources/chen_2026_m_lt_sup_gt_6_lt_sup_gt.pdf#Passage 3|[3]]]. This suppression of FTO leads to a pronounced increase in global m6A levels, which correlates with myocardial inflammation, contractile failure, and the upregulation of inflammatory cytokines like IL-6 and TNF-α  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q20-fto-otros-tejidos-cromatina/Sources/chen_2026_m_lt_sup_gt_6_lt_sup_gt.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q20-fto-otros-tejidos-cromatina/Sources/chen_2026_m_lt_sup_gt_6_lt_sup_gt.pdf#Passage 2|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q20-fto-otros-tejidos-cromatina/Sources/chen_2026_m_lt_sup_gt_6_lt_sup_gt.pdf#Passage 3|[3]]].
*   **Hypoxia and Heart Failure:** FTO expression has also been reported as **decreased in failing mammalian hearts and hypoxic cardiomyocytes**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q20-fto-otros-tejidos-cromatina/Sources/xiulin_2021_the_role_of_m6a_modification_in_the_biological.pdf#Passage 1|[4]]]. In these contexts, the downregulation of FTO is associated with impaired contractility, disrupted calcium homeostasis, and elevated global m6A levels  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q20-fto-otros-tejidos-cromatina/Sources/xiulin_2021_the_role_of_m6a_modification_in_the_biological.pdf#Passage 1|[4]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q20-fto-otros-tejidos-cromatina/Sources/chen_2026_m_lt_sup_gt_6_lt_sup_gt.pdf#Passage 4|[5]]].
*   **Cardioprotective Role:** The sources emphasize that FTO normally exerts cardioprotective effects under stress—such as ischemia-reperfusion—by stabilizing transcripts for anti-apoptotic and calcium-handling proteins  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q20-fto-otros-tejidos-cromatina/Sources/chen_2026_m_lt_sup_gt_6_lt_sup_gt.pdf#Passage 4|[5]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q20-fto-otros-tejidos-cromatina/Sources/chen_2026_m_lt_sup_gt_6_lt_sup_gt.pdf#Passage 5|[6]]]. Consequently, its depletion or suppression by persistent oxidative stress exacerbates cardiac injury and promotes cardiomyocyte loss through pathways like ferroptosis and apoptosis  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q20-fto-otros-tejidos-cromatina/Sources/chen_2026_m_lt_sup_gt_6_lt_sup_gt.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q20-fto-otros-tejidos-cromatina/Sources/chen_2026_m_lt_sup_gt_6_lt_sup_gt.pdf#Passage 2|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q20-fto-otros-tejidos-cromatina/Sources/chen_2026_m_lt_sup_gt_6_lt_sup_gt.pdf#Passage 6|[7]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q20-fto-otros-tejidos-cromatina/Sources/li_2026_the_role_of_n_lt_sup_gt_6.pdf#Passage 1|[8]]].

While the sources also discuss FTO in other tissues, the effects of oxidative stress on its expression vary or are not as explicitly linked to a reduction in the provided excerpts:
*   **Lung Tissue:** Conversely, in some lung injury models, obesity-induced **upregulation** of FTO has been reported to aggravate LPS-induced acute lung injury  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q20-fto-otros-tejidos-cromatina/Sources/li_2026_the_role_of_n_lt_sup_gt_6.pdf#Passage 2|[9]]].
*   **Brain Tissue:** While FTO is downregulated in the **hippocampus** in models of depression  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q20-fto-otros-tejidos-cromatina/Sources/li_2026_the_role_of_n_lt_sup_gt_6.pdf#Passage 3|[10]]], the sources primarily focus on the effects of FTO *depletion* in the brain (such as increased neuronal damage in stroke) rather than explicitly stating that oxidative stress is the agent that reduces its expression in that specific tissue  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q20-fto-otros-tejidos-cromatina/Sources/li_2026_the_role_of_n_lt_sup_gt_6.pdf#Passage 4|[11]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q20-fto-otros-tejidos-cromatina/Sources/xiulin_2021_the_role_of_m6a_modification_in_the_biological.pdf#Passage 2|[12]]].
