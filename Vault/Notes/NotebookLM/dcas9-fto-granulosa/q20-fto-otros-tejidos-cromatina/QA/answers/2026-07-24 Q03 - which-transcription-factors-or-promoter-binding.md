# Q03 - Which transcription factors or promoter-binding regulators were implicated in controlling FTO expression in those non-granulosa stress models?

In several non-granulosa stress models—most notably in cardiac and metabolic tissues—the regulation of **FTO** expression at the transcriptional and promoter levels involves specific transcription factors and metabolic feedback loops.

The following transcription factors and regulators have been implicated:

*   **SP1 (Specificity Protein 1):** In cardiomyocytes, SP1 is identified as a positive regulator that directly **activates the transcription of FTO**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q20-fto-otros-tejidos-cromatina/Sources/li_2026_the_role_of_n_lt_sup_gt_6.pdf#Passage 5|[1]]]. This activation is part of a protective response to oxidative stress; once transcribed, FTO removes m6A marks from *Ambra1* mRNA, stabilizing it to trigger ULK1-mediated autophagy flux, which helps mitigate oxidative damage  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q20-fto-otros-tejidos-cromatina/Sources/li_2026_the_role_of_n_lt_sup_gt_6.pdf#Passage 5|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q20-fto-otros-tejidos-cromatina/Sources/li_2026_the_role_of_n_lt_sup_gt_6.pdf#Passage 6|[2]]].
*   **CEBPA (CCAAT Enhancer Binding Protein Alpha):** In the context of dilated cardiomyopathy and leukemia models, the metabolite **R-2-hydroxyglutaric acid (R-2HG)** reduces FTO expression through a **positive feedback loop driven by CEBPA suppression**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q20-fto-otros-tejidos-cromatina/Sources/wang_2026_methylation_centric_epigenetic_regulation_in_dilated_cardiomyopathy_mechanisms.pdf#Passage 1|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q20-fto-otros-tejidos-cromatina/Sources/chen_2026_m_lt_sup_gt_6_lt_sup_gt.pdf#Passage 9|[4]]]. Specifically, R-2HG inhibits FTO’s enzymatic activity first, which eventually leads to the downregulation of *CEBPA* transcripts; the loss of CEBPA, in turn, inhibits the transcription of the *FTO* gene itself  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q20-fto-otros-tejidos-cromatina/Sources/wang_2026_methylation_centric_epigenetic_regulation_in_dilated_cardiomyopathy_mechanisms.pdf#Passage 1|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q20-fto-otros-tejidos-cromatina/Sources/chen_2026_m_lt_sup_gt_6_lt_sup_gt.pdf#Passage 9|[4]]].
*   **NRF2 (Nuclear Factor Erythroid 2-Related Factor 2):** While direct evidence in the heart during sepsis is still limited, studies in other non-cardiac inflammatory systems have reported that certain antioxidant agents (such as quercetin or resveratrol) influence the m6A network via an **NRF2–FTO pathway**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q20-fto-otros-tejidos-cromatina/Sources/li_2026_the_role_of_n_lt_sup_gt_6.pdf#Passage 4|[5]]]. FTO is also known to inhibit oxidative stress by mediating the m6A demethylation of *Nrf2* mRNA, creating a regulatory link between this master antioxidant factor and FTO levels  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q20-fto-otros-tejidos-cromatina/Sources/li_2026_the_role_of_n_lt_sup_gt_6.pdf#Passage 7|[6]]].
*   **FOXO1 (Forkhead Box O1):** FTO is implicated in a metabolic regulatory axis with FOXO1  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q20-fto-otros-tejidos-cromatina/Sources/li_2026_the_role_of_n_lt_sup_gt_6.pdf#Passage 8|[7]]]. For example, the drug entacapone acts as an FTO inhibitor that modulates body weight and blood glucose by targeting the **FTO-FOXO1 axis** in an m6A-dependent manner  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q20-fto-otros-tejidos-cromatina/Sources/li_2026_the_role_of_n_lt_sup_gt_6.pdf#Passage 8|[7]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q20-fto-otros-tejidos-cromatina/Sources/chen_2026_dna_methylation_a_key_epigenetic_regulator_of_cardiovascular.pdf#Passage 2|[8]]].
*   **Promoter Methylation Dynamics:** Beyond specific protein factors, the physical state of the *FTO* promoter is a major regulator. **Hypomethylation of the FTO promoter** (often induced by a high-fat diet or physical inactivity) markedly upregulates its expression, whereas **promoter hypermethylation** silences the gene  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q20-fto-otros-tejidos-cromatina/Sources/chen_2026_dna_methylation_a_key_epigenetic_regulator_of_cardiovascular.pdf#Passage 1|[9]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q20-fto-otros-tejidos-cromatina/Sources/li_2026_the_role_of_n_lt_sup_gt_6.pdf#Passage 9|[10]]].

While the sources also discuss **HIF1α** as a major transcriptional driver of metabolic reprogramming under stress, they primarily highlight FTO's role in **stabilizing HIF1α transcripts** (e.g., in adipose tissue browning) rather than HIF1α acting as a direct promoter-binding regulator of FTO  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q20-fto-otros-tejidos-cromatina/Sources/li_2026_the_role_of_n_lt_sup_gt_6.pdf#Passage 10|[11]]]. Conversely, in hypoxia models, HIF1α is reported to directly transactivate **ALKBH5** (another m6A eraser), but a similar direct link to *FTO* transcription is not explicitly detailed in these excerpts [12].
