### Passage 1

Eraser: DNA demethylase Strictly speaking, no enzyme in humans could be called ‘DNA demethylase’. The TET family members, belonging to an evolutionarily conserved family of dioxygenases, play key regulatory roles during DNA demethylation (Fig. 1). On TET catalysis, m5C is hydroxylated into 5-hydroxymethyl cytosine (5hmC) followed by further oxidisation into 5-formylcytosine and 5-carbox-ylcytosine. Thymine DNA glycosylase further converts m5C into cytosine through the base-excision repair pathway (Ref. 14).
