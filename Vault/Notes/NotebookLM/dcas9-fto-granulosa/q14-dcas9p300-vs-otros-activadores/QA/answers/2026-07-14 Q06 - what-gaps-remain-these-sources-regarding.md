# Q06 - What gaps remain in these sources regarding a direct head-to-head comparison of dCas9-p300 versus VP64, VPR, and SunTag-p300 specifically at the FTO locus?

Based on the provided sources, there is a total absence of information regarding the **FTO locus**, and consequently, several significant gaps exist regarding a direct head-to-head comparison of dCas9-p300, VP64, VPR, and SunTag-p300 at this specific site.

The following gaps remain in the sources:

### **1. Absence of FTO-Specific Data**
None of the 17 sources provided mention the **FTO (Fat Mass and Obesity-associated) locus** [1–523]. The experimental targets in these studies are confined to:
*   **Pancreatic/Developmental genes:** *PDX1, NGN3, INS, MAFA, NEUROD1*  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/fatma_2025_comparison_of_dcas9_activator_complexes_for_the_activation.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/fatma_2025_comparison_of_dcas9_activator_complexes_for_the_activation.pdf#Passage 17|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/kohei_2022_comparative_analysis_of_dcas9_vp64_variants_and_multiplexed.pdf#Passage 9|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/agustin_2023_epigenetic_reactivation_of_tumor_suppressor_genes_with_crispra.pdf#Passage 5|[4]]].
*   **Tumor Suppressors:** *HHIP, MT1M, PZP, TTC36, CPS1, CDKN2A*  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/agustin_2023_epigenetic_reactivation_of_tumor_suppressor_genes_with_crispra.pdf#Passage 1|[5]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/agustin_2023_epigenetic_reactivation_of_tumor_suppressor_genes_with_crispra.pdf#Passage 4|[6]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/albert_2017_genetic_and_epigenetic_control_of_gene_expression_by.pdf#Passage 5|[7]]].

### **2. Lack of SunTag-p300 Comparisons**
While the sources compare **dCas9-p300** to **VP64** and **VPR** as direct fusions, they do not provide a head-to-head comparison that includes **SunTag-p300**. 
*   **Source 8 (Fatma et al., 2025)** compares dCas9-p300, VP64, and VPR but does not use the SunTag architecture for any of them  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/kohei_2022_comparative_analysis_of_dcas9_vp64_variants_and_multiplexed.pdf#Passage 12|[17]]].
*   **Source 11 (Kohei et al., 2022)** compares p300 to VP64 variants and SunTag-VP64, but does not evaluate a SunTag-p300 construct  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/agustin_2023_epigenetic_reactivation_of_tumor_suppressor_genes_with_crispra.pdf#Passage 5|[4]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/christian_2018_a_modular_dcas9_suntag_dnmt3a_epigenome_editing_system.pdf#Passage 1|[18]]].
*   **Source 7 (Christian et al., 2018)** evaluates SunTag for **DNMT3A** (methylation) and **TET1** (demethylation) but does not apply it to p300  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/christian_2018_a_modular_dcas9_suntag_dnmt3a_epigenome_editing_system.pdf#Passage 8|[19]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/fatma_2025_comparison_of_dcas9_activator_complexes_for_the_activation.pdf#Passage 2|[20]]].

### **3. Missing Enhancer-Promoter Context at FTO**
A critical mechanistic question for the FTO locus—which often involves distal enhancer-promoter interactions—cannot be answered by these sources. Although the sources note that **dCas9-p300 is uniquely capable of activating genes from distal enhancers** while VP64 and VPR are largely promoter-centric, there is no data to confirm if this advantage holds true at the FTO locus  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/mazhar_2018_the_crispr_tool_kit_for_genome_editing_and.pdf#Passage 1|[21]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/kohei_2022_comparative_analysis_of_dcas9_vp64_variants_and_multiplexed.pdf#Passage 6|[22]]].

### **4. Comparative Toxicity and Scaling at FTO**
The sources discuss potential **in vivo toxicity** for VPR and SAM ( tripartite/bipartite activators) compared to the better-tolerated dCas9-VP64, but they do not provide data on whether these tools (or p300) would be viable or effective for targeting FTO in a therapeutic or cellular context  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/mahata_2023_compact_engineered_human_mechanosensitive_transactivation_modules_enable_potent.pdf#Passage 1|[23]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/kohei_2022_comparative_analysis_of_dcas9_vp64_variants_and_multiplexed.pdf#Passage 2|[24]]]. Furthermore, the "spatial constraints" noted for the large p300 protein in high-density target regions (like some promoters) are not evaluated for the FTO regulatory landscape  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/kohei_2022_comparative_analysis_of_dcas9_vp64_variants_and_multiplexed.pdf#Passage 7|[25]]].
