# Q04 - According to these sources, does dCas9-p300 leave a different epigenetic 'memory' or persistence signature at the target locus compared to VP64/VPR, given that p300 deposits a histone modification (H3K27ac) while VP64/VPR recruit transcriptional machinery without necessarily modifying chromatin marks?

Based on the sources, **dCas9-p300** does leave a different functional and epigenetic signature at target loci compared to **VP64** or **VPR**, specifically because it enables **sustainable, long-term regulation** rather than just immediate transcriptional recruitment  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/fatma_2025_comparison_of_dcas9_activator_complexes_for_the_activation.pdf#Passage 3|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/fatma_2025_comparison_of_dcas9_activator_complexes_for_the_activation.pdf#Passage 13|[2]]].

### **Sustainability and Long-Term Regulation**
The primary difference in the "persistence" of these tools stems from their fundamental mechanisms:
*   **p300 (Sustainable Activation):** Because dCas9-p300 possesses **histone acetyltransferase (HAT) activity**, it directly modifies the epigenetic landscape by depositing **H3K27ac**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/fatma_2025_comparison_of_dcas9_activator_complexes_for_the_activation.pdf#Passage 3|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/fatma_2025_comparison_of_dcas9_activator_complexes_for_the_activation.pdf#Passage 2|[3]]]. This enzymatic activity opens the local chromatin structure, facilitating a state of "sustainable activation" that does not permanently alter the DNA sequence but changes its accessibility  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/fatma_2025_comparison_of_dcas9_activator_complexes_for_the_activation.pdf#Passage 13|[2]]].
*   **VP64/VPR (Recruitment-Based):** These tools function as synthetic transcription factors that recruit the endogenous machinery (like RNA Polymerase II) to the promoter  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/fatma_2025_comparison_of_dcas9_activator_complexes_for_the_activation.pdf#Passage 3|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/andreas_2020_activation_of_silent_secondary_metabolite_gene_clusters_by.pdf#Passage 2|[4]]]. The sources suggest their effect is largely determined by the **persistent presence of the activator** at the locus  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/andreas_2020_activation_of_silent_secondary_metabolite_gene_clusters_by.pdf#Passage 3|[5]]]. While highly potent, they are often described as tools for "basic" or "high-level" activation rather than sustainable epigenetic remodeling  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/fatma_2025_comparison_of_dcas9_activator_complexes_for_the_activation.pdf#Passage 3|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/fatma_2025_comparison_of_dcas9_activator_complexes_for_the_activation.pdf#Passage 13|[2]]].

### **The Epigenetic 'Memory' Signature**
The sources indicate that epigenetic modifications like those deposited by p300 provide a mechanism for **heritable gene expression**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/albert_2017_genetic_and_epigenetic_control_of_gene_expression_by.pdf#Passage 6|[6]]]. However, the persistence of this "memory" for gene activation is subject to several constraints:
*   **DNA Methylation Status:** The maintenance of a reactivated state after targeted epigenetic editing is strongly dependent on the **DNA methylation status of CpG islands** at the promoter  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/albert_2017_genetic_and_epigenetic_control_of_gene_expression_by.pdf#Passage 3|[7]]]. Gene expression is more likely to be maintained at hypomethylated loci, whereas it may be only transient at hypermethylated loci  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/albert_2017_genetic_and_epigenetic_control_of_gene_expression_by.pdf#Passage 3|[7]]].
*   **Combinatorial Requirements:** For a truly stable "memory" signature (particularly for silencing), the recruitment of multiple modifiers—such as combining DNA methyltransferases (DNMT3A) with histone modifiers (KRAB or EZH2)—is often required to achieve **persistent and inheritable** changes  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/albert_2017_genetic_and_epigenetic_control_of_gene_expression_by.pdf#Passage 7|[8]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/tessa_2022_a_modular_dcas9_based_recruitment_platform_for_combinatorial.pdf#Passage 3|[9]]].
*   **Direct Modification vs. Recruitment:** While VP64/VPR can achieve robust upregulation, they do not necessarily reprogram the underlying chromatin marks in the same way as p300  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/fatma_2025_comparison_of_dcas9_activator_complexes_for_the_activation.pdf#Passage 3|[1]]]. p300’s unique ability to activate genes from **both promoters and enhancers** by increasing H3K27ac marks suggests a more versatile and durable epigenetic impact on regulatory elements  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/albert_2017_genetic_and_epigenetic_control_of_gene_expression_by.pdf#Passage 1|[10]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/mazhar_2018_the_crispr_tool_kit_for_genome_editing_and.pdf#Passage 1|[11]]].
