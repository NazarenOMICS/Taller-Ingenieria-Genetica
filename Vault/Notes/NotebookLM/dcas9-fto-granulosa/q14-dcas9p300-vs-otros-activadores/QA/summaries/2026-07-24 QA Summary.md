## Q02
- Pregunta: According to these sources, what is the mechanistic difference between dCas9-p300 (direct histone acetyltransferase activity) and VP64/VPR (transcription factor domain recruitment) in how they activate gene expression?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/QA/answers/2026-07-24 Q02 - according-to-these-sources-what-mechanistic.md]]

Based on the sources, the fundamental mechanistic difference between **dCas9-p300** and **VP64/VPR** is that the former functions as an **enzymatic epigenetic modifier**, while the latter act as **synthetic transcription factors** that physically recruit cellular machinery.

## Q04
- Pregunta: According to these sources, does dCas9-p300 leave a different epigenetic 'memory' or persistence signature at the target locus compared to VP64/VPR, given that p300 deposits a histone modification (H3K27ac) while VP64/VPR recruit transcriptional machinery without necessarily modifying chromatin marks?

Based on the sources, **dCas9-p300** leaves a distinct functional and epigenetic signature at target loci compared to **VP64** and **VPR** because it acts as an **enzymatic modifier** that enables **sustainable, long-term regulation** rather than relying solely on the physical recruitment of the transcriptional machinery  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/fatma_2025_comparison_of_dcas9_activator_complexes_for_the_activation.pdf#Passage 18|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/fatma_2025_comparison_of_dcas9_activator_complexes_for_the_activation.pdf#Passage 14|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/kohei_2022_comparative_analysis_of_dcas9_vp64_variants_and_multiplexed.pdf#Passage 9|[3]]].

The sources indicate that different CRISPR activators exhibit significant **context-dependence**, varying widely in their effectiveness based on whether they target promoters or enhancers, the local nucleosome landscape, and the basal expression state of the target gene.
