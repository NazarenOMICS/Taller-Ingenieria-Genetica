## Q01
- Pregunta: According to these sources (e.g., comparison of dCas9 activator complexes), how does dCas9-p300 compare to VP64 and VPR in terms of activation magnitude and target-gene applicability?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/QA/answers/2026-07-14 Q01 - according-to-these-sources-e-g.md]]

Based on the sources, **dCas9-p300** serves as a potent epigenetic modifier that compares variably to **VP64** and **VPR** depending on whether it is targeted to a promoter or an enhancer and the specific gene involved  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/albert_2017_genetic_and_epigenetic_control_of_gene_expression_by.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/chenya_2021_spatiotemporal_control_of_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 1|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/fatma_2025_comparison_of_dcas9_activator_complexes_for_the_activation.pdf#Passage 1|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/fatma_2025_comparison_of_dcas9_activator_complexes_for_the_activation.pdf#Passage 2|[4]]].
## Q02
- Pregunta: According to these sources, what is the mechanistic difference between dCas9-p300 (direct histone acetyltransferase activity) and VP64/VPR (transcription factor domain recruitment) in how they activate gene expression?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/QA/answers/2026-07-14 Q02 - according-to-these-sources-what-mechanistic.md]]

The sources identify a fundamental mechanistic distinction between **dCas9-p300**, which functions as an **epigenetic modifier**, and **VP64/VPR**, which act as **synthetic transcription factors**.
## Q03
- Pregunta: According to these sources (e.g., modular dCas9-SunTag systems), how does the SunTag signal-amplification architecture change the potency or specificity of a recruited effector (e.g., p300) compared to a direct 1:1 dCas9-effector fusion?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/QA/answers/2026-07-14 Q03 - according-to-these-sources-e-g.md]]

The **SunTag** signal-amplification architecture fundamentally alters the performance of recruited effectors by transitioning from a **1:1 dCas9-effector ratio** to a **multivalent scaffold** capable of recruiting many effectors to a single genomic target  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/albert_2017_genetic_and_epigenetic_control_of_gene_expression_by.pdf#Passage 5|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/tessa_2022_a_modular_dcas9_based_recruitment_platform_for_combinatorial.pdf#Passage 1|[2]]]. This shift significantly enhances **potency** through signal amplification while improving **specificity** by allowing for independent tuning of system components  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/christian_2018_a_modular_dcas9_suntag_dnmt3a_epigenome_editing_system.pdf#Passage 1|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/christian_2018_a_modular_dcas9_suntag_dnmt3a_epigenome_editing_system.pdf#Passage 2|[4]]].
## Q04
- Pregunta: According to these sources, does dCas9-p300 leave a different epigenetic 'memory' or persistence signature at the target locus compared to VP64/VPR, given that p300 deposits a histone modification (H3K27ac) while VP64/VPR recruit transcriptional machinery without necessarily modifying chromatin marks?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/QA/answers/2026-07-14 Q04 - according-to-these-sources-does-dcas9.md]]

Based on the sources, **dCas9-p300** does leave a different functional and epigenetic signature at target loci compared to **VP64** or **VPR**, specifically because it enables **sustainable, long-term regulation** rather than just immediate transcriptional recruitment  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/fatma_2025_comparison_of_dcas9_activator_complexes_for_the_activation.pdf#Passage 3|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/fatma_2025_comparison_of_dcas9_activator_complexes_for_the_activation.pdf#Passage 13|[2]]].
## Q05
- Pregunta: According to these sources, do these different CRISPR activators (dCas9-p300, VP64, VPR, SunTag) show different context-dependence on promoter/enhancer state or chromatin accessibility at the target locus?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/QA/answers/2026-07-14 Q05 - according-to-these-sources-do-these.md]]

The sources indicate that CRISPR activators exhibit significant differences in context-dependence, particularly regarding their ability to function at different regulatory elements (promoters vs. enhancers), their sensitivity to nucleosome positioning, and their performance in silent versus active chromatin states.
