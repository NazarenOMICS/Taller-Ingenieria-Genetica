## Hilo central del bloque

- Based on the provided sources, the comparison between **dCas9-p300**, **VP64**, and **VPR** depends heavily on the target gene, the regulatory element involved (promoter vs. enhancer), and the desired duration of activation.
- Based on the sources, the fundamental mechanistic difference between **dCas9-p300** and **VP64/VPR** is that the former functions as an **enzymatic epigenetic modifier**, while the latter act as **synthetic transcription factors** that physically recruit cellular machinery.
- The **SunTag** signal-amplification architecture fundamentally transforms the performance of recruited effectors by replacing the **1:1 dCas9-effector ratio** with a **multivalent protein scaffold** capable of recruiting many effector molecules to a single genomic locus  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/albert_2017_genetic_and_epigenetic_control_of_gene_expression_by.pdf#Passage 6|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/christian_2018_a_modular_dcas9_suntag_dnmt3a_epigenome_editing_system.pdf#Passage 1|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/christi...
- Based on the sources, **dCas9-p300** leaves a distinct functional and epigenetic signature at target loci compared to **VP64** and **VPR** because it acts as an **enzymatic modifier** that enables **sustainable, long-term regulation** rather than relying solely on the physical recruitment of the transcriptional machinery  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/fatma_2025_comparison_of_dcas9_activator_complexes_for_the_activation.pdf#Passage 18|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q14-dcas9p300-vs-otros-activadores/Sources/fatma_2025_comparison_of_dcas9_activator_complexes_for_the_activation.pdf#Passage 14|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-g...
- The sources indicate that different CRISPR activators exhibit significant **context-dependence**, varying widely in their effectiveness based on whether they target promoters or enhancers, the local nucleosome landscape, and the basal expression state of the target gene.
- Based on the provided sources, there are several significant gaps regarding a direct head-to-head comparison of **dCas9-p300**, **VP64**, **VPR**, and **SunTag-p300** at the **FTO locus**.
