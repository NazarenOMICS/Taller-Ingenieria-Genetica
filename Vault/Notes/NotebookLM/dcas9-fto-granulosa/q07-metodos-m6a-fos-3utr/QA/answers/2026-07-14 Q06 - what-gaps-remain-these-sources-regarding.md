# Q06 - What gaps remain in these sources regarding methods specifically validated for quantifying m6A on the FOS 3'UTR and its effect on FOS mRNA stability, as opposed to m6A methodology in general?

Based on the provided sources, several gaps remain regarding methods specifically validated for quantifying m6A on the *FOS* (or *c-Fos*) 3'UTR and its subsequent effect on mRNA stability. While the sources discuss *FOS* in the context of ovarian aging and myocardial infarction, the following gaps in methodology and validation are evident:

### **1. Contradictory Findings on Functional m6A Regions**
There is a significant gap in reconciling which region of the *FOS* transcript—the 5′UTR or the 3′UTR—is the primary site for functional m6A modification. 
*   **The 3′UTR Focus:** One source identifies the **3′UTR of *FOS*** as the key target for the demethylase **FTO** in human ovarian granulosa cells  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q07-metodos-m6a-fos-3utr/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 4|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q07-metodos-m6a-fos-3utr/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 2|[2]]]. It uses MeRIP-seq to identify peaks and a dual-luciferase reporter assay to validate that m6A in the 3′UTR reinforces *FOS* mRNA stability  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q07-metodos-m6a-fos-3utr/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 5|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q07-metodos-m6a-fos-3utr/Sources/hao_2024_mettl3_drives_heart_failure_by_regulating_spp1_and.pdf#Passage 3|[4]]].
*   **The 5′UTR Focus:** Conversely, a more recent study on myocardial infarction reports that **METTL3** deposits m6A into the **5′UTR of *Fos*** (specifically at position 786) to maintain its stability  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q07-metodos-m6a-fos-3utr/Sources/hao_2024_mettl3_drives_heart_failure_by_regulating_spp1_and.pdf#Passage 1|[5]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q07-metodos-m6a-fos-3utr/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 6|[6]]].
*   **The Gap:** The sources do not address whether these are tissue-specific differences or if the different methods used (**MeRIP-seq/qPCR** for the 3′UTR vs. **SELECT** and site-specific reporters for the 5′UTR) resulted in one study overlooking modifications in the other region  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q07-metodos-m6a-fos-3utr/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 5|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q07-metodos-m6a-fos-3utr/Sources/alexa_2020_limits_in_the_detection_of_m6a_changes_using.pdf#Passage 2|[7]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q07-metodos-m6a-fos-3utr/Sources/alexa_2020_limits_in_the_detection_of_m6a_changes_using.pdf#Passage 3|[8]]].

### **2. Absence of Absolute Stoichiometry Data**
While the sources use **MeRIP-qPCR** and **SELECT** to show relative changes in *FOS* methylation  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q07-metodos-m6a-fos-3utr/Sources/alexa_2020_limits_in_the_detection_of_m6a_changes_using.pdf#Passage 2|[7]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q07-metodos-m6a-fos-3utr/Sources/alexa_2020_limits_in_the_detection_of_m6a_changes_using.pdf#Passage 3|[8]]], they do not provide **absolute stoichiometry** (the precise fraction of *FOS* transcripts that are modified at a specific site). 
*   **Methodological Limit:** General methodological sources in the notebook explicitly state that MeRIP-based methods **cannot determine the precise fraction** of transcript copies that are methylated  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q07-metodos-m6a-fos-3utr/Sources/anna_2023_exclusion_of_m6a_from_splice_site_proximal_regions.pdf#Passage 6|[9]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q07-metodos-m6a-fos-3utr/Sources/neha_2026_a_novel_method_for_the_identification_and_quantification.pdf#Passage 1|[10]]].
*   **The Gap:** Although advanced quantitative methods like **GLORI** or **MAQ-G** are mentioned for other transcripts (like *IRF8* or *TP53*), they have not been specifically applied or validated for the *FOS* 3'UTR in these sources  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q07-metodos-m6a-fos-3utr/Sources/alexa_2020_limits_in_the_detection_of_m6a_changes_using.pdf#Passage 1|[11]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q07-metodos-m6a-fos-3utr/Sources/christopher_2022_detection_of_m6a_from_direct_rna_sequencing_using.pdf#Passage 1|[12]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q07-metodos-m6a-fos-3utr/Sources/mengnuo_2020_the_emerging_roles_of_n6_methyladenosine_m6a_deregulation.pdf#Passage 2|[13]]].

### **3. Lack of Single-Nucleotide Resolution Mapping for Native *FOS***
Although site-specific reporters and predictions were used to narrow down *FOS* modification sites, there is a gap in **single-nucleotide resolution mapping of native *FOS*** transcripts.
*   **Prediction vs. Validation:** Researchers relied on the **SRAMP** server to predict sites in both the 5′UTR and 3′UTR  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q07-metodos-m6a-fos-3utr/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 5|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q07-metodos-m6a-fos-3utr/Sources/alexa_2020_limits_in_the_detection_of_m6a_changes_using.pdf#Passage 2|[7]]].
*   **The Gap:** While **miCLIP** is described as a tool for single-nucleotide discovery, the sources do not provide miCLIP-validated data for *FOS* specifically  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q07-metodos-m6a-fos-3utr/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 3|[14]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q07-metodos-m6a-fos-3utr/Sources/hao_2024_mettl3_drives_heart_failure_by_regulating_spp1_and.pdf#Passage 4|[15]]]. Validation in the sources often relies on **MeRIP-seq**, which only localizes modifications to broad "peak" windows of 50–200 nucleotides  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q07-metodos-m6a-fos-3utr/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 3|[14]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q07-metodos-m6a-fos-3utr/Sources/lei_2023_rna_modification_mechanisms_and_therapeutic_targets.pdf#Passage 1|[16]]].

### **4. Reconciliation of Reader Proteins and Stability Effects**
The sources report conflicting mechanisms for how m6A influences *FOS* stability, leaving a gap in our understanding of the "canonical" regulatory pathway for this transcript.
*   **Stabilizing Readers:** One study suggests that **IGF2BP2** acts as the reader for m6A on the *FOS* 3'UTR, enhancing its stability  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q07-metodos-m6a-fos-3utr/Sources/mengnuo_2020_the_emerging_roles_of_n6_methyladenosine_m6a_deregulation.pdf#Passage 3|[17]]]. Another study suggests **HuR** maintains *Fos* stability by binding to **ARE domains** in the 3'UTR, while METTL3 handles the 5'UTR m6A  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q07-metodos-m6a-fos-3utr/Sources/hao_2024_mettl3_drives_heart_failure_by_regulating_spp1_and.pdf#Passage 1|[5]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q07-metodos-m6a-fos-3utr/Sources/zhiyuan_2023_exon_intron_boundary_inhibits_m6a_deposition_enabling_m6a.pdf#Passage 4|[18]]].
*   **The Gap:** These findings diverge from the general mammalian model where **YTHDF2** typically recognizes m6A to promote mRNA **decay**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q07-metodos-m6a-fos-3utr/Sources/hao_2024_mettl3_drives_heart_failure_by_regulating_spp1_and.pdf#Passage 5|[19]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q07-metodos-m6a-fos-3utr/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 7|[20]]]. The sources do not reconcile why *FOS* appears resistant to m6A-mediated decay across different biological contexts or whether YTHDF2 is ever involved in its regulation.

### **5. Cross-Species and Intron-Context Validation**
Finally, a gap exists in validating how the "intronless" nature of *FOS* affects its 3'UTR modification across species.
*   **Exon-Intron Architecture:** One source notes that *c-Fos* is a naturally **intronless gene**, which usually leads to higher m6A levels and shorter half-lives  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q07-metodos-m6a-fos-3utr/Sources/anna_2023_exclusion_of_m6a_from_splice_site_proximal_regions.pdf#Passage 2|[21]]]. 
*   **The Gap:** Most *FOS*-specific data provided is from human cells or rat/mouse models in isolation  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q07-metodos-m6a-fos-3utr/Sources/anna_2023_exclusion_of_m6a_from_splice_site_proximal_regions.pdf#Passage 7|[22]]]. There is no comparative analysis in the sources validating whether the **exclusion zone** created by splice junctions (which normally prevents m6A deposition near junctions) is a factor in the *FOS* 3'UTR modification pattern across different species [24, 25]
