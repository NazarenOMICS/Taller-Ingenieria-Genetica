m6A-IP-qPCR. m6A-IP-qPCR was performed following a protocol with minor modifications70. Total RNAs were extracted using TRIzol (Invitrogen) and purified through GenElute mRNA Miniprep kit (Sigma). In total, 5 µg of purified mRNAs were fragmented into ~200 300 nt fragments by incubation in RNA fragmentation reagent (Thermo Fisher) at 94 °C for 30 s and then stopped with stop solution. Ten percent of the fragmented mRNAs were kept as input. The remaining mRNAs were incubated with 6.25 µg m6A-specific antibody (202003, Synaptic Systems) or rabbit IgG (Beyotime) in IP buffer (50 mM Tris-HCl (pH 7.4), 750 mM NaCl, 0.5% Igepal CA-630, 0.4 U/µl RNasin (Promega)) for 2 h at 4 °C. Then the mixture was incubated with 25 µl Dynabeads Protein A (Thermo Fisher) for another 2 h at 4 °C. After extensive washing, the bound mRNAs were eluted with 6.7 mM N6-methyladenosine (Sigma) in IP buffer and then recovered with ethanol precipitation. The immunoprecipitated mRNAs and input mRNAs were processed as in RT-PCR.
