M6A immunoprecipitation The MeRIP assay was conducted with the Magna MeRIP™ m6A Kit (Millipore, USA) to determine m6A modification of individual transcripts. In brief, total RNA was isolated from pretreated cells and randomly fragmented into a size of 100 nucleotides. RNA samples were then immunoprecipitated with magnetic beads precoated with anti-m6A antibody or anti-IgG. N6-methyladenosine 5’-monophosphate sodium salt was applied to elute the m6A-modified RNA fragments for further RT-qPCR analysis. Specific primers were designed for RT-qPCR analysis according to the SRAMP website (m6A loci predictor http://www.cuilab.cn/sramp/) and are listed in Table S4. The relative enrichment of m6A was normalised to the input.
