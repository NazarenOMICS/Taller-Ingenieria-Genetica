each strand were provided with an A-base. Each adapter included a T-base overhang, allowing for the ligation of the adapter to the fragmented DNA (A-tailed). Subse-quently, the A-tailed DNA was combined with single- or dual-index adapters, and the desired size range was carefully chosen using AMPureXP beads. The ligated products were subjected to PCR amplification using the following conditions: an initial denaturation for 3  min at 95 ℃, followed by 8 denaturation cycles for 15 s at 98 ℃, annealing at 60 ℃ for 15  s, and extension at 72 ℃ for 30 s. The final extension step was performed at 72℃ for 5  min. The resulting cDNA library had an average size of 300 ± 50 bp. Paired-end sequencing (PE150) with 2 × 150 bp read length was carried out using the Illumina Novaseq™ 6000, following the recommended protocol provided by the vendor.
