## Hilo central del bloque

- Dysregulation of the FTO (fat mass and obesity-associated protein) demethylase, through either reduced activity (often stress-induced or via knockdown) or overexpression, has been observed to cause significant phenotypic changes across several tissues and physiological systems.
- Altering the activity of **FTO** (fat mass and obesity-associated protein) creates a high risk of **pleiotropic or unintended transcriptome-wide effects** because of its fundamental role as a central "eraser" for the most prevalent internal RNA modification, **m6A** (N6-methyladenosine)  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/dipanjana_2026_fto_dependent_m6a_rna_dysregulation_underlies_memory_deficits.pdf#Passage 7|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/paz_2021_the_role_of_m6a_m5c_and_rna_modifications.pdf#Passage 4|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/paz_2021_the_ro...
- Based on the sources, several strategies exist for titrating or dose-controlling CRISPRa (CRISPR activation) to maintain endogenous gene expression within a **physiological range** and avoid the risks associated with supraphysiological overexpression.
- **FTO (fat mass and obesity-associated protein)** was originally identified through genome-wide association studies (GWAS) as a major genetic susceptibility factor for **obesity and increased Body Mass Index (BMI)**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/bernard_2012_diabetes_and_hypertension_is_there_a_common_metabolic.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/xiulin_2021_the_role_of_m6a_modification_in_the_biological.pdf#Passage 6|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/yanlin_2023_epigenetic_regulation_in_metabolic_diseases_mechanisms_and_advances.pdf#Passa...
