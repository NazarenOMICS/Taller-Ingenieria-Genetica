## Q01
- Pregunta: According to these sources, what unintended or off-target phenotypes have been observed from FTO dysregulation (overexpression or altered activity) in tissues other than the ovary (e.g., memory/neurological deficits, cardiac dysfunction, metabolic effects)?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/QA/answers/2026-07-14 Q01 - according-to-these-sources-what-unintended.md]]

Dysregulation of the FTO (fat mass and obesity-associated protein) demethylase, through either reduced activity (often stress-induced or via knockdown) or overexpression, has been observed to cause significant phenotypic changes across several tissues and physiological systems.
## Q02
- Pregunta: According to these sources, why does FTO's role as a demethylase acting on many m6A-marked transcripts create a risk of pleiotropic or unintended transcriptome-wide effects when its activity is altered?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/QA/answers/2026-07-14 Q02 - according-to-these-sources-why-does.md]]

Altering the activity of **FTO** (fat mass and obesity-associated protein) creates a high risk of **pleiotropic or unintended transcriptome-wide effects** because of its fundamental role as a central "eraser" for the most prevalent internal RNA modification, **m6A** (N6-methyladenosine)  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/dipanjana_2026_fto_dependent_m6a_rna_dysregulation_underlies_memory_deficits.pdf#Passage 7|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/paz_2021_the_role_of_m6a_m5c_and_rna_modifications.pdf#Passage 4|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/paz_2021_the_role_of_m6a_m5c_and_rna_modifications.pdf#Passage 5|[3]]].

## Q04
- Pregunta: According to these sources, what is known about FTO's original link to obesity and metabolic phenotypes, and how does this inform the risk of activating FTO beyond physiological levels?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/QA/answers/2026-07-14 Q04 - according-to-these-sources-what-known.md]]

**FTO (fat mass and obesity-associated protein)** was originally identified through genome-wide association studies (GWAS) as a major genetic susceptibility factor for **obesity and increased Body Mass Index (BMI)**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/bernard_2012_diabetes_and_hypertension_is_there_a_common_metabolic.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/xiulin_2021_the_role_of_m6a_modification_in_the_biological.pdf#Passage 6|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/yanlin_2023_epigenetic_regulation_in_metabolic_diseases_mechanisms_and_advances.pdf#Passage 2|[3]]]. It is closely linked to metabolic regulation, including associations with **hyperinsulinemia**, adiposity, and resting metabolic rates  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/anni_2021_hyperinsulinemia_in_obesity_inflammation_and_cancer.pdf#Passage 1|[4]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/anni_2021_hyperinsulinemia_in_obesity_inflammation_and_cancer.pdf#Passage 3|[5]]].
## Q05

To monitor or limit off-target and pleiotropic transcriptome-wide effects when using CRISPRa to activate a gene like **FTO**, the sources suggest several integrated design and experimental strategies. Because FTO acts as a global "eraser" for m6A marks on thousands of transcripts  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/alexander_2020_direct_rna_sequencing_reveals_m6a_modifications_on_adenovirus.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/xiulin_2021_the_role_of_m6a_modification_in_the_biological.pdf#Passage 19|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/xingxing_2023_overexpression_of_fto_inhibits_excessive_proliferation_and_promotes.pdf#Passage 7|[3]]], precision in both the **magnitude of activation** and the **breadth of monitoring** is essential.
