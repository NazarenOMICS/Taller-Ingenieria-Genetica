# Q01 - According to these sources, what unintended or off-target phenotypes have been observed from FTO dysregulation (overexpression or altered activity) in tissues other than the ovary (e.g., memory/neurological deficits, cardiac dysfunction, metabolic effects)?

Dysregulation of the FTO (fat mass and obesity-associated protein) demethylase, through either reduced activity (often stress-induced or via knockdown) or overexpression, has been observed to cause significant phenotypic changes across several tissues and physiological systems.

According to the sources, the observed unintended or disease-associated phenotypes include:

### **1. Neurological and Cognitive Deficits**
*   **Memory Deficits:** Downregulation of FTO in the **hippocampus** is a key mechanism underlying **spatial memory deficits** induced by early-life stress (ELS)  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/dipanjana_2026_fto_dependent_m6a_rna_dysregulation_underlies_memory_deficits.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/dipanjana_2026_fto_dependent_m6a_rna_dysregulation_underlies_memory_deficits.pdf#Passage 2|[2]]]. While FTO overexpression can selectively rescue these memory deficits, it notably **does not alleviate elevated anxiety** induced by the same stress, suggesting a dissociation in the molecular pathways governing these behaviors  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/dipanjana_2026_fto_dependent_m6a_rna_dysregulation_underlies_memory_deficits.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/dipanjana_2026_fto_dependent_m6a_rna_dysregulation_underlies_memory_deficits.pdf#Passage 3|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/dipanjana_2026_fto_dependent_m6a_rna_dysregulation_underlies_memory_deficits.pdf#Passage 4|[4]]].
*   **Neurogenesis and Brain Structure:** Loss of FTO in adult **neural stem cells (NSCs)** reduces their proliferation and neuronal differentiation, which can lead to **decreased brain size**, reduced body weight, and impaired learning and memory  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/xiulin_2021_the_role_of_m6a_modification_in_the_biological.pdf#Passage 1|[5]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/xiulin_2021_the_role_of_m6a_modification_in_the_biological.pdf#Passage 2|[6]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/xiulin_2021_the_role_of_m6a_modification_in_the_biological.pdf#Passage 3|[7]]].
*   **Specific Memory Types:** Reduced FTO expression in the hippocampus is associated with impaired **working memory**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/dipanjana_2026_fto_dependent_m6a_rna_dysregulation_underlies_memory_deficits.pdf#Passage 5|[8]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/paz_2021_the_role_of_m6a_m5c_and_rna_modifications.pdf#Passage 1|[9]]], while its depletion in adult neurons has also been linked to **increased fear memory**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/xiulin_2021_the_role_of_m6a_modification_in_the_biological.pdf#Passage 4|[10]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/dipanjana_2026_fto_dependent_m6a_rna_dysregulation_underlies_memory_deficits.pdf#Passage 6|[11]]].

### **2. Cardiac Dysfunction**
*   **Heart Failure Progression:** In the context of pressure overload-induced **heart failure**, FTO expression is significantly decreased  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/beijian_2021_m6a_demethylase_fto_attenuates_cardiac_dysfunction_by_regulating.pdf#Passage 1|[12]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/xiulin_2021_the_role_of_m6a_modification_in_the_biological.pdf#Passage 5|[13]]]. 
*   **Knockdown Phenotypes:** FTO knockdown in the heart exacerbates **cardiac dysfunction and remodeling**, worsens mitochondrial structure disorders (such as arrayed cristae), and **decreases cardiac energy supply** by reducing glucose uptake and ATP production  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/beijian_2021_m6a_demethylase_fto_attenuates_cardiac_dysfunction_by_regulating.pdf#Passage 2|[14]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/beijian_2021_m6a_demethylase_fto_attenuates_cardiac_dysfunction_by_regulating.pdf#Passage 3|[15]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/beijian_2021_m6a_demethylase_fto_attenuates_cardiac_dysfunction_by_regulating.pdf#Passage 4|[16]]].
*   **Overexpression Effects:** Conversely, FTO overexpression attenuates these symptoms by regulating glucose metabolism and stabilizing transcripts like *Pgam2* and *Serca2a*  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/xiulin_2021_the_role_of_m6a_modification_in_the_biological.pdf#Passage 5|[13]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/beijian_2021_m6a_demethylase_fto_attenuates_cardiac_dysfunction_by_regulating.pdf#Passage 4|[16]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/beijian_2021_m6a_demethylase_fto_attenuates_cardiac_dysfunction_by_regulating.pdf#Passage 5|[17]]].

### **3. Metabolic and Adipose Effects**
*   **Obesity and Hyperinsulinemia:** Genetic variants or dysregulation of FTO are strongly associated with **increased Body Mass Index (BMI)**, persistent central obesity, and **hyperinsulinemia**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/anni_2021_hyperinsulinemia_in_obesity_inflammation_and_cancer.pdf#Passage 1|[18]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/anni_2021_hyperinsulinemia_in_obesity_inflammation_and_cancer.pdf#Passage 2|[19]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/bernard_2012_diabetes_and_hypertension_is_there_a_common_metabolic.pdf#Passage 1|[20]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/xiulin_2021_the_role_of_m6a_modification_in_the_biological.pdf#Passage 6|[21]]]. 
*   **Adipogenesis:** FTO plays a critical role in **adipogenesis** (fat cell formation). Silencing FTO can lead to reduced autophagy and adipogenesis by promoting the degradation of *Atg5* and *Atg7* mRNAs  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/xiulin_2021_the_role_of_m6a_modification_in_the_biological.pdf#Passage 7|[22]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/xiulin_2021_the_role_of_m6a_modification_in_the_biological.pdf#Passage 8|[23]]]. 
*   **Thermogenesis:** Repairing specific FTO obesity variants using CRISPR can repair thermogenic pathways, promoting **adipocyte browning** and weight loss  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/hongyi_2020_applications_of_genome_editing_technology_in_the_targeted.pdf#Passage 1|[24]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/yanlin_2023_epigenetic_regulation_in_metabolic_diseases_mechanisms_and_advances.pdf#Passage 1|[25]]].

### **4. Renal Dysfunction**
*   **Chronic Glomerulonephritis (CGN):** FTO expression is reduced in patients with CGN  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/xingxing_2023_overexpression_of_fto_inhibits_excessive_proliferation_and_promotes.pdf#Passage 1|[26]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/xingxing_2023_overexpression_of_fto_inhibits_excessive_proliferation_and_promotes.pdf#Passage 2|[27]]]. 
*   **Mesangial Cell Proliferation:** Reduced FTO activity leads to the **excessive proliferation** and **insufficient apoptosis** of human glomerular mesangial cells (HGMCs)  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/xingxing_2023_overexpression_of_fto_inhibits_excessive_proliferation_and_promotes.pdf#Passage 1|[26]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/xingxing_2023_overexpression_of_fto_inhibits_excessive_proliferation_and_promotes.pdf#Passage 3|[28]]]. This contributes to mesangial expansion and decreased glomerular filtration rates  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/xingxing_2023_overexpression_of_fto_inhibits_excessive_proliferation_and_promotes.pdf#Passage 4|[29]]].
*   **Fibrosis:** Reduced FTO can also lead to the formation of autophagosomes that promote **renal fibrosis**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/xingxing_2023_overexpression_of_fto_inhibits_excessive_proliferation_and_promotes.pdf#Passage 2|[27]]].

### **5. Oncogenic Activity (Cancer)**
*   **Leukemogenesis:** FTO is highly expressed in **Acute Myeloid Leukemia (AML)**, where its overexpression acts as an oncogene by promoting cell proliferation and viability while **blocking cell differentiation**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/paz_2021_the_role_of_m6a_m5c_and_rna_modifications.pdf#Passage 2|[30]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/xiulin_2021_the_role_of_m6a_modification_in_the_biological.pdf#Passage 9|[31]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/xiulin_2021_the_role_of_m6a_modification_in_the_biological.pdf#Passage 10|[32]]].
*   **Tumor Progression and Resistance:** FTO overexpression facilitates the progression (proliferation and invasion) of **lung, cervical, breast, and pancreatic cancers**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/paz_2021_the_role_of_m6a_m5c_and_rna_modifications.pdf#Passage 2|[30]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/paz_2021_the_role_of_m6a_m5c_and_rna_modifications.pdf#Passage 3|[33]]]. In **melanoma**, increased FTO activity promotes tumorigenesis and leads to **resistance to immunotherapy** (anti-PD-1)  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/paz_2021_the_role_of_m6a_m5c_and_rna_modifications.pdf#Passage 3|[33]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/xiulin_2021_the_role_of_m6a_modification_in_the_biological.pdf#Passage 11|[34]]].
