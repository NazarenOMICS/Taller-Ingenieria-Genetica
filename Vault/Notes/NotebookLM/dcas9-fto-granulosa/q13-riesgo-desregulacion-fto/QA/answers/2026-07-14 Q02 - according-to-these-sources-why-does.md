# Q02 - According to these sources, why does FTO's role as a demethylase acting on many m6A-marked transcripts create a risk of pleiotropic or unintended transcriptome-wide effects when its activity is altered?

Altering the activity of **FTO** (fat mass and obesity-associated protein) creates a high risk of **pleiotropic or unintended transcriptome-wide effects** because of its fundamental role as a central "eraser" for the most prevalent internal RNA modification, **m6A** (N6-methyladenosine)  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/dipanjana_2026_fto_dependent_m6a_rna_dysregulation_underlies_memory_deficits.pdf#Passage 7|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/paz_2021_the_role_of_m6a_m5c_and_rna_modifications.pdf#Passage 4|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/paz_2021_the_role_of_m6a_m5c_and_rna_modifications.pdf#Passage 5|[3]]]. 

According to the sources, the following factors drive this systemic risk:

### **1. Vast and Diverse Target Range**
FTO does not target a single gene; instead, it recognizes a specific chemical mark (m6A) that is found on approximately **one-third of all mammalian messenger RNAs** and many non-coding RNAs (lncRNAs, snRNAs, and circRNAs)  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/paz_2021_the_role_of_m6a_m5c_and_rna_modifications.pdf#Passage 6|[4]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/xiulin_2021_the_role_of_m6a_modification_in_the_biological.pdf#Passage 12|[5]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/dipanjana_2026_fto_dependent_m6a_rna_dysregulation_underlies_memory_deficits.pdf#Passage 8|[6]]]. Because these m6A marks are present on thousands of different transcripts across the genome, any change in FTO activity—whether through stress-induced downregulation or therapeutic overexpression—results in a **broad "shift" in the epitranscriptomic landscape** rather than a isolated genetic change  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/dipanjana_2026_fto_dependent_m6a_rna_dysregulation_underlies_memory_deficits.pdf#Passage 9|[7]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/xiulin_2021_the_role_of_m6a_modification_in_the_biological.pdf#Passage 13|[8]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/xingxing_2023_overexpression_of_fto_inhibits_excessive_proliferation_and_promotes.pdf#Passage 5|[9]]].

### **2. Control Over Fundamental RNA Fates**
The m6A marks that FTO removes are "master regulators" of almost every stage of an RNA's life cycle  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/paz_2021_the_role_of_m6a_m5c_and_rna_modifications.pdf#Passage 7|[10]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/xiulin_2021_the_role_of_m6a_modification_in_the_biological.pdf#Passage 14|[11]]]. Altering FTO activity impacts:
*   **Splicing and Processing:** FTO regulates alternative splicing and the maturation of primary miRNAs  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/dipanjana_2026_fto_dependent_m6a_rna_dysregulation_underlies_memory_deficits.pdf#Passage 8|[6]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/paz_2021_the_role_of_m6a_m5c_and_rna_modifications.pdf#Passage 7|[10]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/xiulin_2021_the_role_of_m6a_modification_in_the_biological.pdf#Passage 15|[12]]].
*   **Stability and Decay:** By removing m6A, FTO can either stabilize or destabilize transcripts depending on which "reader" proteins (like YTHDF2) interact with the remaining marks  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/dipanjana_2026_fto_dependent_m6a_rna_dysregulation_underlies_memory_deficits.pdf#Passage 10|[13]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/dipanjana_2026_fto_dependent_m6a_rna_dysregulation_underlies_memory_deficits.pdf#Passage 11|[14]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/dipanjana_2026_fto_dependent_m6a_rna_dysregulation_underlies_memory_deficits.pdf#Passage 1|[15]]].
*   **Translation Efficiency:** FTO levels directly influence the rate of global protein synthesis by altering how ribosomes and initiation factors recognize modified transcripts  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/dipanjana_2026_fto_dependent_m6a_rna_dysregulation_underlies_memory_deficits.pdf#Passage 10|[13]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/dipanjana_2026_fto_dependent_m6a_rna_dysregulation_underlies_memory_deficits.pdf#Passage 11|[14]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/xiulin_2021_the_role_of_m6a_modification_in_the_biological.pdf#Passage 16|[16]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/xiulin_2021_the_role_of_m6a_modification_in_the_biological.pdf#Passage 4|[17]]].

### **3. Simultaneous Disruption of Disparate Physiological Systems**
Because FTO's targets are involved in many independent pathways, its dysregulation triggers a cascade of unintended phenotypes across multiple tissues  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/anni_2021_hyperinsulinemia_in_obesity_inflammation_and_cancer.pdf#Passage 1|[18]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/anni_2021_hyperinsulinemia_in_obesity_inflammation_and_cancer.pdf#Passage 3|[19]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/xiulin_2021_the_role_of_m6a_modification_in_the_biological.pdf#Passage 7|[20]]]:
*   **Metabolic Pleiotropy:** FTO activity is inextricably linked to **adipogenesis** (fat cell formation), **obesity**, and **hyperinsulinemia**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/xiulin_2021_the_role_of_m6a_modification_in_the_biological.pdf#Passage 7|[20]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/beijian_2021_m6a_demethylase_fto_attenuates_cardiac_dysfunction_by_regulating.pdf#Passage 2|[21]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/xingxing_2023_overexpression_of_fto_inhibits_excessive_proliferation_and_promotes.pdf#Passage 1|[22]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/xingxing_2023_overexpression_of_fto_inhibits_excessive_proliferation_and_promotes.pdf#Passage 6|[23]]].
*   **Cardiac and Renal Dysfunction:** In the heart, altering FTO disrupts mitochondrial structure and **glucose metabolism**, leading to remodeling and dysfunction  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/xingxing_2023_overexpression_of_fto_inhibits_excessive_proliferation_and_promotes.pdf#Passage 2|[24]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/dipanjana_2026_fto_dependent_m6a_rna_dysregulation_underlies_memory_deficits.pdf#Passage 3|[25]]]. In the kidney, it can trigger **excessive cell proliferation** and fibrosis  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/dipanjana_2026_fto_dependent_m6a_rna_dysregulation_underlies_memory_deficits.pdf#Passage 4|[26]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/paz_2021_the_role_of_m6a_m5c_and_rna_modifications.pdf#Passage 2|[27]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/paz_2021_the_role_of_m6a_m5c_and_rna_modifications.pdf#Passage 3|[28]]].
*   **Neurological Dissociation:** In the hippocampus, stress-induced loss of FTO selectively impairs **spatial memory** but fails to reverse anxiety, demonstrating how its dysregulation can impact specific cognitive functions while leaving other related behaviors untouched or worsening  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/anni_2021_hyperinsulinemia_in_obesity_inflammation_and_cancer.pdf#Passage 1|[18]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/xiulin_2021_the_role_of_m6a_modification_in_the_biological.pdf#Passage 9|[29]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/xiulin_2021_the_role_of_m6a_modification_in_the_biological.pdf#Passage 11|[30]]].
*   **Oncogenic Risk:** In many tissues, overactive FTO acts as an **oncogene**, promoting tumor growth, blocking cell differentiation, and inducing **resistance to immunotherapy**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/dipanjana_2026_fto_dependent_m6a_rna_dysregulation_underlies_memory_deficits.pdf#Passage 12|[31]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/xingxing_2023_overexpression_of_fto_inhibits_excessive_proliferation_and_promotes.pdf#Passage 7|[32]]].

In summary, the sources suggest that the "risk" lies in FTO’s role as a **global orchestrator**. Because it functions as a single switch for thousands of transcripts involved in energy homeostasis, development, and cellular survival, altering its activity can rarely be restricted to a single intended effect without triggering a wide range of **unintended systemic consequences**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/dipanjana_2026_fto_dependent_m6a_rna_dysregulation_underlies_memory_deficits.pdf#Passage 9|[7]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q13-riesgo-desregulacion-fto/Sources/xiulin_2021_the_role_of_m6a_modification_in_the_biological.pdf#Passage 7|[20]]].
