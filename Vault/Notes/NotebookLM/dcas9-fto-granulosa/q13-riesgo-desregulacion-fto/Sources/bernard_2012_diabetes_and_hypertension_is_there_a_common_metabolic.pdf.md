### Passage 1

The obese (ob) gene that was discovered in 1950 was the first gene identified to be related to the onset of obesity [26]. From then on, researchers have sought to identify the genetic factors of obesity in addition to studying metabolic physiology. Genome-wide association studies have revealed a number of genes influencing the susceptibility to obesity [27–29]. The FTO gene, promoting obesity and overeating, was one of the key obesity susceptibility genes. Together with the GNDPA 2 gene, they predict persistent central obesity in the Chinese population [10]. Other likely diabetes-related genes include BCDIN3D/FAIM2, SH2B1, and KCTD15 [29–32] as well as CRTC3, which has been shown to slow down the speed of fat oxidation [33].
