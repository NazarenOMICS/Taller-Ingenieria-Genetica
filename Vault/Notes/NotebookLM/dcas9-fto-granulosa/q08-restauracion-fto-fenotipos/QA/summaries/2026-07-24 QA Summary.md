## Q01
- Pregunta: According to these sources, how does FTO overexpression protect human granulosa cells, and what specific phenotypes (senescence markers, viability, function) improve as a result?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/QA/answers/2026-07-24 Q01 - according-to-these-sources-how-does.md]]

FTO overexpression protects human granulosa cells (hGCs) by functioning as a **senescence-retarding protein** that utilizes its **$m^6A$ demethylase activity** to reverse epigenetic disorders and restore cellular homeostasis  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/linshuang_2024_fat_mass_and_obesity_associated_protein_regulates_granulosa.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/linshuang_2024_fat_mass_and_obesity_associated_protein_regulates_granulosa.pdf#Passage 2|[2]]]. Mechanistically, FTO targets and regulates several key molecular pathways: it destabilizes aging-related **FOS** mRNA to prevent GC-mediated ovarian aging, stabilizes **MMP2** mRNA to activate the **ERK signaling pathway**, suppresses the pro-apoptotic factor **BNIP3**, and promotes the **circBRCA1/miR-642a-5p/FOXO1 axis** to repair oxidative damage  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/linshuang_2024_fat_mass_and_obesity_associated_protein_regulates_granulosa.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/linshuang_2024_fat_mass...
