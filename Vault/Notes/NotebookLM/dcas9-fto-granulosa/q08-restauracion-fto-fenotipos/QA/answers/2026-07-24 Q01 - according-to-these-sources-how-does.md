# Q01 - According to these sources, how does FTO overexpression protect human granulosa cells, and what specific phenotypes (senescence markers, viability, function) improve as a result?

FTO overexpression protects human granulosa cells (hGCs) by functioning as a **senescence-retarding protein** that utilizes its **$m^6A$ demethylase activity** to reverse epigenetic disorders and restore cellular homeostasis  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/linshuang_2024_fat_mass_and_obesity_associated_protein_regulates_granulosa.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/linshuang_2024_fat_mass_and_obesity_associated_protein_regulates_granulosa.pdf#Passage 2|[2]]]. Mechanistically, FTO targets and regulates several key molecular pathways: it destabilizes aging-related **FOS** mRNA to prevent GC-mediated ovarian aging, stabilizes **MMP2** mRNA to activate the **ERK signaling pathway**, suppresses the pro-apoptotic factor **BNIP3**, and promotes the **circBRCA1/miR-642a-5p/FOXO1 axis** to repair oxidative damage  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/linshuang_2024_fat_mass_and_obesity_associated_protein_regulates_granulosa.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/linshuang_2024_fat_mass_and_obesity_associated_protein_regulates_granulosa.pdf#Passage 2|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/rongli_2021_overexpression_of_fto_protects_human_granulosa_cells_from.pdf#Passage 1|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/rongli_2021_overexpression_of_fto_protects_human_granulosa_cells_from.pdf#Passage 2|[4]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 1|[5]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 2|[6]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 1|[7]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/chuan_2023_bone_marrow_mesenchymal_stem_cells_reversed_ovarian_aging.pdf#Passage 1|[8]]].

As a result of FTO overexpression or restoration, the following specific phenotypes improve:

### Senescence Markers
*   **Reduced SA-$\beta$-gal activity:** There is a significant decrease in the proportion of cells positive for senescence-associated beta-galactosidase  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/linshuang_2024_fat_mass_and_obesity_associated_protein_regulates_granulosa.pdf#Passage 3|[9]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 3|[10]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/jin_2026_fto_mediated_m6a_demethylation_regulates_pgc_1_dependent.pdf#Passage 1|[11]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/xiaoyao_2024_klotho_derived_peptide_1_inhibits_cellular_senescence_in.pdf#Passage 1|[12]]].
*   **Downregulation of cell cycle inhibitors:** Expression levels of key aging markers, including **p16, p21, p27, and p53**, are markedly reduced  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/linshuang_2024_fat_mass_and_obesity_associated_protein_regulates_granulosa.pdf#Passage 3|[9]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/jin_2026_fto_mediated_m6a_demethylation_regulates_pgc_1_dependent.pdf#Passage 1|[11]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/xiaoyao_2024_klotho_derived_peptide_1_inhibits_cellular_senescence_in.pdf#Passage 2|[13]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 4|[14]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 2|[15]]].
*   **Decreased DNA damage:** Markers of selective DNA double-strand breaks, such as **$\gamma$-H2AX**, are significantly lowered  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/xiaoyao_2024_klotho_derived_peptide_1_inhibits_cellular_senescence_in.pdf#Passage 1|[12]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 2|[15]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/linshuang_2024_fat_mass_and_obesity_associated_protein_regulates_granulosa.pdf#Passage 4|[16]]].
*   **Lower oxidative stress:** Intracellular levels of **reactive oxygen species (ROS)** are diminished, and levels of antioxidant enzymes like **SOD2** and **Gpx** are restored  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/linshuang_2024_fat_mass_and_obesity_associated_protein_regulates_granulosa.pdf#Passage 3|[9]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/jin_2026_fto_mediated_m6a_demethylation_regulates_pgc_1_dependent.pdf#Passage 1|[11]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/rongli_2021_overexpression_of_fto_protects_human_granulosa_cells_from.pdf#Passage 3|[17]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 5|[18]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 6|[19]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/rongli_2021_overexpression_of_fto_protects_human_granulosa_cells_from.pdf#Passage 4|[20]]].

### Viability and Proliferation
*   **Enhanced cell proliferation:** FTO restoration increases the number of active, dividing cells, as shown by higher **Ki67, BrdU, and EdU** staining  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/linshuang_2024_fat_mass_and_obesity_associated_protein_regulates_granulosa.pdf#Passage 3|[9]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 7|[21]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 8|[22]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 9|[23]]].
*   **Decreased apoptosis:** Overexpression reduces programmed cell death induced by natural aging or toxins like cisplatin  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/rongli_2021_overexpression_of_fto_protects_human_granulosa_cells_from.pdf#Passage 3|[17]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 8|[22]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 9|[23]]].
*   **Improved anti-apoptotic profile:** This is molecularly characterized by **increased Bcl-2** (anti-apoptotic) and **decreased Bax** (pro-apoptotic) expression, resulting in a higher **Bcl-2/Bax ratio**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 4|[14]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 8|[22]]].
*   **Restored morphology:** Aged GCs, which appear flat and wide with numerous vacuoles, regain a healthier, more normal morphology following treatment  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/linshuang_2024_fat_mass_and_obesity_associated_protein_regulates_granulosa.pdf#Passage 3|[9]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/jin_2026_fto_mediated_m6a_demethylation_regulates_pgc_1_dependent.pdf#Passage 1|[11]]].

### Cellular and Ovarian Function
*   **Restored mitochondrial function:** Overexpression improves **mitochondrial membrane potential (MMP)** and increases both **ATP production** and **oxygen consumption rates (OCR)**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 3|[10]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/jin_2026_fto_mediated_m6a_demethylation_regulates_pgc_1_dependent.pdf#Passage 1|[11]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 5|[18]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 6|[19]]].
*   **Improved mitochondrial integrity:** The number of abnormal or damaged mitochondria decreases, and mitochondrial morphology is repaired  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 2|[6]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/jin_2026_fto_mediated_m6a_demethylation_regulates_pgc_1_dependent.pdf#Passage 1|[11]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 5|[18]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 9|[23]]].
*   **Hormonal restoration:** In vivo models demonstrate improved ovarian reserve indicators, characterized by **increased AMH and Estradiol (E2)** and **decreased FSH and LH** [27-30].
*   **Follicular health:** There is a reduction in atretic follicles, a restoration of normal **estrous cycles**, and improved fertility outcomes, such as higher pregnancy rates and offspring counts  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 2|[6]]].

> Abstract In this study, we aimed to investigate the molecular mechanisms of RNA N6-methyladenosine (m6A) modification and how its associated proteins affect granulosa cell aging. A granulosa cell senescence model was constructed to detect the differences in total RNA m6A modification levels and the expression of related enzymes. Changes in downstream molecular expression and the effects on the cellular senescence phenotype were explored by repeatedly knocking down and overexpressing the key genes fat mass and obesity-associated protein (FTO), YT521-B homology domain family member 2 (YTHDF2), and matrix metalloproteinase-2 (MMP2). There was an increased total RNA m6A modification and decreased expression of the demethylase FTO and target gene MMP2 in senescent granulosa cells. FTO and MMP2 knockdown promoted granulosa cell senescence, whereas FTO and MMP2 overexpression retarded it. YTHDF2 and FTO can bind to the messenger RNA of MMP2. The extracellular signal-regulated kinase (ERK) pathway, which is downstream of MMP2, retarded the process of granulosa cell senescence through ERK activators. In granulosa cells, FTO can regulate the expression of MMP2 in an m6A-YTHDF2-dependent manner, influencing the activation status of the ERK pathway and contributing to the aging process of granulosa cells.
