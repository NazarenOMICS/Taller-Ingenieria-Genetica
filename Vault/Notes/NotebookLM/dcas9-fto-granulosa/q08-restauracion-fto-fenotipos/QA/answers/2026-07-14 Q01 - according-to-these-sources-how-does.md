# Q01 - According to these sources, how does FTO overexpression protect human granulosa cells, and what specific phenotypes (senescence markers, viability, function) improve as a result?

FTO overexpression protects human granulosa cells (hGCs) by acting as a **senescence-retarding protein** that reverses epigenetic disorders and restores cellular homeostasis  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 2|[2]]]. Mechanistically, FTO functions as an **m6A demethylase** to regulate several key pathways: it destabilizes the aging-related **FOS** mRNA  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/linshuang_2024_fat_mass_and_obesity_associated_protein_regulates_granulosa.pdf#Passage 1|[3]]], stabilizes **MMP2** to activate the **ERK signaling pathway**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/linshuang_2024_fat_mass_and_obesity_associated_protein_regulates_granulosa.pdf#Passage 2|[4]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/rongli_2021_overexpression_of_fto_protects_human_granulosa_cells_from.pdf#Passage 1|[5]]], downregulates the pro-apoptotic factor **BNIP3**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/rongli_2021_overexpression_of_fto_protects_human_granulosa_cells_from.pdf#Passage 2|[6]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 1|[7]]], and promotes the **circBRCA1/miR-642a-5p/FOXO1 axis** to repair oxidative damage  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 2|[8]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 3|[9]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/chuan_2023_bone_marrow_mesenchymal_stem_cells_reversed_ovarian_aging.pdf#Passage 1|[10]]].

As a result of FTO overexpression or restoration, the following specific phenotypes improve:

### Senescence Markers
*   **Decreased SA-β-gal activity:** There is a significant reduction in the proportion of cells staining positive for senescence-associated beta-galactosidase  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/chuan_2023_bone_marrow_mesenchymal_stem_cells_reversed_ovarian_aging.pdf#Passage 2|[11]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 4|[12]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 3|[13]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 4|[14]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/rongli_2021_overexpression_of_fto_protects_human_granulosa_cells_from.pdf#Passage 3|[15]]].
*   **Downregulation of cell cycle inhibitors:** Expression levels of key senescence markers **p16, p21, p27, and p53** are markedly reduced  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/chuan_2023_bone_marrow_mesenchymal_stem_cells_reversed_ovarian_aging.pdf#Passage 2|[11]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 4|[12]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 3|[13]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 4|[14]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/rongli_2021_overexpression_of_fto_protects_human_granulosa_cells_from.pdf#Passage 3|[15]]].
*   **Reduced DNA damage:** Markers of DNA damage and cellular stress, such as **γ-H2AX**, are decreased  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 5|[16]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 6|[17]]].
*   **Lower oxidative stress:** Intracellular **reactive oxygen species (ROS)** levels are significantly diminished  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/chuan_2023_bone_marrow_mesenchymal_stem_cells_reversed_ovarian_aging.pdf#Passage 2|[11]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 4|[12]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 4|[14]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/rongli_2021_overexpression_of_fto_protects_human_granulosa_cells_from.pdf#Passage 3|[15]]].

### Viability and Proliferation
*   **Enhanced cell proliferation:** Overexpression restores the proliferative capacity of hGCs, increasing the number of active, dividing cells (evidenced by higher **Ki67** and **BrdU** staining)  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/chuan_2023_bone_marrow_mesenchymal_stem_cells_reversed_ovarian_aging.pdf#Passage 2|[11]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 4|[12]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/rongli_2021_overexpression_of_fto_protects_human_granulosa_cells_from.pdf#Passage 3|[15]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 7|[18]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/rongli_2021_overexpression_of_fto_protects_human_granulosa_cells_from.pdf#Passage 4|[19]]].
*   **Decreased apoptosis:** FTO reduces programmed cell death induced by aging or toxins like cisplatin  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/rongli_2021_overexpression_of_fto_protects_human_granulosa_cells_from.pdf#Passage 2|[6]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/rongli_2021_overexpression_of_fto_protects_human_granulosa_cells_from.pdf#Passage 3|[15]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 7|[18]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/rongli_2021_overexpression_of_fto_protects_human_granulosa_cells_from.pdf#Passage 4|[19]]]. 
*   **Improved anti-apoptotic profile:** Molecularly, this is characterized by **increased Bcl-2** (anti-apoptotic) and **decreased Bax** (pro-apoptotic) expression, resulting in a higher **Bcl-2/Bax ratio**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 3|[13]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 7|[18]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 8|[20]]].
*   **Restored morphology:** Aged GCs, which typically appear flat and wide with numerous vacuoles, regain a more healthy, normal morphology  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/chuan_2023_bone_marrow_mesenchymal_stem_cells_reversed_ovarian_aging.pdf#Passage 2|[11]]].

### Cellular and Ovarian Function
*   **Restored mitochondrial function:** FTO overexpression improves **mitochondrial membrane potential (MMP)** and increases **ATP production** and oxygen consumption rates (OCR)  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 3|[9]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 3|[13]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/rongli_2021_overexpression_of_fto_protects_human_granulosa_cells_from.pdf#Passage 3|[15]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 9|[21]]].
*   **Improved mitochondrial integrity:** The number of abnormal or damaged mitochondria decreases, and mitochondrial morphology is repaired  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 3|[9]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/rongli_2021_overexpression_of_fto_protects_human_granulosa_cells_from.pdf#Passage 3|[15]]].
*   **Hormonal and reproductive restoration:** In vivo models show that restoring FTO levels leads to improved **ovarian reserve indicators**, including increased **AMH and Estradiol (E2)** and decreased **FSH and LH** [24-27].
*   **Follicular health:** There is a reduction in the number of atretic follicles and a restoration of normal estrous cycles  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 2|[8]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/rongli_2021_overexpression_of_fto_protects_human_granulosa_cells_from.pdf#Passage 3|[15]]].

> Abstract In this study, we aimed to investigate the molecular mechanisms of RNA N6-methyladenosine (m6A) modification and how its associated proteins affect granulosa cell aging. A granulosa cell senescence model was constructed to detect the differences in total RNA m6A modification levels and the expression of related enzymes. Changes in downstream molecular expression and the effects on the cellular senescence phenotype were explored by repeatedly knocking down and overexpressing the key genes fat mass and obesity-associated protein (FTO), YT521-B homology domain family member 2 (YTHDF2), and matrix metalloproteinase-2 (MMP2). FTO and MMP2 knockdown promoted granulosa cell senescence, whereas FTO and MMP2 overexpression retarded it. YTHDF2 and FTO can bind to the messenger RNA of MMP2. The extracellular signal-regulated kinase (ERK) pathway, which is downstream of MMP2, retarded the process of granulosa cell senescence through ERK activators. In granulosa cells, FTO can regulate the expression of MMP2 in an m6A-YTHDF2-dependent manner, influencing the activation status of the ERK pathway and contributing to the aging process of granulosa cells.
