# Q06 - What gaps remain in these sources regarding FTO restoration specifically via epigenetic/CRISPRa-based methods (as opposed to plasmid overexpression), and regarding senescence/DNA-damage phenotype reversal specifically in granulosa cells?

Based on the sources, several critical gaps remain regarding site-specific epigenetic restoration of **FTO** and the specific reversal of senescence and DNA-damage phenotypes in human granulosa cells (hGCs).

### Gaps in Epigenetic and CRISPRa-based FTO Restoration
While the sources describe the use of tools like the **dCas9-p300 system** for gene expression activation and dCas13-FTO fusions for RNA editing, they highlight significant technical and clinical hurdles  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/kachanov_2025.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/kachanov_2025.pdf#Passage 2|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/ruochen_2023_methylation_across_the_central_dogma_in_health_and.pdf#Passage 1|[3]]]:

*   **Transient vs. Persistent Activation:** A major limitation is the **"transient nature of transcription activation"** using current CRISPRa systems like dCas9-p300  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/kachanov_2025.pdf#Passage 3|[4]]]. Many genes show only "modest and transient" expression alterations, and the "contextual cues" required to achieve the persistent, heritable changes necessary for long-term therapy are not yet clarified  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/ruochen_2023_methylation_across_the_central_dogma_in_health_and.pdf#Passage 3|[5]]].
*   **In Vivo Delivery Bottlenecks:** The **specificity, efficacy, and safety of in vivo delivery** for these precise molecular tools remain significant bottlenecks  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/ruochen_2023_methylation_across_the_central_dogma_in_health_and.pdf#Passage 4|[6]]]. Delivery requirements vary by tissue, and the potential **human immune response** to these editors has not been adequately characterized  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/ruochen_2023_methylation_across_the_central_dogma_in_health_and.pdf#Passage 4|[6]]].
*   **Identifying Driver Sites:** While editors help dissect causal relationships, researchers still need to "delve deeply into the details" to identify the most **"faithful disease-driver"** methylation sites among complex regulatory networks  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/ruochen_2023_methylation_across_the_central_dogma_in_health_and.pdf#Passage 4|[6]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/ruochen_2023_methylation_across_the_central_dogma_in_health_and.pdf#Passage 5|[7]]].

### Gaps in Granulosa Cell Senescence and DNA-Damage Research
Research focused on reversing aging in human granulosa cells face unique challenges related to cell sourcing and mechanistic unknowns  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/chuan_2023_bone_marrow_mesenchymal_stem_cells_reversed_ovarian_aging.pdf#Passage 13|[8]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 1|[9]]]:

*   **Cell Source Heterogeneity:** Most clinical GC samples collected during IVF are technically **"cumulus cells"** rather than mural granulosa cells  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/gustavo_2021_reproductive_senescence_impairs_the_energy_metabolism_of_human.pdf#Passage 1|[10]]]. It remains unconfirmed whether findings in these luteinized, preovulatory cells can be extrapolated to cells from **"growing and non-preovulatory follicles"**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/jin_2026_fto_mediated_m6a_demethylation_regulates_pgc_1_dependent.pdf#Passage 6|[11]]].
*   **Reliance on Immortalized Lines:** Much of the mechanistic data (such as the FTO-MMP2 axis) relies on **immortalized cell lines** (e.g., KGN, COV434, or mouse HT22 neurons)  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/linshuang_2024_fat_mass_and_obesity_associated_protein_regulates_granulosa.pdf#Passage 8|[12]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 12|[13]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/rongli_2021_overexpression_of_fto_protects_human_granulosa_cells_from.pdf#Passage 9|[14]]]. Sources acknowledge that these simplified models cannot fully recapitulate the complexity of an **intact brain or ovary**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/linshuang_2024_fat_mass_and_obesity_associated_protein_regulates_granulosa.pdf#Passage 8|[12]]].
*   **Lack of Primary Tissue Access:** Researchers report **"no access to collect the ovary samples"** from patients with premature ovarian failure (POF) due to ethical and clinical requirements, limiting the ability to verify FTO-dependent aging mechanisms in actual diseased human tissue  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/jin_2026_fto_mediated_m6a_demethylation_regulates_pgc_1_dependent.pdf#Passage 7|[15]]].
*   **Unknown Paracrine Factors:** While mesenchymal stem cells (MSCs) are known to restore FTO expression in injured hGCs, the **specific "factors secreted from the stem cells"** that trigger this upregulation remain unidentified  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/jin_2026_fto_mediated_m6a_demethylation_regulates_pgc_1_dependent.pdf#Passage 7|[15]]].
*   **Need for Direct Functional Evidence:** Although markers like **$\gamma$-H2AX** show reduced DNA damage, there is a lack of comprehensive evidence using real-time assays for **respiratory chain activity** or precise site-specific mapping of m6A residues to clarify the post-transcriptional regulation of repair genes  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q08-restauracion-fto-fenotipos/Sources/linshuang_2024_fat_mass_and_obesity_associated_protein_regulates_granulosa.pdf#Passage 8|[12]]].
*   **Clinical Predictive Value:** It is still a **"long way to go"** to determine if the FTO-FOS axis can reliably predict single oocyte quality or ovarian reserve in a clinical setting [16].

> N6-methyladenosine (m6A) is a common RNA modification, which plays a critical role in RNA fate and regulating such aspects as splicing, stability, nuclear export, and translation efficiency. The introduction, removal, and recognition of m6A modifications in RNA are regulated by a number of factors, known as writer, eraser, and reader proteins. It is known that the m6A modification can play an important role in the life cycle of viruses, including hepatitis B virus. The m6A methylation system has a significant impact on the hepatitis B viral cycle (HBV), particularly, on stability of mRNA transcripts, encapsidation efficiency, and reverse transcription of HBV pgRNA. In this study, we assessed the effect of knockout and activation of expression of several factors of the m6A methylation system on the HBV viral cycle, including pregenomic RNA (pgRNA) and circular covalently closed DNA (cccDNA). The study was carried out using the StCas9 nuclease system for knockout and the dCas9-p300 system for activation of gene expression. The levels of pgRNA and cccDNA were estimated by real-time PCR. The data obtained show the restriction of the viral cycle at the basal level by the factors METTL3, METTL14, METTL16, FTO, JMJD6, and hnRNPA2B1, as well as suppression of the viral cycle with overexpression of all of the above factors, except for hnRNPA2B1.
