# Q04 - Do these sources report promoter-region mutations, cis-regulatory variants, or structural alterations that silence or reduce FTO transcription in any relevant tissue, and are any such findings applicable to granulosa biology?

Based on the provided sources, there are **no reports of promoter-region mutations, cis-regulatory variants, or structural alterations that directly silence or reduce *FTO* transcription** in granulosa cells or other tissues. Instead, the sources focus on a specific common genetic variant and attribute transcriptional changes to stress-induced pathways or hormonal signaling.

### 1. Genetic Variants in the *FTO* Locus
The sources identify one primary genetic variant associated with *FTO* regulation and disease susceptibility:
*   **rs9939609 (Intronic Polymorphism):** This is the most frequently cited variant  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/kuai_2026_unravelling_multilayered_rna_modification_networks_in_female_reproduction.pdf#Passage 7|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/kuai_2026_unravelling_multilayered_rna_modification_networks_in_female_reproduction.pdf#Passage 4|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/liu_2023_mechanisms_of_and_potential_medications_for_oxidative_stress.pdf#Passage 2|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/paper_2022_abstracts_from_the_54_sup_th_sup_european.pdf#Passage 1|[4]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/paper_2022_abstracts_from_the_54_sup_th_sup_european.pdf#Passage 2|[5]]]. Importantly, this is an **intronic SNP** (located in the first intron of the *FTO* gene), not a promoter mutation or a structural alteration  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/kuai_2026_unravelling_multilayered_rna_modification_networks_in_female_reproduction.pdf#Passage 4|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/paper_2022_abstracts_from_the_54_sup_th_sup_european.pdf#Passage 3|[6]]]. 
*   **Association with PCOS:** In population-based genomics and GWAS studies, this variant is significantly associated with **Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS)** susceptibility and increased androgen levels  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/kuai_2026_unravelling_multilayered_rna_modification_networks_in_female_reproduction.pdf#Passage 7|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/kuai_2026_unravelling_multilayered_rna_modification_networks_in_female_reproduction.pdf#Passage 8|[7]]].
*   **Effect on Activity:** The sources state that this genetic variation is linked to **altered m6A demethylation activity** and dysregulated lipid and androgen metabolism  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/kuai_2026_unravelling_multilayered_rna_modification_networks_in_female_reproduction.pdf#Passage 7|[1]]].

### 2. Applicability to Granulosa Biology
The findings regarding *FTO* genetic variants are directly applicable to granulosa biology, particularly in the context of **PCOS**:
*   **Increased Expression in PCOS:** Counter-intuitively, in the granulosa cells (GCs) of PCOS patients, **FTO expression is actually increased**, not silenced  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/kuai_2026_unravelling_multilayered_rna_modification_networks_in_female_reproduction.pdf#Passage 4|[2]]]. This upregulation is driven by a positive feedback loop involving the androgen receptor (AR)  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/kuai_2026_unravelling_multilayered_rna_modification_networks_in_female_reproduction.pdf#Passage 4|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/kuai_2026_unravelling_multilayered_rna_modification_networks_in_female_reproduction.pdf#Passage 3|[8]]].
*   **Delayed Puberty:** One source reports an association between the *FTO* rs9939609 polymorphism and **delayed sexual development** in adolescent boys, suggesting its role in systemic reproductive maturation  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/paper_2022_abstracts_from_the_54_sup_th_sup_european.pdf#Passage 4|[9]]].
*   **IUGR:** Another study investigated the association of this variant with **Intrauterine Growth Restriction (IUGR)** but found no statistically significant link  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/paper_2022_abstracts_from_the_54_sup_th_sup_european.pdf#Passage 5|[10]]].

### 3. Mechanisms for Reduced *FTO* Transcription
While the query asks for mutations that *silence* *FTO*, the sources attribute the observed **downregulation of *FTO*** (seen in aging and POI) to non-mutational factors:
*   **Oxidative Stress and ROS:** Reactive oxygen species (ROS) and environmental toxins (like cadmium or chemotherapy) are the primary drivers identified for **reduced *FTO* transcription** in GCs  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/kuai_2026_unravelling_multilayered_rna_modification_networks_in_female_reproduction.pdf#Passage 9|[11]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/kuai_2026_unravelling_multilayered_rna_modification_networks_in_female_reproduction.pdf#Passage 6|[12]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/kuai_2026_unravelling_multilayered_rna_modification_networks_in_female_reproduction.pdf#Passage 10|[13]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/rongli_2021_overexpression_of_fto_protects_human_granulosa_cells_from.pdf#Passage 6|[14]]].
*   **Loss of Transcription Factor Activation:** The reduction of *FTO* is also linked to the loss of activation by the transcription factor **SP1**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/li_2026_the_role_of_n_lt_sup_gt_6.pdf#Passage 1|[15]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/li_2026_the_role_of_n_lt_sup_gt_6.pdf#Passage 2|[16]]].
*   **Feedback Loops:** In certain metabolic stress contexts, **CEBPA suppression** is reported to inhibit *FTO* transcription [17].

In summary, the sources document that **GWAS-identified variants (like rs9939609)** exist and are relevant to granulosa-mediated diseases like PCOS, but they do not report specific mutations or structural alterations that function to silence the *FTO* promoter. The reduction of *FTO* transcription observed in aging and ovarian insufficiency is consistently treated as a **stress-induced regulatory event** rather than a consequence of a primary DNA sequence alteration at the promoter.
