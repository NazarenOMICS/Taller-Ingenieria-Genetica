# Q03 - Which transcription factors or promoter-bound repressors are reported to regulate FTO transcription directly, and is any of them linked to oxidative stress, senescence, or ovarian aging?

Based on the provided sources, several transcription factors are reported to directly regulate the transcription of the ***FTO*** **gene**, primarily acting as activators whose suppression or activation is linked to oxidative stress and reproductive pathologies. No specific proteins are explicitly identified as "promoter-bound repressors" for *FTO* in these contexts; instead, downregulation is typically described as a result of lost activation or complex feedback loops.

The following regulators and their links to stress and aging are reported:

### 1. Specific Transcription Factors Regulating *FTO*
*   **SP1 (Specificity Protein 1):**
    *   **Regulation:** SP1 is identified as a positive regulator that **activates the transcription of *FTO***  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/kordowitzki_2024_oocyte_aging_a_multifactorial_phenomenon_in_a_unique.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/kordowitzki_2024_oocyte_aging_a_multifactorial_phenomenon_in_a_unique.pdf#Passage 2|[2]]].
    *   **Link to Oxidative Stress:** SP1-mediated activation of *FTO* is specifically linked to the response to **ischemia-reperfusion injury** (a form of oxidative stress)  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/kordowitzki_2024_oocyte_aging_a_multifactorial_phenomenon_in_a_unique.pdf#Passage 2|[2]]]. In this model, SP1-driven *FTO* expression improves recovery by activating autophagy flux  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/kordowitzki_2024_oocyte_aging_a_multifactorial_phenomenon_in_a_unique.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/kordowitzki_2024_oocyte_aging_a_multifactorial_phenomenon_in_a_unique.pdf#Passage 2|[2]]].
    *   **Link to Aging:** While not described as an *FTO* regulator in oocytes, the sources note that under **aging and oxidative stress**, the binding sites for Sp1 in *other* gene promoters (like *LEDGF*) become hypermethylated, suppressing their expression  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/kordowitzki_2024_oocyte_aging_a_multifactorial_phenomenon_in_a_unique.pdf#Passage 3|[3]]]. This suggests a general mechanism where age-related epigenetic changes could interfere with SP1's ability to maintain *FTO* transcription.
*   **CEBPA (CCAAT/Enhancer-Binding Protein Alpha):**
    *   **Regulation:** CEBPA is linked to *FTO* transcription through a feedback loop  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/kordowitzki_2024_oocyte_aging_a_multifactorial_phenomenon_in_a_unique.pdf#Passage 2|[2]]]. Suppression of CEBPA has been shown to **inhibit *FTO* transcription**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/kordowitzki_2024_oocyte_aging_a_multifactorial_phenomenon_in_a_unique.pdf#Passage 2|[2]]].
    *   **Link to Stress/Cancer:** This mechanism is documented in the context of the oncometabolite **R-2HG**, which induces metabolic stress and inhibits *FTO* activity  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/kordowitzki_2024_oocyte_aging_a_multifactorial_phenomenon_in_a_unique.pdf#Passage 2|[2]]]. R-2HG inhibits *FTO* transcription by driving the suppression of CEBPA  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/kordowitzki_2024_oocyte_aging_a_multifactorial_phenomenon_in_a_unique.pdf#Passage 2|[2]]].
*   **Androgen Receptor (AR):**
    *   **Regulation:** AR activation is reported to **trigger increased *FTO* levels** in ovarian contexts  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/kordowitzki_2024_oocyte_aging_a_multifactorial_phenomenon_in_a_unique.pdf#Passage 2|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/dilek_2019_psychological_and_oxidative_stress_induce_apoptosis_through_trpv1.pdf#Passage 1|[4]]].
    *   **Link to Ovarian Pathologies:** In granulosa cells from **Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS)** patients, AR activation creates a positive feedback loop that upregulates *FTO*, which in turn exacerbates hyperandrogenism  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/kordowitzki_2024_oocyte_aging_a_multifactorial_phenomenon_in_a_unique.pdf#Passage 2|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/dilek_2019_psychological_and_oxidative_stress_induce_apoptosis_through_trpv1.pdf#Passage 1|[4]]].

### 2. Links to Ovarian Aging and Senescence
While the sources document that ***FTO* expression significantly drops** during **ovarian aging** and **granulosa cell (GC) senescence**, they do not always name the specific transcription factor responsible for this decline. However, they establish the following:
*   **ROS-Driven Downregulation:** Reactive oxygen species (ROS) are explicitly reported to activate pathways that lead to **the downregulation of *FTO***, which then promotes GC senescence by allowing the accumulation of m6A on aging-related transcripts like ***FOS***  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/kordowitzki_2024_oocyte_aging_a_multifactorial_phenomenon_in_a_unique.pdf#Passage 2|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/dilek_2019_psychological_and_oxidative_stress_induce_apoptosis_through_trpv1.pdf#Passage 1|[4]]].
*   **Chemotherapy-Induced Silencing:** Iatrogenic stress from agents like **cisplatin or cyclophosphamide** (which induce oxidative stress and DNA damage) causes a marked decrease in *FTO* transcription and protein levels in GCs  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/kordowitzki_2024_oocyte_aging_a_multifactorial_phenomenon_in_a_unique.pdf#Passage 2|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/dilek_2019_psychological_and_oxidative_stress_induce_apoptosis_through_trpv1.pdf#Passage 1|[4]]].
*   **Absence of Named Repressors:** The sources focus on the *loss* of *FTO* as a driver of the **SASP (senescence-associated secretory phenotype)** and organic decline, rather than identifying a specific repressor protein that actively binds the *FTO* promoter to silence it during aging  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/kordowitzki_2024_oocyte_aging_a_multifactorial_phenomenon_in_a_unique.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/kordowitzki_2024_oocyte_aging_a_multifactorial_phenomenon_in_a_unique.pdf#Passage 2|[2]]].
