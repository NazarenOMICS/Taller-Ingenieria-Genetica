## Resumen por QA

## Q01
- Pregunta: According to these sources, is there direct evidence that DNA methylation at the FTO promoter (or promoter-proximal CpG island) explains decreased FTO transcription under oxidative stress, aging, or ovarian aging/granulosa contexts?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/QA/answers/2026-07-24 Q01 - according-to-these-sources-there-direct.md]]

Based on the provided sources, there is **no direct evidence** that DNA methylation at the *FTO* promoter (or a promoter-proximal CpG island) explains the decreased *FTO* transcription observed under oxidative stress, aging, or ovarian/granulosa contexts.
## Q02
- Pregunta: According to these sources, is reduced H3K27ac or HDAC-mediated deacetylation at the FTO promoter proposed or demonstrated as a mechanism for FTO downregulation, especially in granulosa or oxidative-stress models?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/QA/answers/2026-07-24 Q02 - according-to-these-sources-reduced-h3k27ac.md]]

Based on the provided sources, there is **no direct evidence or specific proposal** that reduced H3K27ac or HDAC-mediated deacetylation at the *FTO* promoter is the mechanism for *FTO* downregulation in granulosa cells or oxidative stress models.
## Q03
- Pregunta: Which transcription factors or promoter-bound repressors are reported to regulate FTO transcription directly, and is any of them linked to oxidative stress, senescence, or ovarian aging?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/QA/answers/2026-07-24 Q03 - which-transcription-factors-or-promoter-bound.md]]

Based on the provided sources, several transcription factors are reported to directly regulate the transcription of the ***FTO*** **gene**, primarily acting as activators whose suppression or activation is linked to oxidative stress and reproductive pathologies. No specific proteins are explicitly identified as "promoter-bound repressors" for *FTO* in these contexts; instead, downregulation is typically described as a result of lost activation or complex feedback loops.
## Q04
- Pregunta: Do these sources report promoter-region mutations, cis-regulatory variants, or structural alterations that silence or reduce FTO transcription in any relevant tissue, and are any such findings applicable to granulosa biology?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/QA/answers/2026-07-24 Q04 - do-these-sources-report-promoter-region.md]]

Based on the provided sources, there are **no reports of promoter-region mutations, cis-regulatory variants, or structural alterations that directly silence or reduce *FTO* transcription** in granulosa cells or other tissues. Instead, the sources focus on a specific common genetic variant and attribute transcriptional changes to stress-induced pathways or hormonal signaling.
## Q05
- Pregunta: Across these candidate mechanisms—DNA methylation, H3K27ac/HDAC changes, promoter-bound repressors, or promoter sequence alterations—which is best supported for explaining FTO downregulation, and how strong is the evidence specifically in granulosa versus only by extrapolation from other tissues?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/QA/answers/2026-07-24 Q05 - across-these-candidate-mechanisms-dna-methylation.md]]

Among the candidate mechanisms provided, **transcription factor-mediated regulation (specifically the loss of SP1-mediated activation)** is the only one with direct support in the sources for explaining the control of ***FTO* transcription**, although this evidence is primarily derived from non-granulosa tissues and applied to stress models by extrapolation  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/Sources/li_2026_the_role_of_n_lt_sup_gt_6.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]].
## Q06
- Pregunta: What key evidence gaps remain before concluding the promoter-level mechanism of FTO downregulation in human granulosa cells under oxidative stress or ovarian aging?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q15-promotor-fto-silenciamiento/QA/answers/2026-07-24 Q06 - what-key-evidence-gaps-remain-before.md]]

While the sources provide extensive phenotypic evidence that **FTO expression (mRNA and protein) is significantly downregulated** in human granulosa cells (GCs) under oxidative stress and aging, they leave a substantial **mechanistic gap** regarding the primary events occurring at the *FTO* gene promoter.
