Methods An Fto knockout (FtoKO) mouse model was generated to investigate the role of Fto in cerebellar devel‑ opment. Cerebellar function was assessed using the behavioral tests and Nissl staining. Immunofluorescence was performed to detect molecular expression levels and subcellular localization. Dot blot,  m6A‑RIP‑seq, ATAC‑seq and CUT&Tag‑seq were used to confirm  m6A levels and chromatin accessibility. Co‑IP was employed to test molecular interactions.
