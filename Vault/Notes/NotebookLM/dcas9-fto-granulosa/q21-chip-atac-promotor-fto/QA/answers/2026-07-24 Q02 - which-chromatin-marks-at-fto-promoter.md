# Q02 - Which chromatin marks at the FTO promoter are reported across public datasets, especially H3K27ac, H3K4me3, H3K27me3, or accessibility signals?

Public datasets, including those from **ENCODE**, **Roadmap Epigenomics**, and independent studies, report several key chromatin marks and accessibility signals at the ***FTO*** locus and its promoter/regulatory regions across various tissues.

### **Active Chromatin Marks (H3K27ac and H3K4me3)**
*   **H3K27ac (Acetylation):** This mark, associated with **active enhancers and promoters**, is reported at the ***FTO*** locus in pathologically relevant cell types such as **pancreatic islets**, **HUVECs**, and **brain microvascular endothelial cells (BMECs)**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/madhusudhan_2019_atac_seq_reveals_alterations_in_open_chromatin_in.pdf#Passage 2|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/srivastava_2026_regulatory_risk_loci_link_disrupted_androgen_response_to.pdf#Passage 6|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/srivastava_2026_regulatory_risk_loci_link_disrupted_androgen_response_to.pdf#Passage 5|[3]]]. In human brain datasets (adult and fetal cortex), H3K27ac is used to define high-confidence regulatory chromatin interactions involving ***FTO***  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/paola_2018_using_three_dimensional_regulatory_chromatin_interactions_from_adult.pdf#Passage 1|[4]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/paola_2018_using_three_dimensional_regulatory_chromatin_interactions_from_adult.pdf#Passage 2|[5]]].
*   **H3K4me3 (Trimethylation):** Reported as a mark for **active promoters**, H3K4me3 is enriched at islet open chromatin regions  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/madhusudhan_2019_atac_seq_reveals_alterations_in_open_chromatin_in.pdf#Passage 2|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/madhusudhan_2019_atac_seq_reveals_alterations_in_open_chromatin_in.pdf#Passage 3|[6]]]. Public datasets from human brain tissue and pig epigenomic atlases also utilize H3K4me3 signals to map the interaction between promoters and distal enhancers within the ***FTO*** regulatory environment  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/paola_2018_using_three_dimensional_regulatory_chromatin_interactions_from_adult.pdf#Passage 1|[4]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/paola_2018_using_three_dimensional_regulatory_chromatin_interactions_from_adult.pdf#Passage 2|[5]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/yunxia_2021_a_compendium_and_comparative_epigenomics_analysis_of_cis.pdf#Passage 1|[7]]].

### **Repressive Marks (H3K27me3)**
*   **H3K27me3:** This **repressive heterochromatin mark** is generally reported as **underrepresented** in the accessible regions of the ***FTO*** locus  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/madhusudhan_2019_atac_seq_reveals_alterations_in_open_chromatin_in.pdf#Passage 2|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/madhusudhan_2019_atac_seq_reveals_alterations_in_open_chromatin_in.pdf#Passage 4|[8]]]. In human pancreatic islet datasets, H3K27me3 overlaps with only a small fraction (approximately **1.5%**) of open chromatin peaks, contrasting with the high enrichment of active marks  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/madhusudhan_2019_atac_seq_reveals_alterations_in_open_chromatin_in.pdf#Passage 2|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/madhusudhan_2019_atac_seq_reveals_alterations_in_open_chromatin_in.pdf#Passage 3|[6]]].

### **Chromatin Accessibility Signals**
*   **ATAC-seq:** Extensive open chromatin profiling has been performed on the ***FTO*** region. In human **pancreatic islets**, ATAC-seq peaks representing open chromatin overlap with at least 13 T2D-associated SNPs and 67 SNPs in linkage disequilibrium (LD) annotated to ***FTO***  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/madhusudhan_2019_atac_seq_reveals_alterations_in_open_chromatin_in.pdf#Passage 1|[9]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/zhang_2025_fosl2_facilitates_chromatin_accessibility_to_determine_developmental_events.pdf#Passage 4|[10]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/zhang_2025_fosl2_facilitates_chromatin_accessibility_to_determine_developmental_events.pdf#Passage 2|[11]]]. Similarly, studies in **granulosa cells** have provided Integrated Genomics Viewer (IGV) tracks showing distinct ATAC-seq signals at the ***Fto*** locus  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/srivastava_2026_regulatory_risk_loci_link_disrupted_androgen_response_to.pdf#Passage 4|[12]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/srivastava_2026_regulatory_risk_loci_link_disrupted_androgen_response_to.pdf#Passage 3|[13]]].
*   **DNase-seq:** ENCODE datasets provide **DNase I hypersensitivity maps** that have been integrated with other omics data to predict regulatory variants (reSNVs) within the intronic enhancers of ***FTO***  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/srivastava_2026_regulatory_risk_loci_link_disrupted_androgen_response_to.pdf#Passage 6|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/e_2021_disease_associated_genetic_variants_in_the_regulatory_regions.pdf#Passage 6|[14]]].

### **Summary of Regulatory Features**
The active regulatory regions at the ***FTO*** locus are consistently marked by a combination of **chromatin accessibility (ATAC/DNase-seq)** and **active histone modifications (H3K27ac and H3K4me3)**, while repressive marks like H3K27me3 are largely absent from these functional sites  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/madhusudhan_2019_atac_seq_reveals_alterations_in_open_chromatin_in.pdf#Passage 2|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/srivastava_2026_regulatory_risk_loci_link_disrupted_androgen_response_to.pdf#Passage 6|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/paola_2018_using_three_dimensional_regulatory_chromatin_interactions_from_adult.pdf#Passage 1|[4]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/paola_2018_using_three_dimensional_regulatory_chromatin_interactions_from_adult.pdf#Passage 3|[15]]]. These signals collectively identify distal enhancers within the ***FTO*** gene that form long-range 3D contacts with target genes like *IRX3* and *IRX5* [16-18].
