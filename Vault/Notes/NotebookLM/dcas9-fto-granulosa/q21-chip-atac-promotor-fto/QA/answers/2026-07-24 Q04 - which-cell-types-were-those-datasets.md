# Q04 - In which cell types were those datasets generated, and how relevant are they to ovarian or oxidative-stress biology?

The datasets profiling the ***FTO*** locus and its regulatory environment were generated across a diverse array of cell types, many of which are directly relevant to metabolic, reproductive, and stress-related biological processes.

### **Cell Types Used for Dataset Generation**
Public datasets from repositories like ENCODE, Cistrome, and specific studies were generated in the following cell and tissue types:
*   **Reproductive Lineages:** Human **granulosa-like cells (KGN)**, porcine and murine **granulosa cells (GCs)**, and single **oocytes** (at germinal vesicle and MII stages)  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/huan_2023_scm6a_seq_reveals_single_cell_landscapes_of_the.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/srivastava_2026_regulatory_risk_loci_link_disrupted_androgen_response_to.pdf#Passage 10|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/zhang_2025_fosl2_facilitates_chromatin_accessibility_to_determine_developmental_events.pdf#Passage 5|[3]]].
*   **Metabolic and Endocrine Tissues:** Human **pancreatic islets** (from diabetic and non-diabetic donors), **adipocytes** (and pre-adipocytes), **liver**, and **skeletal muscle**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/anthony_2018_shared_genetic_contribution_to_type_1_and_type.pdf#Passage 1|[4]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/madhusudhan_2019_atac_seq_reveals_alterations_in_open_chromatin_in.pdf#Passage 5|[5]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/srivastava_2026_regulatory_risk_loci_link_disrupted_androgen_response_to.pdf#Passage 11|[6]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/yunxia_2021_a_compendium_and_comparative_epigenomics_analysis_of_cis.pdf#Passage 2|[7]]].
*   **Neuroendocrine and Brain Regions:** **Fetal brain**, adult **temporal cortex**, **hypothalamus** (specifically the arcuate nucleus), and **pituitary**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/srivastava_2026_regulatory_risk_loci_link_disrupted_androgen_response_to.pdf#Passage 10|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/yi_chun_2010_hypothalamic_specific_manipulation_of_fto_the_ortholog_of.pdf#Passage 1|[8]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/srivastava_2026_regulatory_risk_loci_link_disrupted_androgen_response_to.pdf#Passage 6|[9]]].
*   **Endothelial Proxies:** **Human umbilical vein endothelial cells (HUVECs)** and **brain microvascular endothelial cells (BMECs)**, which are sometimes used as proxies for granulosa cell epigenomics due to similar H3K27ac profiles  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/srivastava_2026_regulatory_risk_loci_link_disrupted_androgen_response_to.pdf#Passage 10|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/anthony_2018_shared_genetic_contribution_to_type_1_and_type.pdf#Passage 2|[10]]].
*   **Immune and Research Cell Lines:** **CD19+ B cells**, **CD8+ T cells**, and **Jurkat cells**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/haiyan_2024_ythdf2_upregulation_and_subcellular_localization_dictate_cd8_t.pdf#Passage 1|[11]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/haiyan_2024_ythdf2_upregulation_and_subcellular_localization_dictate_cd8_t.pdf#Passage 2|[12]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/srivastava_2026_regulatory_risk_loci_link_disrupted_androgen_response_to.pdf#Passage 12|[13]]].

### **Relevance to Ovarian Biology**
The sources establish several critical links between these datasets and ovarian function, particularly regarding **Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS)** and **folliculogenesis**:
*   **PCOS Pathogenesis:** The ***FTO*** locus is used as a primary case study for understanding how noncoding risk variants link obesity to PCOS  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/zhang_2025_fosl2_facilitates_chromatin_accessibility_to_determine_developmental_events.pdf#Passage 1|[14]]]. Regulatory disruptions at the ***FTO*** locus in tissues like the fetal brain and pancreatic islets are hypothesized to drive PCOS through **impaired follicular maturation** and metabolic dysfunction  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/zhang_2025_fosl2_facilitates_chromatin_accessibility_to_determine_developmental_events.pdf#Passage 1|[14]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/zhang_2025_fosl2_facilitates_chromatin_accessibility_to_determine_developmental_events.pdf#Passage 2|[15]]].
*   **Androgen Signaling:** Variants in the ***FTO*** region (such as **rs1421085**) are predicted to disrupt the binding of repressors like **ONECUT2**, which is a known suppressor of androgen receptor signaling and a marker of follicle growth  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/anthony_2018_shared_genetic_contribution_to_type_1_and_type.pdf#Passage 2|[10]]].
*   **Follicular Maturation:** Research in porcine and murine GCs identifies a dynamic chromatin accessibility landscape during maturation. While some genes like *Cyp11a1* and *Star* show massive shifts, ***Fto*** expression was found to be relatively **unchanged** during these specific developmental transitions, serving as a stable control in certain models  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/paper_2024_eacr_2024_innovative_cancer_science_10_13_june.pdf#Passage 1|[16]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/paper_2024_eacr_2024_innovative_cancer_science_10_13_june.pdf#Passage 2|[17]]].
*   **Ovarian Cancer:** Clinical datasets explore the role of oxidative stress and T-cell exhaustion in high-grade serous ovarian cancer, highlighting the intersection of metabolic regulation and tumor microenvironments  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/angelo_2007_genome_wide_association_scan_shows_genetic_variants_in.pdf#Passage 1|[18]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/haiyan_2024_ythdf2_upregulation_and_subcellular_localization_dictate_cd8_t.pdf#Passage 3|[19]]].

### **Relevance to Oxidative-Stress Biology**
Relevance to oxidative stress is documented through metabolic regulation and the cell's response to environmental stressors:
*   **Heat Shock Response:** Early research suggests ***FTO*** is involved in stress responses, as the gene is **down-regulated** when the heat shock response transcription factor **Htf1** is inhibited  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/yang_2026_the_orchestrated_interplay_between_dna_methylation_and_n6.pdf#Passage 2|[20]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/yang_2026_the_orchestrated_interplay_between_dna_methylation_and_n6.pdf#Passage 3|[21]]].
*   **Mitochondrial Stress and ROS:** The m6A reader **YTHDF2** (which interacts with m6A marks placed or erased by the ***FTO*** machinery) is essential for preventing **mitochondrial stress**. Loss of this regulation leads to the accumulation of **reactive oxygen species (ROS)** and T-cell exhaustion  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/haiyan_2024_ythdf2_upregulation_and_subcellular_localization_dictate_cd8_t.pdf#Passage 2|[12]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/paper_2024_eacr_2024_innovative_cancer_science_10_13_june.pdf#Passage 3|[22]]].
*   **Hypoxia and Senescence:** Interplay between DNA methylation and m6A at the ***FTO*** locus is observed in responses to **hypoxia** and **oxidative stress-induced senescence** (using H2O2 models), where these mechanisms synergize to regulate cell-cycle arrest [24-26].
*   **Ferroptosis in Ovarian Models:** Ovarian cancer research utilizes these datasets to understand how **oxidative stress** and **ferroptosis** (an iron-dependent form of cell death) can be harnessed to overcome chemotherapy resistance  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/haiyan_2024_ythdf2_upregulation_and_subcellular_localization_dictate_cd8_t.pdf#Passage 3|[19]]].

> Abstract A major challenge in deciphering the complex genetic landscape of polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS) lies in the limited understanding of how susceptibility loci drive molecular mechanisms across diverse phenotypes. To address this, we integrated molecular and epigenomic annotations from proposed causal cell types and employed a deep learning (DL) framework to predict cell type–specific regulatory effects of PCOS-risk variants.
