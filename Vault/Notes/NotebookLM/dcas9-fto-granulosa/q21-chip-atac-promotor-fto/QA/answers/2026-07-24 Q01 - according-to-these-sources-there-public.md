# Q01 - According to these sources, are there public ENCODE, Cistrome, or related datasets that profile the FTO promoter region by ChIP-seq, CUT&RUN, or ATAC-seq?

Based on the provided sources, there are multiple public datasets from **ENCODE**, **Cistrome**, and related repositories that profile the ***FTO*** locus—including its promoter and regulatory regions—using ChIP-seq, ATAC-seq, and CUT&Tag (a related method to CUT&RUN).

### **Public Repositories and Data Types**
The sources explicitly identify several major information resources that aggregate these experimental data types:
*   **ENCODE (The Encyclopedia of DNA Elements):** Provides genome-wide profiles of **histone modifications** (ChIP-seq), **transcription factor (TF) binding** (ChIP-seq), **chromatin accessibility** (DNase-seq and ATAC-seq), and **3D chromatin interactions**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/e_2021_disease_associated_genetic_variants_in_the_regulatory_regions.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/e_2021_disease_associated_genetic_variants_in_the_regulatory_regions.pdf#Passage 2|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/srivastava_2026_regulatory_risk_loci_link_disrupted_androgen_response_to.pdf#Passage 1|[3]]].
*   **Cistrome Data Browser:** A resource specifically for **ChIP-seq**, **DNase-seq**, and **ATAC-seq** data, including genomic regions interacting with TFs and the binding locations of modified histone proteins  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/e_2021_disease_associated_genetic_variants_in_the_regulatory_regions.pdf#Passage 3|[4]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/e_2021_disease_associated_genetic_variants_in_the_regulatory_regions.pdf#Passage 4|[5]]].
*   **Other Related Databases:** Sources also mention **ReMap** and **GTRD** for TF binding profiling  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/e_2021_disease_associated_genetic_variants_in_the_regulatory_regions.pdf#Passage 3|[4]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/e_2021_disease_associated_genetic_variants_in_the_regulatory_regions.pdf#Passage 5|[6]]], and the **UDACHA** and **ADASTRA** databases for evaluating allele-specific effects on TF binding and chromatin accessibility  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/srivastava_2026_regulatory_risk_loci_link_disrupted_androgen_response_to.pdf#Passage 2|[7]]].

### **Specific Profiling of the *FTO* Region**
The sources highlight that the *FTO* region has been extensively mapped in public and specialized datasets:
*   **ATAC-seq and Open Chromatin:** Public ATAC-seq data from human **pancreatic islets** have been used to map open chromatin, identifying peaks that overlap with SNPs annotated to ***FTO***  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/madhusudhan_2019_atac_seq_reveals_alterations_in_open_chromatin_in.pdf#Passage 1|[8]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/zhang_2025_fosl2_facilitates_chromatin_accessibility_to_determine_developmental_events.pdf#Passage 1|[9]]]. Furthermore, research into follicular maturation provided **IGV views** specifically displaying **ATAC-seq** signals at the ***Fto*** locus  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/zhang_2025_fosl2_facilitates_chromatin_accessibility_to_determine_developmental_events.pdf#Passage 2|[10]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/zhang_2025_fosl2_facilitates_chromatin_accessibility_to_determine_developmental_events.pdf#Passage 3|[11]]].
*   **CUT&Tag / CUT&RUN:** While the query asks for CUT&RUN, the sources describe the use of **CUT&Tag** (a similar technology) to profile ***Fto***. Specifically, studies in porcine granulosa cells generated genome-wide **Fosl2 CUT&Tag** data, which includes signal tracks at the ***Fto*** locus  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/zhang_2025_fosl2_facilitates_chromatin_accessibility_to_determine_developmental_events.pdf#Passage 3|[11]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/madhusudhan_2019_atac_seq_reveals_alterations_in_open_chromatin_in.pdf#Passage 2|[12]]].
*   **ChIP-seq (Histone Marks and TFs):** The sources note that active regulatory regions in the human genome, including those at the ***FTO*** locus, are marked by histone ChIP-seq data (such as **H3K27ac** and **H3K4me3**) sourced from ENCODE and the Roadmap Epigenomics Consortium  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/madhusudhan_2019_atac_seq_reveals_alterations_in_open_chromatin_in.pdf#Passage 3|[13]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/srivastava_2026_regulatory_risk_loci_link_disrupted_androgen_response_to.pdf#Passage 3|[14]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/srivastava_2026_regulatory_risk_loci_link_disrupted_androgen_response_to.pdf#Passage 4|[15]]]. 
*   **3D Interactions:** Data from the **ENCODE-rE2G model** and Hi-C maps (such as ENCODE accession **ENCSR788FB**) have been used to study the 3D interaction landscape of the ***FTO*** locus, particularly its distal enhancers that target other genes like *IRX3* and *IRX5*  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/srivastava_2026_regulatory_risk_loci_link_disrupted_androgen_response_to.pdf#Passage 5|[16]]].

In summary, the sources confirm that the ***FTO*** promoter and its wider regulatory environment are covered by extensive public profiling in the ENCODE and Cistrome collections across various tissue and cell types  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/e_2021_disease_associated_genetic_variants_in_the_regulatory_regions.pdf#Passage 3|[4]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/e_2021_disease_associated_genetic_variants_in_the_regulatory_regions.pdf#Passage 4|[5]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/zhang_2025_fosl2_facilitates_chromatin_accessibility_to_determine_developmental_events.pdf#Passage 1|[9]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q21-chip-atac-promotor-fto/Sources/zhang_2025_fosl2_facilitates_chromatin_accessibility_to_determine_developmental_events.pdf#Passage 3|[11]]].
