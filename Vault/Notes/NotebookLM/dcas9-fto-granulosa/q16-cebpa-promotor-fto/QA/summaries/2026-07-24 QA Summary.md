## Q01
- Pregunta: According to these sources, is C/EBPα directly reported to bind the FTO promoter, and in which cell types or tissues was that binding demonstrated?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q16-cebpa-promotor-fto/QA/answers/2026-07-24 Q01 - according-to-these-sources-c-ebp.md]]

Yes, according to the sources, **C/EBPα is directly reported to bind to the FTO promoter**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q16-cebpa-promotor-fto/Sources/zewei_2024_insights_into_the_m6a_demethylases_fto_and_alkbh5.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q16-cebpa-promotor-fto/Sources/zewei_2024_insights_into_the_m6a_demethylases_fto_and_alkbh5.pdf#Passage 2|[2]]].
## Q02
- Pregunta: What molecular mechanisms regulate C/EBPα occupancy at the FTO promoter, including chromatin accessibility, co-factors, DNA methylation, or histone acetylation?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q16-cebpa-promotor-fto/QA/answers/2026-07-24 Q02 - what-molecular-mechanisms-regulate-c-ebp.md]]

Based on the sources, the regulation of **C/EBPα occupancy** and overall transcriptional activity at the ***FTO* promoter** involves a combination of direct transcription factor binding, recruitment of histone modifiers, DNA methylation changes, and metabolic sensing.
## Q03
- Pregunta: Is there evidence that oxidative stress changes C/EBPα occupancy, activity, or promoter binding at FTO or analogous loci in any cell type?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q16-cebpa-promotor-fto/QA/answers/2026-07-24 Q03 - there-evidence-that-oxidative-stress-changes.md]]

While the sources do not provide direct evidence that oxidative stress specifically changes **C/EBPα occupancy** at the ***FTO* promoter**, they do establish C/EBPα as a key regulator of *FTO* and provide extensive evidence of oxidative stress directing transcription factor occupancy at **analogous stress-responsive loci**.
## Q04
- Pregunta: Is any C/EBPα-to-FTO regulatory mechanism shown directly in granulosa or ovarian cells, or is the evidence only indirect or from other tissues?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q16-cebpa-promotor-fto/QA/answers/2026-07-24 Q04 - any-c-ebp-to-fto-regulatory.md]]

Based on the sources, the direct regulatory mechanism of **C/EBPα binding to the *FTO* promoter** is primarily demonstrated in **Acute Myeloid Leukemia (AML)** and **obesity-associated contexts**, rather than in granulosa or ovarian cells.
## Q05
- Pregunta: What is the best-supported model linking C/EBPα regulation to FTO transcriptional control, and how strong is the evidence for extrapolating that model to oxidative-stress-induced FTO loss in granulosa?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q16-cebpa-promotor-fto/QA/answers/2026-07-24 Q05 - what-best-supported-model-linking-c.md]]

The best-supported model for **C/EBPα** regulation of ***FTO*** transcriptional control is one of **direct positive activation** through promoter occupancy. However, the evidence for extrapolating this model to oxidative-stress-induced *FTO* loss in granulosa cells is **indirect and relatively weak** compared to established mechanisms in other tissues.
