# Q04 - Is any C/EBPα-to-FTO regulatory mechanism shown directly in granulosa or ovarian cells, or is the evidence only indirect or from other tissues?

Based on the sources, the direct regulatory mechanism of **C/EBPα binding to the *FTO* promoter** is primarily demonstrated in **Acute Myeloid Leukemia (AML)** and **obesity-associated contexts**, rather than in granulosa or ovarian cells.

### **Direct Evidence in Other Tissues**
The sources provide direct evidence of C/EBPα occupying the *FTO* promoter in the following specific contexts:
*   **Acute Myeloid Leukemia (AML):** C/EBPα is identified as an **exclusive transcription factor** that regulates *FTO* transcription  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q16-cebpa-promotor-fto/Sources/xiaoyu_2019_the_role_of_m6a_rna_methylation_in_human.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]]. There is a noted positive correlation between C/EBPα and *FTO* levels in these cells  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q16-cebpa-promotor-fto/Sources/xiaoyu_2019_the_role_of_m6a_rna_methylation_in_human.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]].
*   **Human Obesity-Associated Contexts:** Researchers identified a **highly conserved C/EBP binding site** near the transcription start site (TSS) of the human *FTO* gene  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q16-cebpa-promotor-fto/Sources/zewei_2024_insights_into_the_m6a_demethylases_fto_and_alkbh5.pdf#Passage 1|[2]]]. **Chromatin immunoprecipitation (ChIP)** experiments demonstrated that C/EBPα directly binds to this region to act as a **positive regulatory factor**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q16-cebpa-promotor-fto/Sources/zewei_2024_insights_into_the_m6a_demethylases_fto_and_alkbh5.pdf#Passage 1|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q16-cebpa-promotor-fto/Sources/zewei_2024_insights_into_the_m6a_demethylases_fto_and_alkbh5.pdf#Passage 2|[3]]].
*   **Peripheral Blood Mononuclear Cells (PBMCs):** Coordinated downregulation of *CEBPα* and *FTO* mRNA has been observed in PBMCs following weight loss, suggesting an integrated response, though promoter occupancy was not the primary focus in this tissue  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q16-cebpa-promotor-fto/Sources/bathina_2026_lifestyle_intervention_therapy_modulates_global_dna_methylation_and.pdf#Passage 1|[4]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q16-cebpa-promotor-fto/Sources/bathina_2026_lifestyle_intervention_therapy_modulates_global_dna_methylation_and.pdf#Passage 5|[5]]].

### **Indirect or Absent Evidence in Ovarian Contexts**
While the sources discuss *FTO* activity in ovarian cells, they do not directly link it to C/EBPα promoter occupancy in those specific cells:
*   **FTO Function in Ovarian Cancer:** *FTO* is characterized as an **antioncogene** in ovarian cancer and ovarian cancer stem cells (CSCs)  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q16-cebpa-promotor-fto/Sources/zewei_2024_insights_into_the_m6a_demethylases_fto_and_alkbh5.pdf#Passage 8|[6]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q16-cebpa-promotor-fto/Sources/zewei_2024_insights_into_the_m6a_demethylases_fto_and_alkbh5.pdf#Passage 9|[7]]]. Its role involves demethylating **PDE1C and PDE4B** mRNAs to activate cAMP signaling and suppress stemness  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q16-cebpa-promotor-fto/Sources/zhang_2026_rna_modifications_in_cancer_stem_cells_molecular_mechanisms.pdf#Passage 1|[8]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q16-cebpa-promotor-fto/Sources/zhang_2026_rna_modifications_in_cancer_stem_cells_molecular_mechanisms.pdf#Passage 2|[9]]].
*   **Regulatory Mechanism Not Specified:** Although low *FTO* expression is noted in ovarian CSCs, the sources focus on its **downstream targets** (like PDE1C and PDE4B) rather than demonstrating that **C/EBPα** is the factor responsible for its expression or silencing at the promoter level in these tissues  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q16-cebpa-promotor-fto/Sources/zhang_2026_rna_modifications_in_cancer_stem_cells_molecular_mechanisms.pdf#Passage 2|[9]]].
*   **Granulosa Cells:** While one source mentions the effects of nanoplastics on human ovarian granulosa cells, it does not detail a C/EBPα-to-FTO regulatory mechanism within that cell type  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q16-cebpa-promotor-fto/Sources/rajaganapathi_2025_tead1_is_a_novel_regulator_of_nrf2_and.pdf#Passage 6|[10]]].

In summary, the **C/EBPα-to-*FTO*** promoter binding mechanism is established as a general regulatory principle for the human *FTO* gene and specifically shown in **AML**, but there is **no direct evidence** in the sources demonstrating this occupancy specifically in **granulosa or ovarian cells**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q16-cebpa-promotor-fto/Sources/xiaoyu_2019_the_role_of_m6a_rna_methylation_in_human.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q16-cebpa-promotor-fto/Sources/zewei_2024_insights_into_the_m6a_demethylases_fto_and_alkbh5.pdf#Passage 1|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q16-cebpa-promotor-fto/Sources/zhang_2026_rna_modifications_in_cancer_stem_cells_molecular_mechanisms.pdf#Passage 2|[9]]].
