# Q01 - According to these sources, is C/EBPα directly reported to bind the FTO promoter, and in which cell types or tissues was that binding demonstrated?

Yes, according to the sources, **C/EBPα is directly reported to bind to the FTO promoter**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q16-cebpa-promotor-fto/Sources/zewei_2024_insights_into_the_m6a_demethylases_fto_and_alkbh5.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q16-cebpa-promotor-fto/Sources/zewei_2024_insights_into_the_m6a_demethylases_fto_and_alkbh5.pdf#Passage 2|[2]]].

### **Demonstration of Binding**
The direct binding of C/EBPα to the *FTO* promoter was demonstrated using **chromatin immunoprecipitation (ChIP) experiments**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q16-cebpa-promotor-fto/Sources/zewei_2024_insights_into_the_m6a_demethylases_fto_and_alkbh5.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]]. Researchers identified a **highly conserved binding site** for C/EBP located near the **transcription start site (TSS)** of the human *FTO* gene  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q16-cebpa-promotor-fto/Sources/zewei_2024_insights_into_the_m6a_demethylases_fto_and_alkbh5.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]]. This binding allows C/EBPα to act as a **positive regulatory factor**, binding to the promoter to enhance the transcription of *FTO*  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q16-cebpa-promotor-fto/Sources/zewei_2024_insights_into_the_m6a_demethylases_fto_and_alkbh5.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q16-cebpa-promotor-fto/Sources/zewei_2024_insights_into_the_m6a_demethylases_fto_and_alkbh5.pdf#Passage 2|[2]]].

### **Demonstrated Cell Types and Tissues**
The binding and transcriptional regulation of *FTO* by C/EBPα have been demonstrated in the following contexts:
*   **Acute Myeloid Leukemia (AML):** C/EBPα has been identified as an exclusive transcription factor that regulates *FTO* transcription in AML  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q16-cebpa-promotor-fto/Sources/xiaoyu_2019_the_role_of_m6a_rna_methylation_in_human.pdf#Passage 1|[3]]]. There is a noted positive correlation between the levels of C/EBPα and *FTO* in these cells  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q16-cebpa-promotor-fto/Sources/xiaoyu_2019_the_role_of_m6a_rna_methylation_in_human.pdf#Passage 1|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q16-cebpa-promotor-fto/Sources/zewei_2024_insights_into_the_m6a_demethylases_fto_and_alkbh5.pdf#Passage 3|[4]]].
*   **Human Obesity-Associated Contexts:** The interaction is also characterized in studies investigating the transcription of the "human fat mass and obesity associated gene," where C/EBPα is described as a crucial regulator of its expression  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q16-cebpa-promotor-fto/Sources/bathina_2026_lifestyle_intervention_therapy_modulates_global_dna_methylation_and.pdf#Passage 1|[5]]].
*   **Peripheral Blood Mononuclear Cells (PBMCs):** While direct promoter occupancy was not explicitly stated for this tissue, one study observed a **coordinated downregulation** of both *CEBPα* and *FTO* mRNA in the PBMCs of severely obese men following a 12-month lifestyle intervention, suggesting they part of a broad, integrated transcriptional response  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q16-cebpa-promotor-fto/Sources/bathina_2026_lifestyle_intervention_therapy_modulates_global_dna_methylation_and.pdf#Passage 2|[6]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q16-cebpa-promotor-fto/Sources/wen_2025_dynamic_rendition_of_adipose_genes_under_epigenetic_regulation.pdf#Passage 1|[7]]].

Conversely, in **adipose tissue** and preadipocytes, the sources describe a different relationship: FTO deficiency leads to reduced *C/EBPβ* transcription and impaired adipogenesis via the JAK2-STAT3 pathway, rather than focusing on C/EBPα binding to the *FTO* promoter  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q16-cebpa-promotor-fto/Sources/zewei_2024_insights_into_the_m6a_demethylases_fto_and_alkbh5.pdf#Passage 4|[8]]].

> Additionally, FTO influences lipid cell differentiation by regulating classical signaling pathways. FTO deficiency in porcine and murine preadipocytes inhibits adipogenesis via the JAK2-STAT3-C/EBPβ signaling pathway. Mecha-nistically, FTO deficiency inhibits JAK2 expression and STAT3 phosphorylation, leading to reduced C/EBPβ transcription, crucial for the early stages of adipocyte differentiation [44].
