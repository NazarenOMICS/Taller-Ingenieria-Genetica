## Hilo central del bloque

- The sources provide direct experimental and observational evidence linking **oxidative stress (ROS and hydrogen peroxide)** to a decline in **FTO expression and activity**, primarily in ovarian granulosa cells and ischemic somatic tissues.
- Based on the sources, oxidative stress (primarily from reactive oxygen species or hydrogen peroxide) causes downstream cellular senescence through several interconnected molecular mechanisms, most notably via **$m^6A$ dysregulation**, the **Nrf2 signaling pathway**, and the **DNA damage response**.
- The sources provide **direct causal evidence** that FTO decline mediates oxidative-stress-induced senescence through loss-of-function and rescue experiments, primarily in human ovarian granulosa cells (GCs).
- According to the sources, **Nrf2 (Nuclear factor erythroid 2-related factor 2) signaling** serves as a master regulator of cellular antioxidant defense, acting as a critical bridge that connects oxidative stress and $m^6A$ regulation to either promote cellular protection or allow downstream senescence.
