# Q06 - What gaps, unresolved questions, or lack of independent replication remain in these sources regarding the FTO-m6A-FOS axis in human granulosa cells?

Based on the provided sources, several significant gaps, unresolved questions, and instances of limited independent replication remain regarding the **FTO-m6A-FOS axis** in human granulosa cells (GCs).

### 1. Lack of Independent Replication of the Specific FOS Mechanism
While the corpuses' original research papers consistently confirm that **FTO expression is downregulated** and **global m6A levels are increased** in aged human GCs and POI patients, the specific identification of **FOS** as the critical downstream target is largely restricted to **Jiang et al. (2021)**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/sun_2022_the_role_of_m_sup_6_sup_a.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/li_2024_fat_mass_and_obesity_associated_protein_regulates_granulosa.pdf#Passage 10|[2]]]. 
*   **Target Divergence:** Subsequent original research in the corpus has focused on extending FTO's role to different targets, such as the **FTO-MMP2 axis**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/zhu_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 3|[3]]] and the **FTO-circBRCA1 axis**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/li_2024_fat_mass_and_obesity_associated_protein_regulates_granulosa.pdf#Passage 3|[4]]]. While these studies cite Jiang et al. (2021) as foundational, they do not explicitly replicate the FOS-mRNA stability experiments, leaving the "FOS-dependent" nature of GC senescence primarily supported by a single primary study  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/li_2024_fat_mass_and_obesity_associated_protein_regulates_granulosa.pdf#Passage 10|[2]]].
*   **Species Divergence:** Some related studies utilize mouse models (e.g., Li et al., 2024), and those authors explicitly note that it remains uncertain whether these specific aging pathways apply identically to **human ovaries** and GCs  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/chang_2022_transcriptome_wide_n6_methyladenine_methylation_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 4|[5]]].

### 2. Small Clinical Sample Sizes
A recurring limitation noted in the original research is the **small size of study groups** for high-throughput sequencing.
*   **Sequencing Limitations:** The General m6A profiling study (Chang et al., 2022) and the Jiang et al. (2021) study both utilized small cohorts (often only **three samples per group**) for MeRIP-seq and mRNA-seq  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jiang_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 15|[6]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jiang_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 9|[7]]]. 
*   **Requirement for Validation:** Authors emphasize that these findings must be interpreted with "great caution" and that confirmatory studies with **larger sample sizes** are required to validate global m6A patterns and specific target identification in human populations  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jiang_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 15|[6]]].

### 3. Unresolved "Reader" Coordination and Specificity
While **IGF2BP2** was identified as the primary reader for the FOS transcript, the "writer-eraser-reader" paradigm in GCs is not fully resolved.
*   **Reader Redundancy:** Jiang et al. (2021) noted that while IGF2BP2 knockdown accelerated FOS mRNA decay, differences between the functions of **IGF2BP1, 2, and 3** were not fully clarified, and residual differences in mRNA stability suggested that other readers might also contribute to the mechanism  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/erqing_2023_rna_n6_methyladenosine_modification_in_female_reproductive_biology.pdf#Passage 4|[8]]].
*   **Competitive Interactions:** General reviews in the corpus note that how various readers (e.g., YTHDF family vs. IGF2BP family) coordinate their functions on the same m6A-modified mRNA remains a "mystery" and requires further elucidation  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/liuer_2019_functions_of_n6_methyladenosine_and_its_role_in.pdf#Passage 1|[9]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/xiulin_2021_the_role_of_m6a_modification_in_the_biological.pdf#Passage 2|[10]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/erqing_2023_rna_n6_methyladenosine_modification_in_female_reproductive_biology.pdf#Passage 5|[11]]].

### 4. Integration of the Multi-Axis Network
The sources identify multiple pathways for FTO-mediated regulation in GCs (FOS, MMP2, circBRCA1), but they lack a **unified model** explaining how these axes interact.
*   **Co-regulation:** It is unclear if these targets are regulated by FTO simultaneously or if different GCs subpopulations prioritize one axis over the other  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/xinru_2024_the_role_of_epigenetics_in_women_s_reproductive.pdf#Passage 3|[12]]]. 
*   **Non-Coding RNA Gaps:** While the discovery of the **FTO-circBRCA1 axis** extends the mechanism to circular RNAs, the full scope of FTO's influence on the non-coding epitranscriptome (lncRNAs, miRNAs, circRNAs) in human GCs is still considered "only the tip of the iceberg"  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/liuer_2019_functions_of_n6_methyladenosine_and_its_role_in.pdf#Passage 1|[9]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/xinru_2024_the_role_of_epigenetics_in_women_s_reproductive.pdf#Passage 4|[13]]].

### 5. Interplay with the Endocrine System
Despite the ovary being a primary endocrine organ, research on the FTO-m6A-FOS axis has remained focused on **cellular behavior** (senescence, apoptosis).
*   **Hormonal Milieu:** The interplay between the **hormonal environment** (e.g., FSH/LH levels, estrogen) and the regulation of m6A modification remains poorly understood  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/liuer_2019_functions_of_n6_methyladenosine_and_its_role_in.pdf#Passage 1|[9]]]. 
*   **Endocrine Disruption:** While oxidative stress (ROS) is established as a trigger for FTO downregulation, the impact of other internal and external factors, such as psychological stress or dietary choices, on this specific axis is still fragmented  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/erqing_2023_rna_n6_methyladenosine_modification_in_female_reproductive_biology.pdf#Passage 6|[14]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jin_2022_the_potential_role_of_m6a_rna_methylation_in.pdf#Passage 2|[15]]].

### 6. Barriers to Clinical Translation
The corpus acknowledges that while the FTO-FOS axis is a potential diagnostic and therapeutic target, actual **clinical application** is far off.
*   **Sensitivity and Specificity:** Improving the sensitivity and specificity of m6A-related biomarkers is necessary before they can be used to classify clinical phenotypes of POI or predict ART outcomes  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/sun_2022_the_role_of_m_sup_6_sup_a.pdf#Passage 4|[16]]].
*   **Targeted Therapies:** There is a lack of identified drugs or inhibitors that can **specifically target m6A regulatory proteins** in a tissue-specific manner without causing systemic off-target effects [18, 19].
