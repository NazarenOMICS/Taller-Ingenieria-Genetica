# Q02 - What experimental models (cell lines, primary cells, animal models) and molecular techniques were used to establish the FTO-m6A-FOS axis, according to these sources?

According to the sources, particularly **Jiang et al. (2021)**, the establishment of the **FTO-m6A-FOS axis** relied on a combination of human clinical samples, specific granulosa cell lines, and a wide array of high-throughput and molecular biology techniques  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jiang_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 12|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jiang_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 9|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jiang_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 13|[3]]].

### Experimental Models
The researchers utilized three primary models to study the axis:

*   **Primary Human Cells:** Clinical samples of **ovarian granulosa cells (GCs)** were collected from women undergoing Assisted Reproductive Technology (ART)  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jiang_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 13|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jiang_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 14|[4]]]. These were categorized into "Normal Ovarian Reserve" (younger controls) and "Aged Ovary" groups to compare expression levels in vivo  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jiang_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 9|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jiang_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 13|[3]]]. Most of these samples were specifically **cumulus cells**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jiang_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 13|[3]]].
*   **Human Cell Lines:** Two immortalized human ovarian granulosa cell lines, **KGN** and **COV434**, were used for all functional and mechanistic experiments, including knockdown and overexpression studies  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jiang_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 9|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jiang_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 15|[5]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jiang_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 3|[6]]].
*   **Senescence Induction Model:** An in vitro model of granulosa cell senescence was established by treating the cell lines with **hydrogen peroxide ($H_2O_2$)** to simulate the accumulation of **reactive oxygen species (ROS)** seen in aging  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jiang_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 16|[7]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jiang_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 17|[8]]].

### Molecular Techniques
A series of advanced molecular techniques were employed to characterize and validate the axis:

*   **Epigenetic and Transcriptomic Profiling:**
    *   **MeRIP-seq (Methylated RNA Immunoprecipitation Sequencing):** Used to map transcriptome-wide m6A modifications and identify **FOS** as a hypermethylated target in FTO-deficient cells  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jiang_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 12|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jiang_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 16|[7]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jiang_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 18|[9]]].
    *   **mRNA-seq (RNA sequencing):** Conducted alongside MeRIP-seq to identify genes with both altered methylation and altered expression levels  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jiang_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 16|[7]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jiang_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 6|[10]]].
    *   **Colorimetric m6A Quantification:** Used for a global assessment of total RNA m6A levels in clinical samples and cell lines  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jiang_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 9|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jiang_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 15|[5]]].
    *   **m6A Dot Blot Assay:** Provided a visual confirmation of increased total m6A content following FTO knockdown  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jiang_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 9|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jiang_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 8|[11]]].

*   **Binding and Mechanism Validation:**
    *   **RIP-qPCR (RNA Immunoprecipitation):** Used to confirm that the **FTO protein** directly binds to **FOS mRNA** to perform its demethylase function  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jiang_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 19|[12]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jiang_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 20|[13]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jiang_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 11|[14]]].
    *   **Luciferase Reporter Assays and Site-Directed Mutagenesis:** Researchers constructed FOS-3'UTR reporter plasmids and mutated predicted m6A sites (substituting **adenosine with cytosine**) to prove that FOS expression is specifically controlled by m6A modifications at those sites  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jiang_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 21|[15]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jiang_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 22|[16]]].
    *   **RNA Stability Assay:** Cells were treated with **Actinomycin D** to inhibit new transcription, allowing the measurement of FOS mRNA half-life and turnover rate, which established that increased m6A levels (due to FTO loss) reinforced mRNA stability  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jiang_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 21|[15]]].

*   **Gene Manipulation and Expression Analysis:**
    *   **Lentiviral Transduction:** Used to create stable cell lines with **FTO knockdown (shFTO)** or **FTO overexpression**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jiang_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 9|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jiang_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 3|[6]]].
    *   **siRNA Interference:** Specifically used to **silence FOS** to determine if its loss could rescue the senescence phenotype caused by FTO deficiency  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jiang_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 3|[6]]].
    *   **RT-PCR and Western Blotting:** Standard techniques used to quantify RNA and protein levels of FTO and FOS across all experimental groups  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jiang_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 9|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jiang_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 18|[9]]].

*   **Senescence Phenotyping:**
    *   **SA-$\beta$-gal Staining:** Used to detect and quantify the proportion of senescent cells  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jiang_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 9|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jiang_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 16|[7]]].
    *   **Immunofluorescence (IF):** Employed to observe the localization and levels of aging markers, such as **$\gamma$H2A.X** (a marker for DNA damage and senescence), as well as FTO and FOS proteins  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jiang_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 9|[2]]].
