# Q03 - Beyond Jiang et al. (2021), what other sources in this corpus independently support, replicate, or extend evidence for a functional link between FTO, m6A methylation, and FOS (or related immediate-early genes) in granulosa or other reproductive cells?

Several sources in the corpus independently support, integrate, or extend the evidence for the functional role of the **FTO-m6A-FOS axis** (and related pathways) in granulosa cells and other reproductive contexts.

### Direct Support and Integration of the FTO-FOS Axis
Several review articles summarize and integrate the findings established by Jiang et al. (2021) into the broader understanding of female reproductive biology:
*   **Mechanistic Validation:** Reviews confirm that ovarian aging can be alleviated by upregulating **FTO** in granulosa cells (GCs), which reduces the m6A levels on **FOS mRNA 3′UTR**, thereby retarding FOS-dependent senescence  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/erqing_2023_rna_n6_methyladenosine_modification_in_female_reproductive_biology.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/sun_2022_the_role_of_m_sup_6_sup_a.pdf#Passage 1|[2]]].
*   **Significance in Ovarian Aging:** The FTO-FOS axis is cited as a key epigenetic pathway where FTO acts as a "senescence-retarding protein"  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/sun_2022_the_role_of_m_sup_6_sup_a.pdf#Passage 2|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jin_2022_the_potential_role_of_m6a_rna_methylation_in.pdf#Passage 1|[4]]]. It is formally listed as a recognized mechanism of cellular senescence in GCs  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jiang_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 4|[5]]].
*   **Relationship to Oxidative Stress:** Sources support the link between the accumulation of **reactive oxygen species (ROS)** and the downregulation of FTO, which triggers the subsequent m6A-dependent upregulation of FOS in aged ovaries  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jin_2022_the_potential_role_of_m6a_rna_methylation_in.pdf#Passage 1|[4]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/li_2024_fat_mass_and_obesity_associated_protein_regulates_granulosa.pdf#Passage 1|[6]]].

### Extensions to Other Regulatory Targets in Granulosa Cells
Recent original research (2024) extends the evidence for FTO-mediated m6A regulation in GCs by identifying additional downstream targets:
*   **The FTO-MMP2 Axis:** **Li et al. (2024)** established that FTO regulates **MMP2** (Matrix Metalloproteinase-2) in GCs through an **m6A-YTHDF2-dependent** pathway  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/li_2024_fat_mass_and_obesity_associated_protein_regulates_granulosa.pdf#Passage 2|[7]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/li_2024_fat_mass_and_obesity_associated_protein_regulates_granulosa.pdf#Passage 3|[8]]]. Similar to the FOS mechanism, a decline in FTO expression leads to increased m6A on MMP2 mRNA, facilitating its degradation and contributing to GC senescence  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/zhu_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 1|[9]]].
*   **Regulation of circBRCA1:** **Zhu et al. (2024)** discovered that FTO stabilizes **exosomal circBRCA1** in human GCs via m6A demethylation  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/zhu_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 2|[10]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/zhu_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 3|[11]]]. This suggests FTO's role in GCs extends beyond linear mRNA to include the regulation of circular RNAs that repair oxidative damage  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/chang_2022_transcriptome_wide_n6_methyladenine_methylation_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 1|[12]]].

### Broader Support for FTO Downregulation in Reproductive Aging
Multiple sources independently confirm the foundational observation that **FTO expression is significantly reduced** in aged ovaries and in clinical conditions like **Primary Ovarian Insufficiency (POI)**:
*   **Clinical Evidence:** Case-control studies and transcriptomic profiling confirm that GCs from women with decreased ovarian reserve or POI exhibit significantly **lower FTO mRNA and protein levels** and higher total m6A content  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/xinru_2024_the_role_of_epigenetics_in_women_s_reproductive.pdf#Passage 1|[13]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/xiulin_2021_the_role_of_m6a_modification_in_the_biological.pdf#Passage 1|[14]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/yuqian_2023_premature_ovarian_insufficiency_a_review_on_the_role.pdf#Passage 1|[15]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/erqing_2023_rna_n6_methyladenosine_modification_in_female_reproductive_biology.pdf#Passage 2|[16]]].
*   **Chemotherapy-Induced Senescence:** Evidence shows that alkylating agents (like cyclophosphamide) decrease FTO levels in GCs while increasing global m6A modifications, mirroring the phenotype seen in natural aging  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/xinru_2024_the_role_of_epigenetics_in_women_s_reproductive.pdf#Passage 2|[17]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/jiang_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 2|[18]]].

### Evidence in Other Reproductive and Aging Models
The functional link between FTO, m6A, and immediate-early genes like FOS is suggested to extend to other contexts:
*   **Skin Fibroblasts:** In 2009, mutations in the FTO gene were reported to cause growth retardation and **accelerate senescence in skin fibroblasts**, providing early non-reproductive support for FTO's anti-aging role  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q04-eje-fto-m6a-fos/Sources/sun_2022_the_role_of_m_sup_6_sup_a.pdf#Passage 1|[2]]].
*   **Breast Cancer Cells:** Jiang et al. (2021) notes that FTO knockdown also results in the **high expression of FOS** in breast cancer cell lines (MDA-MB-231 and MCF-7), suggesting the FTO-m6A-FOS mechanism may be conserved across different tissue types [19].

> Abstract In this study, we aimed to investigate the molecular mechanisms of RNA N6-methyladenosine (m6A) modification and how its associated proteins affect granulosa cell aging. A granulosa cell senescence model was constructed to detect the differences in total RNA m6A modification levels and the expression of related enzymes. Changes in downstream molecular expression and the effects on the cellular senescence phenotype were explored by repeatedly knocking down and overexpressing the key genes fat mass and obesity-associated protein (FTO), YT521-B homology domain family member 2 (YTHDF2), and matrix metalloproteinase-2 (MMP2). There was an increased total RNA m6A modification and decreased expression of the demethylase FTO and target gene MMP2 in senescent granulosa cells. FTO and MMP2 knockdown promoted granulosa cell senescence, whereas FTO and MMP2 overexpression retarded it. YTHDF2 and FTO can bind to the messenger RNA of MMP2. The extracellular signal-regulated kinase (ERK) pathway, which is downstream of MMP2, retarded the process of granulosa cell senescence through ERK activators. In granulosa cells, FTO can regulate the expression of MMP2 in an m6A-YTHDF2-dependent manner, influencing the activation status of the ERK pathway and contributing to the aging process of granulosa cells.
