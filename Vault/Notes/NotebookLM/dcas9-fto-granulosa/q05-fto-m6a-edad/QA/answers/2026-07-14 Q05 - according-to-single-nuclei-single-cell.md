# Q05 - According to the single-nuclei/single-cell multi-omics studies of human ovarian aging in this corpus, what molecular and genetic changes accompany ovarian aging, and how do they relate to FTO or m6A regulation?

Based on single-nuclei and single-cell multi-omics studies of human ovarian aging in this corpus, the process is characterized by accelerated cellular decline, shifts in signaling pathways, and a specific dysregulation of the **m6A epitranscriptome** driven by the loss of the demethylase **FTO**.

### Molecular and Genetic Changes in Ovarian Aging
Single-nuclei multi-omics analyses (utilizing **snRNA-seq** and **snATAC-seq**) have revealed several key changes that distinguish the aging ovary from other tissues:

*   **Accelerated Cellular Aging:** Human ovarian tissue exhibits robust age-related changes that are significantly stronger than those found in somatic control tissues, indicating that the ovary undergoes an accelerated aging process  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/chen_2025_molecular_and_genetic_insights_into_human_ovarian_aging.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/chen_2025_molecular_and_genetic_insights_into_human_ovarian_aging.pdf#Passage 2|[2]]].
*   **Signaling and Communication Shifts:** Aging is accompanied by altered signaling in **46 distinct pathways**, affecting cellular communication, cell identity, and signaling networks across all major ovarian cell types  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/chen_2025_molecular_and_genetic_insights_into_human_ovarian_aging.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/chen_2025_molecular_and_genetic_insights_into_human_ovarian_aging.pdf#Passage 3|[3]]].
*   **mTOR Signaling:** Multi-omics data highlight an ovary-specific role for **mTOR signaling** in the aging process  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/chen_2025_molecular_and_genetic_insights_into_human_ovarian_aging.pdf#Passage 4|[4]]].
*   **Genetic Risk Variants:** Research has linked age-related ovarian changes to genetic risk variants identified in **Age at Natural Menopause (ANM)** genome-wide association studies (GWAS)  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/chen_2025_molecular_and_genetic_insights_into_human_ovarian_aging.pdf#Passage 3|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/chen_2025_molecular_and_genetic_insights_into_human_ovarian_aging.pdf#Passage 4|[4]]]. Specifically, variants involved in **DNA repair pathways** are major contributors to the heterogeneity of menopausal age  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/honey_2025_editorial_genetics_and_epigenetics_in_ovarian_aging.pdf#Passage 1|[5]]].
*   **Cell-Specific Molecular Markers:** Molecules such as **CEBPD** have been identified as highly susceptible to age-related expression changes in ovarian tissue  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/chen_2025_molecular_and_genetic_insights_into_human_ovarian_aging.pdf#Passage 3|[3]]].

### Relation to FTO and m6A Regulation
Multi-omics studies focusing on the "epitranscriptome" (using techniques like **MeRIP-seq** and **mRNA-seq**) establish a direct link between these molecular shifts and m6A regulation:

*   **Global m6A Hypermethylation:** Total m6A levels in the RNA of granulosa cells (GCs) **increase significantly** with age  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 2|[6]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/xiaoyan_2022_the_role_of_m6a_on_female_reproduction_and.pdf#Passage 2|[7]]]. This global increase is also observed in patients with **premature ovarian insufficiency (POI)**, a form of pathological aging  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 9|[8]]].
*   **FTO Downregulation:** The increase in methylation is primarily driven by a **significant decrease** in the expression of the m6A "eraser" **FTO** (both mRNA and protein levels) in aged human ovaries  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 2|[6]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/xiaoyan_2022_the_role_of_m6a_on_female_reproduction_and.pdf#Passage 2|[7]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 1|[9]]]. 
*   **The FTO-FOS Axis:** A critical genetic change identified via combined MeRIP-seq and transcriptome sequencing is the **stabilization of FOS mRNA**. When FTO levels decline, m6A marks on the **FOS-mRNA-3′UTR** increase, which impairs the normal degradation of FOS transcripts  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 2|[6]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 8|[10]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 6|[11]]]. The resulting elevation of FOS protein accelerates GC senescence and ovarian aging  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 2|[6]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 5|[12]]].
*   **Oxidative Stress as a Driver:** The accumulation of **reactive oxygen species (ROS)** in the aging ovary is identified as the force that triggers the downregulation of FTO, thereby initiating the m6A modification disorder  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 5|[12]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 3|[13]]].
*   **circRNA Regulation:** FTO also regulates non-coding RNAs; for example, it stabilizes **exosomal circBRCA1** via m6A demethylation  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 5|[14]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 6|[15]]]. As FTO declines with age, circBRCA1 levels also drop, which exacerbates mitochondrial dysfunction and cellular damage in granulosa cells  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 5|[14]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 7|[16]]].
