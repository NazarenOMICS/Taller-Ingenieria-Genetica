# Q02 - According to these sources, how does the global level of m6A RNA methylation change in ovarian or granulosa cell tissue with age?

The global level of m6A RNA methylation in ovarian and granulosa cell tissue **increases significantly** with age  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/xiaoyan_2022_the_role_of_m6a_on_female_reproduction_and.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/yuqian_2023_premature_ovarian_insufficiency_a_review_on_the_role.pdf#Passage 1|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 2|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 1|[4]]]. 

This upward trend is documented across several contexts in the provided sources:

*   **Granulosa Cells (GCs):** Research identifies that the total quantity of m6A is notably higher in the RNA of granulosa cells from aged human ovaries compared to those from younger ovaries with a normal reserve  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 2|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 1|[4]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/xiaoyan_2022_the_role_of_m6a_on_female_reproduction_and.pdf#Passage 2|[5]]].
*   **Pathological Aging (POI):** This same increase is observed in cases of **premature ovarian insufficiency (POI)**, a form of pathological aging, where global m6A levels are significantly elevated in both patient serum and GCs relative to healthy controls  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/yuqian_2023_premature_ovarian_insufficiency_a_review_on_the_role.pdf#Passage 1|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/xiaolan_2024_m6a_demethylase_fto_stabilized_exosomal_circbrca1_alleviates_oxidative.pdf#Passage 2|[6]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 6|[7]]].
*   **The FTO Mechanism:** The increase in global methylation is primarily attributed to a **decrease in the expression of m6A demethylases (erasers)**, specifically **FTO** and sometimes ALKBH5  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/yuqian_2023_premature_ovarian_insufficiency_a_review_on_the_role.pdf#Passage 1|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 2|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/xiaoyan_2022_the_role_of_m6a_on_female_reproduction_and.pdf#Passage 2|[5]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 5|[8]]]. As these proteins decline, their ability to remove m6A marks is compromised, resulting in an abnormal accumulation of methylation  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/yuqian_2023_premature_ovarian_insufficiency_a_review_on_the_role.pdf#Passage 1|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 2|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 6|[7]]].
*   **Role of Oxidative Stress:** The accumulation of **reactive oxygen species (ROS)** in the aging ovary is identified as the driving force that downregulates FTO, thereby initiating the global m6A modification disorder  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 5|[8]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/zhongxin_2021_the_m6a_mrna_demethylase_fto_in_granulosa_cells.pdf#Passage 7|[9]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/Sources/xiaoyan_2022_the_role_of_m6a_on_female_reproduction_and.pdf#Passage 3|[10]]].
*   **Oocyte Exceptions:** While chronological aging leads to a global increase in the tissue, researchers have noted that m6A signaling may actually **decrease** during specific developmental transitions in oocytes—such as the progression from the germinal vesicle (GV) stage to the two-cell stage—due to the degradation of maternal RNAs, before increasing again during the blastocyst stage [11].

> Ovarian aging is characterized by the constant decreasing of the number and the quality of follicles (Figure 1). The number of primordial follicles peaked at nearly seven million at gestational week 20 but dropped to one million at birth. At the menopausal age of 50 years old, about 1,000 follicles remained in ovary (Jerome and Robert, 2017; Laisk et al., 2019). Moreover, the more erroneous rate of meiosis during oocyte maturation increased with aging (Greaney et al., 2018). The aneuploidy rate of oocytes was 20% at the age of 35 and up to 60% at the age of 45 (Kuliev et al., 2011; Franasiak et al., 2014), resulting in the increasing incidence of aneuploid embryos, accompanied rising miscarriage, birth defection rate, gestational and obstetric complications (Hassold and Hunt, 2001; Frederiksen et al., 2018; Laisk et al., 2019; Correa-de-Araujo and Yoon, 2021). m6A modification and its regulators were essential for ovarian development and its aberrant manifestation resulted in ovarian aging. Jiang et al. found that downregulated FTO and increased m6A in granulosa cells (GCs) were accompanied by ovarian aging. They also reported that FTO slowed down the degradation of FOS-mRNA to upregulate FOS expression in GCs, eventually resulted in GC-mediated ovarian aging (Jiang et al., 2021). In 2009, FTO mutation was reported to cause severe growth retardation and accelerate senescence in the skin fibroblasts (Boissel et al., 2009). Meanwhile, Min et al. identified inconsistent results of m6A level during the aging process, which may be related to the tissue differences between the ovary and peripheral blood mononuclear cells (Min et al., 2018).
