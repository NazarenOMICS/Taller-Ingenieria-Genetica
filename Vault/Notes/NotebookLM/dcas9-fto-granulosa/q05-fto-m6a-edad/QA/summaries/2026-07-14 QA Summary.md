## Q03
- Pregunta: What techniques do these sources describe for quantifying m6A methylation in a site-specific manner (e.g., MeRIP-seq, SELECT, miCLIP, m6A-seq), and what are their resolution and sensitivity trade-offs?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/QA/answers/2026-07-14 Q03 - what-techniques-do-these-sources-describe.md]]

The source material describes a wide range of techniques for quantifying m6A methylation, ranging from transcriptome-wide "peak" mapping to site-specific biochemical assays. These methods vary significantly in their **resolution** (ability to pinpoint exact modified bases) and **sensitivity** (ability to detect low-abundance modifications or quantitative differences).
## Q04
- Pregunta: What are the known limitations or technical artifacts in detecting m6A changes with current methods, according to these sources?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/QA/answers/2026-07-14 Q04 - what-known-limitations-or-technical-artifacts.md]]

The sources identify several critical limitations and technical artifacts associated with current m6A detection methods, particularly the widely used **MeRIP-seq (m6A-seq)**. These issues range from low reproducibility and statistical confounding to inherent antibody biases.
## Q05
- Pregunta: According to the single-nuclei/single-cell multi-omics studies of human ovarian aging in this corpus, what molecular and genetic changes accompany ovarian aging, and how do they relate to FTO or m6A regulation?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q05-fto-m6a-edad/QA/answers/2026-07-14 Q05 - according-to-single-nuclei-single-cell.md]]

Based on single-nuclei and single-cell multi-omics studies of human ovarian aging in this corpus, the process is characterized by accelerated cellular decline, shifts in signaling pathways, and a specific dysregulation of the **m6A epitranscriptome** driven by the loss of the demethylase **FTO**.
