# Q06 - What gaps remain in these sources regarding epigenomic editing (CRISPRa/CRISPRi) specifically applied to reproductive tissue or ovarian/granulosa rejuvenation, as opposed to other tissue types?

While the sources provide extensive detail on the use of CRISPRa/CRISPRi for rejuvenating the heart, liver, and pancreas, they reveal significant **knowledge and experimental gaps** regarding the application of these tools to reproductive tissues like the ovary, testis, or granulosa cells.

The following gaps remain in the current literature provided:

### 1. Absence of Interventional CRISPRa/CRISPRi Studies
The most significant gap is the **complete lack of reported studies** using CRISPR-based activation (CRISPRa) or interference (CRISPRi) to reverse aging phenotypes in reproductive organs. While the sources document CRISPRi-mediated telomere restoration in **cardiomyocytes**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/chatterjee_2024_telomerase_is_essential_for_cardiac_differentiation_and_sustained.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/chatterjee_2024_telomerase_is_essential_for_cardiac_differentiation_and_sustained.pdf#Passage 2|[2]]] and targeted hypomethylation for **pancreatic beta cell** expansion  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/tao_2018_rejuvenation_of_cells_by_epigenetic_editing.pdf#Passage 4|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/tao_2018_rejuvenation_of_cells_by_epigenetic_editing.pdf#Passage 3|[4]]], no similar interventional experiments are described for the ovary or testis.

### 2. Lack of Tissue-Specific Epigenetic Targets
The sources identify specific "senescence drivers" or "proliferation controllers" in other tissues that can be modulated via epigenetic editing. For example:
*   **KAT7** was identified via CRISPR screening as a driver of senescence in the **liver**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/shuaifei_2023_cellular_rejuvenation_molecular_mechanisms_and_potential_therapeutic_interventions.pdf#Passage 2|[5]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/shuaifei_2023_cellular_rejuvenation_molecular_mechanisms_and_potential_therapeutic_interventions.pdf#Passage 3|[6]]].
*   **CDKN1C/p57** was targeted to restore regenerative capacity in **pancreatic islets**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/tao_2018_rejuvenation_of_cells_by_epigenetic_editing.pdf#Passage 3|[4]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/tao_2018_rejuvenation_of_cells_by_epigenetic_editing.pdf#Passage 1|[7]]].
*   **TERT** was targeted to rejuvenate **cardiac cells**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/chatterjee_2024_telomerase_is_essential_for_cardiac_differentiation_and_sustained.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]].

In contrast, while the sources mention a **"Single-cell transcriptomic atlas of primate ovarian aging"** that identifies oxidative damage as a key factor  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/xudong_2021_inflammation_epigenetics_and_metabolism_converge_to_cell_senescence.pdf#Passage 6|[8]]], they do not identify any specific epigenetic loci or genes within reproductive tissues that have been successfully targeted for rejuvenation using CRISPRa/CRISPRi.

### 3. Focus on Descriptive Rather Than Interventional Research
The sources acknowledge that "reproductive system aging" is a "matter of great concern"  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/shuaifei_2023_cellular_rejuvenation_molecular_mechanisms_and_potential_therapeutic_interventions.pdf#Passage 4|[9]]]. However, the research cited for these tissues remains largely **descriptive or focused on non-genetic interventions**. For instance:
*   Instead of epigenomic editing, the sources highlight **long-term melatonin treatment** and **resveratrol** supplementation as methods to delay ovarian aging and inflammation  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/shuaifei_2023_cellular_rejuvenation_molecular_mechanisms_and_potential_therapeutic_interventions.pdf#Passage 5|[10]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/xudong_2021_inflammation_epigenetics_and_metabolism_converge_to_cell_senescence.pdf#Passage 3|[11]]].
*   Reproductive aging research is primarily represented by transcriptomic comparisons between young and old tissues rather than the active "rewriting" of the epigenome seen in other organ systems  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/xudong_2021_inflammation_epigenetics_and_metabolism_converge_to_cell_senescence.pdf#Passage 6|[8]]].

### 4. Divergent Use Cases for CRISPR in Reproduction
When CRISPR is mentioned in the context of reproduction, it is for purposes **unrelated to rejuvenation**. These include:
*   **Genic male sterility:** Using CRISPR-Cas9 to mutate genes like *MS26* to create sterile crops for seed production  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/malavika_2023_seed_science_and_technology.pdf#Passage 1|[12]]].
*   **Gene drives:** Developing systems to target female reproduction in mosquitoes for population control  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/mazhar_2018_the_crispr_tool_kit_for_genome_editing_and.pdf#Passage 1|[13]]].
*   **Disease modeling:** Using CRISPR to study hereditary eye or neurodegenerative diseases, but not to reset the reproductive biological clock  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/hongyi_2020_applications_of_genome_editing_technology_in_the_targeted.pdf#Passage 1|[14]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/magdalena_2018_precise_excision_of_the_cag_tract_from_the.pdf#Passage 1|[15]]].

### 5. Exclusion from Major Stem Cell Rejuvenation Reviews
In comprehensive reviews of mammalian tissue stem cell rejuvenation, the focus is almost exclusively on the **brain, muscle, and blood**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/anne_2022_ageing_and_rejuvenation_of_tissue_stem_cells_and.pdf#Passage 6|[16]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/anne_2022_ageing_and_rejuvenation_of_tissue_stem_cells_and.pdf#Passage 7|[17]]]. While the germline contains unique stem cells capable of giving rise to gametes, the sources do not explore how CRISPRa/CRISPRi might be used to restore the "quiescent reservoir" or differentiation potential of these specific cells as they do for MuSCs (muscle) or NSCs (brain)  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/anne_2022_ageing_and_rejuvenation_of_tissue_stem_cells_and.pdf#Passage 8|[18]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/anne_2022_ageing_and_rejuvenation_of_tissue_stem_cells_and.pdf#Passage 9|[19]]].

### Summary of the Gap
In summary, while the sources establish a clear mechanistic framework for using epigenetic editing to restore youthful function in other organs, **reproductive tissues remain an experimental frontier**. There is currently no evidence in these sources that CRISPRa or CRISPRi has been used to target ovarian or granulosa cell senescence, identify reproductive-specific epigenetic checkpoints, or demonstrate functional rejuvenation of gametogenesis *in vivo* or *in vitro*.

> Abstract Telomeres as the protective ends of linear chromosomes, are synthesized by the enzyme telomerase (TERT). Critically short telomeres essentially contribute to aging-related diseases and are associated with a broad spectrum of disorders known as telomeropathies. In cardiomyocytes, telomere length is strongly correlated with cardiomyopathies but it remains ambiguous whether short telomeres are the cause or the result of the disease. In this study, we employed an inducible CRISPRi human induced pluripotent stem cell (hiPSC) line to silence TERT expression enabling the generation of hiPSCs and hiPSC-derived cardiomyocytes with long and short telomeres. Reduced telomerase activity and shorter telomere lengths of hiPSCs induced global transcriptomic changes associated with cardiac developmental pathways. Consequently, the differentiation potential towards cardiomyocytes was strongly impaired and single cell RNA sequencing revealed a shift towards a more smooth muscle cell like identity in the cells with the shortest telomeres. Poor cardiomyocyte function and increased sensitivity to stress directly correlated with the extent of telomere shortening. Collectively our data demonstrates a TERT dependent cardiomyogenic differentiation defect, highlighting the CRISPRi TERT hiPSCs model as a powerful platform to study the mechanisms and consequences of short telomeres in the heart and also in the context of telomeropathies.
