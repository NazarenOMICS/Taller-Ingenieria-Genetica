# Q01 - According to these sources (e.g., rejuvenation of cells by epigenetic editing), what specific epigenetic editing approaches (CRISPRa, CRISPRi, or related) have been used to reverse aging phenotypes in cells or tissues, and what results were reported?

Specific epigenetic editing approaches using **CRISPRa** (activation), **CRISPRi** (interference), and related **dCas9 fusion proteins** have been employed to reverse aging hallmarks and rejuvenate cellular functions by modulating gene expression without altering the underlying DNA sequence  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/allen_2020_the_impact_of_crispr_cas9_on_age_related.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/huimin_2021_application_of_the_crispr_cas9_based_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 1|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/tao_2018_rejuvenation_of_cells_by_epigenetic_editing.pdf#Passage 1|[3]]].

### CRISPRi and Telomere Restoration
A primary approach involves using an inducible **CRISPRi** system to silence the **TERT** (telomerase reverse transcriptase) gene in human induced pluripotent stem cells (hiPSCs)  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/chatterjee_2024_telomerase_is_essential_for_cardiac_differentiation_and_sustained.pdf#Passage 1|[4]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/chatterjee_2024_telomerase_is_essential_for_cardiac_differentiation_and_sustained.pdf#Passage 2|[5]]]. 
*   **The Approach:** Researchers fused a deactivated Cas9 (dCas9) to a **KRAB** repression domain, targeting the TERT promoter to induce replicative telomere shortening, thereby creating an "aged" cellular model  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/chatterjee_2024_telomerase_is_essential_for_cardiac_differentiation_and_sustained.pdf#Passage 2|[5]]].
*   **Results:** Upon the reversal of this CRISPRi-mediated silencing (by withdrawing doxycycline), **telomerase activity was restored**, and telomeres re-extended  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/chatterjee_2024_telomerase_is_essential_for_cardiac_differentiation_and_sustained.pdf#Passage 3|[6]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/chatterjee_2024_telomerase_is_essential_for_cardiac_differentiation_and_sustained.pdf#Passage 4|[7]]]. This rejuvenation of telomere length in hiPSC-derived cardiomyocytes resulted in **improved contractile function**, normalized beat rates, and **reduced sensitivity to doxorubicin-induced stress**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/chatterjee_2024_telomerase_is_essential_for_cardiac_differentiation_and_sustained.pdf#Passage 5|[8]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/chatterjee_2024_telomerase_is_essential_for_cardiac_differentiation_and_sustained.pdf#Passage 6|[9]]].

### Epigenetic Editing for Cell Proliferation and Expansion
Sources highlight the use of CRISPR-based tools to modify the epigenetic state (e.g., methylation levels) of regulatory elements to restore regenerative capacity in aged or damaged tissues  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/tao_2018_rejuvenation_of_cells_by_epigenetic_editing.pdf#Passage 1|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/tao_2018_rejuvenation_of_cells_by_epigenetic_editing.pdf#Passage 2|[10]]].
*   **Beta Cell Expansion:** Epigenetic editing has been used to induce the expansion of mature insulin-secreting **beta cells** in pancreatic tissue, which typically decline with age  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/shuaifei_2023_cellular_rejuvenation_molecular_mechanisms_and_potential_therapeutic_interventions.pdf#Passage 1|[11]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/allen_2020_the_impact_of_crispr_cas9_on_age_related.pdf#Passage 2|[12]]]. Specifically, **targeted hypomethylation** at the imprinting control region 2 (ICR2) or the **CDKN1C/p57** locus promoted beta cell replication  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/tao_2018_rejuvenation_of_cells_by_epigenetic_editing.pdf#Passage 1|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/allen_2020_the_impact_of_crispr_cas9_on_age_related.pdf#Passage 2|[12]]]. 
*   **Result:** When these epigenetically edited islets were transplanted into diabetic mice, they **successfully reduced blood glucose levels**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/allen_2020_the_impact_of_crispr_cas9_on_age_related.pdf#Passage 2|[12]]].
*   **Tissue-Specific Reprogramming:** CRISPR/dCas9-effectors targeted the **ectodysplasin** promoter to induce the reprogramming of human bone marrow-derived mesenchymal stem cells into functional **sweat gland-like cells**, a potential strategy for skin rejuvenation and functional repair  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/allen_2020_the_impact_of_crispr_cas9_on_age_related.pdf#Passage 3|[13]]].

### CRISPRa in Age-Related Cancer Therapy
**CRISPRa** is utilized to reactivate silenced tumor suppressor genes that contribute to aging-associated malignancies  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/huimin_2021_application_of_the_crispr_cas9_based_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 2|[14]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/allen_2020_the_impact_of_crispr_cas9_on_age_related.pdf#Passage 4|[15]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/allen_2020_the_impact_of_crispr_cas9_on_age_related.pdf#Passage 5|[16]]].
*   **PTEN Activation:** Researchers used **dCas9-VPR** (a tripartite transactivation complex) to activate **PTEN** expression in cancer cells where it was expressed at low levels  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/allen_2020_the_impact_of_crispr_cas9_on_age_related.pdf#Passage 5|[16]]]. 
*   **Result:** This activation effectively **inhibited downstream oncogenic pathways** in melanoma and triple-negative breast cancer cell lines  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/allen_2020_the_impact_of_crispr_cas9_on_age_related.pdf#Passage 5|[16]]].
*   **DKK3 Activation:** CRISPR-Cas9 mediated activation of **DKK3** was shown to attenuate TGF-beta signaling, thereby **restricting the migration and proliferation** of prostate cancer cells  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/lei_2022_cellular_senescence_a_key_therapeutic_target_in_aging.pdf#Passage 1|[17]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/shuaifei_2023_cellular_rejuvenation_molecular_mechanisms_and_potential_therapeutic_interventions.pdf#Passage 2|[18]]].

### Targeted Screening for Senescence Drivers
Genome-wide **CRISPR/Cas9 screens** have identified specific drivers of cellular senescence that can be targeted for rejuvenation  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/xudong_2021_inflammation_epigenetics_and_metabolism_converge_to_cell_senescence.pdf#Passage 1|[19]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/shuaifei_2023_cellular_rejuvenation_molecular_mechanisms_and_potential_therapeutic_interventions.pdf#Passage 3|[20]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/allen_2020_the_impact_of_crispr_cas9_on_age_related.pdf#Passage 6|[21]]].
*   **KAT7 Target:** A screen identified the histone acetyltransferase **KAT7** as a key driver of senescence in human stem cells  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/xudong_2021_inflammation_epigenetics_and_metabolism_converge_to_cell_senescence.pdf#Passage 1|[19]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/shuaifei_2023_cellular_rejuvenation_molecular_mechanisms_and_potential_therapeutic_interventions.pdf#Passage 3|[20]]].
*   **Result:** Intravenous administration of lentiviral vectors containing Cas9/sg-KAT7 to target this gene resulted in **reduced liver aging**, improved physiological function, and **extended lifespan** in both progeroid and naturally aged mice  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/shuaifei_2023_cellular_rejuvenation_molecular_mechanisms_and_potential_therapeutic_interventions.pdf#Passage 3|[20]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/allen_2020_the_impact_of_crispr_cas9_on_age_related.pdf#Passage 7|[22]]]. 

### Related Mitochondrial Editing (mitoCas9)
To address the accumulation of age-related mutations in the mitochondrial genome, researchers developed **"mitoCas9"** [23, 24].
*   **The Approach:** This system uses customized sgRNAs to target specific mutations on the mitochondrial genome while leaving nuclear DNA intact [23].
*   **Expected Result:** This strategy aims to **revert or remove mutations** that impair oxidative phosphorylation and increase reactive oxygen species (ROS) production, potentially treating mitochondrial dysfunction-induced disorders like Parkinson’s disease [23, 24].

> Abstract Telomeres as the protective ends of linear chromosomes, are synthesized by the enzyme telomerase (TERT). Critically short telomeres essentially contribute to aging-related diseases and are associated with a broad spectrum of disorders known as telomeropathies. In cardiomyocytes, telomere length is strongly correlated with cardiomyopathies but it remains ambiguous whether short telomeres are the cause or the result of the disease. In this study, we employed an inducible CRISPRi human induced pluripotent stem cell (hiPSC) line to silence TERT expression enabling the generation of hiPSCs and hiPSC-derived cardiomyocytes with long and short telomeres. Reduced telomerase activity and shorter telomere lengths of hiPSCs induced global transcriptomic changes associated with cardiac developmental pathways. Consequently, the differentiation potential towards cardiomyocytes was strongly impaired and single cell RNA sequencing revealed a shift towards a more smooth muscle cell like identity in the cells with the shortest telomeres. Poor cardiomyocyte function and increased sensitivity to stress directly correlated with the extent of telomere shortening. Collectively our data demonstrates a TERT dependent cardiomyogenic differentiation defect, highlighting the CRISPRi TERT hiPSCs model as a powerful platform to study the mechanisms and consequences of short telomeres in the heart and also in the context of telomeropathies.
