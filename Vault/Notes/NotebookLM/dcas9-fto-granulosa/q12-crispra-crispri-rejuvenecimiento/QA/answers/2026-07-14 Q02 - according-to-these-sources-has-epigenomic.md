# Q02 - According to these sources, has epigenomic editing (CRISPRa/CRISPRi) been applied specifically to reproductive tissue rejuvenation (ovary, testis, granulosa), and if so, with what outcomes?

Based on the provided sources, **there is no reported application of epigenomic editing (CRISPRa/CRISPRi) specifically for the rejuvenation of reproductive tissues** such as the ovary, testis, or granulosa cells.

While the sources discuss reproductive tissue aging and the use of CRISPR-based tools for rejuvenation in other contexts, they do not link the two directly. Key findings related to these topics include:

*   **Recognition of Reproductive Aging:** The sources identify reproductive system aging as a "matter of great concern"  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/shuaifei_2023_cellular_rejuvenation_molecular_mechanisms_and_potential_therapeutic_interventions.pdf#Passage 4|[1]]]. Research using single-cell transcriptomics has been used to compare young and aged primate ovaries, identifying **oxidative damage** as a major determinant in ovarian aging  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/xudong_2021_inflammation_epigenetics_and_metabolism_converge_to_cell_senescence.pdf#Passage 2|[2]]].
*   **Alternative Interventions for Ovaries:** Instead of epigenomic editing, the sources mention that long-term **melatonin treatment** has been shown to delay ovarian aging in research models  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/shuaifei_2023_cellular_rejuvenation_molecular_mechanisms_and_potential_therapeutic_interventions.pdf#Passage 5|[3]]]. Additionally, **resveratrol** supplementation was noted for its ability to reduce ovarian inflammation by targeting NF-kB and mTOR signaling  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/xudong_2021_inflammation_epigenetics_and_metabolism_converge_to_cell_senescence.pdf#Passage 3|[4]]].
*   **CRISPR in Reproductive Contexts (Non-Rejuvenation):** CRISPR-Cas9 is mentioned in reproductive research for purposes other than rejuvenation, such as creating **genetic male sterility** in field crops for seed production  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/malavika_2023_seed_science_and_technology.pdf#Passage 1|[5]]] and developing **gene drive systems** to target female reproduction in mosquitoes for population control  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/mazhar_2018_the_crispr_tool_kit_for_genome_editing_and.pdf#Passage 1|[6]]].
*   **Epigenomic Editing in Other Tissues:** The sources extensively detail CRISPRa and CRISPRi for rejuvenating other cell types, such as restoring telomere length in **cardiomyocytes**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/chatterjee_2024_telomerase_is_essential_for_cardiac_differentiation_and_sustained.pdf#Passage 1|[7]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/chatterjee_2024_telomerase_is_essential_for_cardiac_differentiation_and_sustained.pdf#Passage 2|[8]]] and promoting the expansion of **pancreatic beta cells**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/tao_2018_rejuvenation_of_cells_by_epigenetic_editing.pdf#Passage 3|[9]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/tao_2018_rejuvenation_of_cells_by_epigenetic_editing.pdf#Passage 1|[10]]]. These methods are also used to identify drivers of senescence, like **KAT7**, which, when targeted, extended the lifespan of naturally aged mice  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/shuaifei_2023_cellular_rejuvenation_molecular_mechanisms_and_potential_therapeutic_interventions.pdf#Passage 2|[11]]].

In summary, while the sources establish the potential of CRISPRa/CRISPRi for cellular rejuvenation in various organs (heart, liver, pancreas), they do not contain specific results or applications for rejuvenating reproductive tissues.

> Abstract Telomeres as the protective ends of linear chromosomes, are synthesized by the enzyme telomerase (TERT). Critically short telomeres essentially contribute to aging-related diseases and are associated with a broad spectrum of disorders known as telomeropathies. In cardiomyocytes, telomere length is strongly correlated with cardiomyopathies but it remains ambiguous whether short telomeres are the cause or the result of the disease. In this study, we employed an inducible CRISPRi human induced pluripotent stem cell (hiPSC) line to silence TERT expression enabling the generation of hiPSCs and hiPSC-derived cardiomyocytes with long and short telomeres. Reduced telomerase activity and shorter telomere lengths of hiPSCs induced global transcriptomic changes associated with cardiac developmental pathways. Consequently, the differentiation potential towards cardiomyocytes was strongly impaired and single cell RNA sequencing revealed a shift towards a more smooth muscle cell like identity in the cells with the shortest telomeres. Poor cardiomyocyte function and increased sensitivity to stress directly correlated with the extent of telomere shortening. Collectively our data demonstrates a TERT dependent cardiomyogenic differentiation defect, highlighting the CRISPRi TERT hiPSCs model as a powerful platform to study the mechanisms and consequences of short telomeres in the heart and also in the context of telomeropathies.
