# Q05 - According to these sources, has any of this epigenetic-editing-based rejuvenation work been demonstrated in vivo (as opposed to only in cultured cells), and in which tissues?

Yes, several studies within the sources demonstrate that epigenetic-editing-based rejuvenation work has transitioned from cultured cells to **in vivo** animal models, targeting specific tissues to restore youthful function or extend lifespan. 

The following tissues have been the primary focus of these **in vivo** applications:

### **Liver**
*   **Targeted Senescence Driver:** Researchers identified the histone acetyltransferase **KAT7** as a key driver of cellular senescence  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/allen_2020_the_impact_of_crispr_cas9_on_age_related.pdf#Passage 8|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/shuaifei_2023_cellular_rejuvenation_molecular_mechanisms_and_potential_therapeutic_interventions.pdf#Passage 2|[2]]].
*   **Demonstration:** Intravenous administration of lentiviral vectors containing **Cas9/sg-KAT7** (to knock down this driver) resulted in **reduced liver aging**, improved physiological function, and **extended lifespan** in both progeroid and naturally aged mice  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/allen_2020_the_impact_of_crispr_cas9_on_age_related.pdf#Passage 8|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/shuaifei_2023_cellular_rejuvenation_molecular_mechanisms_and_potential_therapeutic_interventions.pdf#Passage 2|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/shuaifei_2023_cellular_rejuvenation_molecular_mechanisms_and_potential_therapeutic_interventions.pdf#Passage 3|[3]]].

### **Pancreas**
*   **Targeted Proliferation:** Epigenetic editing was used to induce the expansion of mature insulin-secreting **beta cells** by targeting the **CDKN1C/p57** locus  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/allen_2020_the_impact_of_crispr_cas9_on_age_related.pdf#Passage 9|[4]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/tao_2018_rejuvenation_of_cells_by_epigenetic_editing.pdf#Passage 4|[5]]].
*   **Demonstration:** Using a **TALE-TET1** fusion protein to induce targeted hypomethylation at the imprinting control region 2 (**ICR2**), researchers promoted beta cell replication  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/allen_2020_the_impact_of_crispr_cas9_on_age_related.pdf#Passage 9|[4]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/tao_2018_rejuvenation_of_cells_by_epigenetic_editing.pdf#Passage 4|[5]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/tao_2018_rejuvenation_of_cells_by_epigenetic_editing.pdf#Passage 3|[6]]]. When these edited human islets were transplanted into **diabetic mice**, they successfully restored insulin secretion and reduced blood glucose levels  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/allen_2020_the_impact_of_crispr_cas9_on_age_related.pdf#Passage 9|[4]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/tao_2018_rejuvenation_of_cells_by_epigenetic_editing.pdf#Passage 4|[5]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/tao_2018_rejuvenation_of_cells_by_epigenetic_editing.pdf#Passage 3|[6]]].

### **Brain and Eyes**
*   **Vision Restoration:** Epigenetic reprogramming has been applied to aged neurons in the visual system  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/allen_2020_the_impact_of_crispr_cas9_on_age_related.pdf#Passage 8|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/ding_2025_the_interplay_of_cellular_senescence_and_reprogramming_shapes.pdf#Passage 4|[7]]].
*   **Demonstration:** Introducing three reprogramming factors (**OSK**: Oct4, Sox2, and Klf4) via viral transduction into the **retinal ganglion cells** of old mice restored youthful epigenetic information, re-enabled axon growth, and **restored vision**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/allen_2020_the_impact_of_crispr_cas9_on_age_related.pdf#Passage 10|[8]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/anne_2022_ageing_and_rejuvenation_of_tissue_stem_cells_and.pdf#Passage 2|[9]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/anne_2022_ageing_and_rejuvenation_of_tissue_stem_cells_and.pdf#Passage 5|[10]]]  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/allen_2020_the_impact_of_crispr_cas9_on_age_related.pdf#Passage 8|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/ding_2025_the_interplay_of_cellular_senescence_and_reprogramming_shapes.pdf#Passage 4|[7]]].
*   **Hippocampal Modification:** Co-injection of AAV vectors into the **adult mouse hippocampus** was used to successfully disrupt a mutant APP allele associated with Alzheimer’s disease  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/allen_2020_the_impact_of_crispr_cas9_on_age_related.pdf#Passage 11|[11]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/allen_2020_the_impact_of_crispr_cas9_on_age_related.pdf#Passage 12|[12]]]. 

### **Heart and Cardiovascular System**
*   **Telomere Re-extension:** While much cardiac work is in the hiPSC-derived cardiomyocyte stage, systemic gene therapy targeting telomerase (TERT) has been performed in mice  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/chatterjee_2024_telomerase_is_essential_for_cardiac_differentiation_and_sustained.pdf#Passage 10|[13]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/chatterjee_2024_telomerase_is_essential_for_cardiac_differentiation_and_sustained.pdf#Passage 11|[14]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/shuaifei_2023_cellular_rejuvenation_molecular_mechanisms_and_potential_therapeutic_interventions.pdf#Passage 15|[15]]].
*   **Demonstration:** Systemic **AAV9-TERT** therapy extended the lifespan of adult and aged mice and provided cardioprotection in a myocardial infarction mouse model  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/chatterjee_2024_telomerase_is_essential_for_cardiac_differentiation_and_sustained.pdf#Passage 10|[13]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/chatterjee_2024_telomerase_is_essential_for_cardiac_differentiation_and_sustained.pdf#Passage 11|[14]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/shuaifei_2023_cellular_rejuvenation_molecular_mechanisms_and_potential_therapeutic_interventions.pdf#Passage 15|[15]]]  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/allen_2020_the_impact_of_crispr_cas9_on_age_related.pdf#Passage 8|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/shuaifei_2023_cellular_rejuvenation_molecular_mechanisms_and_potential_therapeutic_interventions.pdf#Passage 1|[16]]]. 

### **Other Tissues**
*   **Skin/Sweat Glands:** CRISPR/dCas9-effectors were used to target the **ectodysplasin** promoter, inducing the reprogramming of mesenchymal stem cells into functional **sweat gland-like cells** to aid in the repair of severely burned skin  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/allen_2020_the_impact_of_crispr_cas9_on_age_related.pdf#Passage 8|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/shuaifei_2023_cellular_rejuvenation_molecular_mechanisms_and_potential_therapeutic_interventions.pdf#Passage 14|[17]]].
*   **Intervertebral Discs:** In vivo reprogramming with cyclic expression of OSKM was reported to inhibit the progression of **intervertebral disc degeneration** and improve the senescent phenotype of nucleus pulposus cells in mice  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/allen_2020_the_impact_of_crispr_cas9_on_age_related.pdf#Passage 8|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/shuaifei_2023_cellular_rejuvenation_molecular_mechanisms_and_potential_therapeutic_interventions.pdf#Passage 16|[18]]].
*   **Multisystemic Longevity:** A combination gene therapy delivering **FGF21, sTGF-βR2, and αKlotho** into obese and diabetic mice was shown to simultaneously treat **renal fibrosis**, weight gain, and **heart failure**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/allen_2020_the_impact_of_crispr_cas9_on_age_related.pdf#Passage 8|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/shuaifei_2023_cellular_rejuvenation_molecular_mechanisms_and_potential_therapeutic_interventions.pdf#Passage 1|[16]]].
