## Q01
- Pregunta: According to these sources (e.g., rejuvenation of cells by epigenetic editing), what specific epigenetic editing approaches (CRISPRa, CRISPRi, or related) have been used to reverse aging phenotypes in cells or tissues, and what results were reported?

Specific epigenetic editing approaches using **CRISPRa** (activation), **CRISPRi** (interference), and related **dCas9 fusion proteins** have been employed to reverse aging hallmarks and rejuvenate cellular functions by modulating gene expression without altering the underlying DNA sequence  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/allen_2020_the_impact_of_crispr_cas9_on_age_related.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/huimin_2021_application_of_the_crispr_cas9_based_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 1|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/tao_2018_rejuvenation_of_cells_by_epigenetic_editing.pdf#Passage 1|[3]]].

Based on the provided sources, **there is no reported application of epigenomic editing (CRISPRa/CRISPRi) specifically for the rejuvenation of reproductive tissues** such as the ovary, testis, or granulosa cells.
## Q03
- Pregunta: According to these sources, how does epigenetic-editing-based rejuvenation compare mechanistically to cellular reprogramming approaches (e.g., partial Yamanaka factor reprogramming) for reversing aging phenotypes?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/QA/answers/2026-07-14 Q03 - according-to-these-sources-how-does.md]]

Mechanistically, epigenetic-editing-based rejuvenation and cellular reprogramming approaches represent two different philosophies for reversing aging: **targeted modulation** of specific aging drivers versus a **global reset** of the cellular state  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/daniel_2021_cellular_reprogramming_and_epigenetic_rejuvenation.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/daniel_2021_cellular_reprogramming_and_epigenetic_rejuvenation.pdf#Passage 2|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/tao_2018_rejuvenation_of_cells_by_epigenetic_editing.pdf#Passage 1|[3]]].
## Q04
- Pregunta: According to these sources, what cellular senescence or aging markers were used to demonstrate rejuvenation success in these epigenetic editing studies, and how much reversal (partial vs complete) was typically achieved?

In epigenetic editing studies, rejuvenation success is demonstrated through a combination of **molecular biomarkers, functional readouts, and systemic phenotypic improvements**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/chatterjee_2024_telomerase_is_essential_for_cardiac_differentiation_and_sustained.pdf#Passage 3|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/shuaifei_2023_cellular_rejuvenation_molecular_mechanisms_and_potential_therapeutic_interventions.pdf#Passage 7|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/shuaifei_2023_cellular_rejuvenation_molecular_mechanisms_and_potential_therapeutic_interventions.pdf#Passage 8|[3]]]. While global cellular reprogramming often aims for a "complete reset" of biological age to zero, targeted epigenetic editing typically achieves **significant functional restoration** or "re-extension" of specific youthful traits without stripping cellular identity  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/chatterjee_2024_telomerase_is_essential_for_cardiac_differentiation_and_sustained.p...
