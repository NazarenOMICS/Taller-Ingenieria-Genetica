- Based on the provided sources, **there is no reported application of epigenomic editing (CRISPRa/CRISPRi) specifically for the rejuvenation of reproductive tissues** such as the ovary, testis, or granulosa cells.
- Mechanistically, epigenetic-editing-based rejuvenation and cellular reprogramming approaches represent two different philosophies for reversing aging: **targeted modulation** of specific aging drivers versus a **global reset** of the cellular state  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/daniel_2021_cellular_reprogramming_and_epigenetic_rejuvenation.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/daniel_2021_cellular_reprogramming_and_epigenetic_rejuvenation.pdf#Passage 2|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/tao_2018_rejuvenation_of_cells_b...
- In epigenetic editing studies, rejuvenation success is demonstrated through a combination of **molecular biomarkers, functional readouts, and systemic phenotypic improvements**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/chatterjee_2024_telomerase_is_essential_for_cardiac_differentiation_and_sustained.pdf#Passage 3|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/shuaifei_2023_cellular_rejuvenation_molecular_mechanisms_and_potential_therapeutic_interventions.pdf#Passage 7|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q12-crispra-crispri-rejuvenecimiento/Sources/shuaifei_2023_cellular_rejuvenation_molecular_mecha...
- Yes, several studies within the sources demonstrate that epigenetic-editing-based rejuvenation work has transitioned from cultured cells to **in vivo** animal models, targeting specific tissues to restore youthful function or extend lifespan.
- While the sources provide extensive detail on the use of CRISPRa/CRISPRi for rejuvenating the heart, liver, and pancreas, they reveal significant **knowledge and experimental gaps** regarding the application of these tools to reproductive tissues like the ovary, testis, or granulosa cells.
