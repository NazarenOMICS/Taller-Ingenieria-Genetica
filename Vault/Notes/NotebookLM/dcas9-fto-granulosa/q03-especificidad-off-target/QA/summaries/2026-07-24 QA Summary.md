## Resumen por QA

## Q01
- Pregunta: According to these sources, what is known specifically about dCas9-p300 (nuclease-dead CRISPRa fusion) off-target binding or off-target transcriptional activation in human cells, as distinct from wild-type Cas9 nuclease off-target cutting?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/QA/answers/2026-07-24 Q01 - according-to-these-sources-what-known.md]]

Based on the provided sources, **dCas9-p300** (a fusion of nuclease-dead Cas9 and the catalytic core of the human p300 acetyltransferase) exhibits a distinct specificity profile compared to wild-type (WT) Cas9, characterized by its focus on **epigenetic modification rather than permanent sequence alteration.**
## Q02
- Pregunta: What guide RNA mismatch tolerance or sequence features have been reported in these sources that predispose dCas9-based systems to bind or activate unintended genomic loci?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/QA/answers/2026-07-24 Q02 - what-guide-rna-mismatch-tolerance-or.md]]

Based on the provided sources, dCas9-based systems are predisposed to unintended binding or activation due to specific guide RNA (sgRNA) mismatch tolerances, positional sensitivities along the guide sequence, and broader genomic or structural features.
## Q03
- Pregunta: What genome-wide profiling methods (e.g., ChIP-seq, CUT&RUN, GUIDE-seq, RNA-seq) do these sources describe for assessing dCas9 or CRISPRa binding specificity and global transcriptomic off-target effects?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/QA/answers/2026-07-24 Q03 - what-genome-wide-profiling-methods-e.md]]

The sources describe several genome-wide profiling methods for assessing the **DNA binding specificity** of dCas9 and the **functional transcriptomic consequences** of CRISPRa systems. These methods differentiate between where a dCas9 complex physically associates with the genome and where it successfully induces changes in gene expression or the epigenome.
## Q04
- Pregunta: How does the specificity profile of dCas9 fusion-based CRISPR activators compare to the off-target profile of catalytically active Cas9 nuclease editing, according to these sources?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/QA/answers/2026-07-24 Q04 - how-does-specificity-profile-dcas9-fusion.md]]

The specificity profile of **dCas9 fusion-based CRISPR activators (CRISPRa)** differs from **catalytically active Cas9 nuclease editing** primarily in the nature of the genomic modification, the resulting cellular toxicity, and the stringency of the requirements for functional activity versus physical binding.
## Q05
- Pregunta: What gaps or limitations remain in these sources regarding direct experimental evidence of dCas9-p300-specific off-target binding or off-target transcriptomic changes in human cells (as opposed to general CRISPR-Cas9 off-target literature)?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/QA/answers/2026-07-24 Q05 - what-gaps-or-limitations-remain-these.md]]

While the sources characterize **dCas9-p300** as a potent epigenetic editor, they reveal several significant gaps and limitations regarding direct experimental evidence of its specificity in human cells, particularly when compared to the broader body of literature on general CRISPR-Cas9 off-targets.
