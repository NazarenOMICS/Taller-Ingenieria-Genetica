## Q01
- Pregunta: According to these sources, what is known specifically about dCas9-p300 (nuclease-dead CRISPRa fusion) off-target binding or off-target transcriptional activation in human cells, as distinct from wild-type Cas9 nuclease off-target cutting?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/QA/answers/2026-07-14 Q01 - according-to-these-sources-what-known.md]]

Specifically for **dCas9-p300** (a nuclease-dead Cas9 fused to the histone acetyltransferase core domain of p300), the sources highlight several key insights regarding its off-target binding and transcriptional activation in human cells, distinguishing it from wild-type (WT) Cas9 nuclease cutting.
## Q02
- Pregunta: What guide RNA mismatch tolerance or sequence features have been reported in these sources that predispose dCas9-based systems to bind or activate unintended genomic loci?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/QA/answers/2026-07-14 Q02 - what-guide-rna-mismatch-tolerance-or.md]]

Based on the provided sources, dCas9-based systems are predisposed to bind or activate unintended genomic loci due to specific guide RNA (sgRNA) mismatch tolerances, sequence-specific regional sensitivities, and broader genomic features like chromatin accessibility.
## Q03
- Pregunta: What genome-wide profiling methods (e.g., ChIP-seq, CUT&RUN, GUIDE-seq, RNA-seq) do these sources describe for assessing dCas9 or CRISPRa binding specificity and global transcriptomic off-target effects?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/QA/answers/2026-07-14 Q03 - what-genome-wide-profiling-methods-e.md]]

The sources describe several genome-wide profiling methods for assessing the binding specificity and functional transcriptomic consequences of dCas9 and CRISPRa systems. These methods are categorized into those that detect physical binding, those that measure transcriptomic changes, and those that profile off-target epigenetic modifications.
## Q04
- Pregunta: How does the specificity profile of dCas9 fusion-based CRISPR activators compare to the off-target profile of catalytically active Cas9 nuclease editing, according to these sources?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/QA/answers/2026-07-14 Q04 - how-does-specificity-profile-dcas9-fusion.md]]

According to the sources, the specificity profile of **dCas9 fusion-based CRISPR activators (CRISPRa)** differs significantly from **catalytically active Cas9 nuclease editing** in terms of the nature of the genomic modification, the functional consequences of off-target activity, and the specific toxicity risks.
## Q05
- Pregunta: What gaps or limitations remain in these sources regarding direct experimental evidence of dCas9-p300-specific off-target binding or off-target transcriptomic changes in human cells (as opposed to general CRISPR-Cas9 off-target literature)?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/QA/answers/2026-07-14 Q05 - what-gaps-or-limitations-remain-these.md]]

While the sources describe **dCas9-p300** as a potent and potentially specific tool for epigenetic editing, they identify several significant gaps and limitations regarding direct experimental evidence of its specificity in human cells.
