# Q03 - What genome-wide profiling methods (e.g., ChIP-seq, CUT&RUN, GUIDE-seq, RNA-seq) do these sources describe for assessing dCas9 or CRISPRa binding specificity and global transcriptomic off-target effects?

The sources describe several genome-wide profiling methods for assessing the **DNA binding specificity** of dCas9 and the **functional transcriptomic consequences** of CRISPRa systems. These methods differentiate between where a dCas9 complex physically associates with the genome and where it successfully induces changes in gene expression or the epigenome.

### **Genome-Wide Binding Specificity**
*   **ChIP-seq (Chromatin Immunoprecipitation sequencing):** This is the primary method identified for mapping the **physical DNA binding sites** of catalytically inactive **dCas9** in vivo  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/congting_2023_off_target_effects_in_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 2|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/mazhar_2018_the_crispr_tool_kit_for_genome_editing_and.pdf#Passage 1|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/congting_2023_off_target_effects_in_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 3|[3]]]. In human HEK293T cells, dCas9 ChIP-seq has been used to determine that the complex can associate with many off-target sites that do not necessarily result in functional gene activation  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/congting_2023_off_target_effects_in_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 3|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/ashot_2019_site_specific_manipulation_of_arabidopsis_loci_using_crispr.pdf#Passage 1|[4]]]. In plant models, ChIP-seq confirmed the high specificity of dCas9-SunTag binding to its intended promoter  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/ashot_2019_site_specific_manipulation_of_arabidopsis_loci_using_crispr.pdf#Passage 2|[5]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/yu_2015_making_sense_of_gwas_using_epigenomics_and_genome.pdf#Passage 2|[6]]].
*   **DNase-seq and ATAC-seq:** These methods identify regions of **open chromatin** (chromatin accessibility)  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/yu_2015_making_sense_of_gwas_using_epigenomics_and_genome.pdf#Passage 3|[7]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/mazhar_2018_the_crispr_tool_kit_for_genome_editing_and.pdf#Passage 4|[8]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/ashot_2019_site_specific_manipulation_of_arabidopsis_loci_using_crispr.pdf#Passage 3|[9]]]. The sources emphasize that because dCas9 off-target binding is heavily **enriched in open chromatin**, integrating data from DNase-seq or ATAC-seq into prediction algorithms significantly improves the identification of unintended binding sites  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/congting_2023_off_target_effects_in_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 3|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/hilton_2015.pdf#Passage 1|[10]]].

### **Functional Transcriptomic and Epigenomic Specificity**
*   **RNA-seq (RNA sequencing):** This method is described as the standard for profiling the **functional specificity** of CRISPRa by identifying **differentially expressed genes (DEGs)**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/ashot_2019_site_specific_manipulation_of_arabidopsis_loci_using_crispr.pdf#Passage 2|[5]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/ashot_2019_site_specific_manipulation_of_arabidopsis_loci_using_crispr.pdf#Passage 4|[11]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/ashot_2019_site_specific_manipulation_of_arabidopsis_loci_using_crispr.pdf#Passage 5|[12]]]. RNA-seq analysis of **dCas9-p300** targeting the *IL1RN* promoter in human cells demonstrated "limited" off-target effects, with only two unintended transcripts significantly induced above background  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/ashot_2019_site_specific_manipulation_of_arabidopsis_loci_using_crispr.pdf#Passage 5|[12]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/ashot_2019_site_specific_manipulation_of_arabidopsis_loci_using_crispr.pdf#Passage 6|[13]]].
*   **WGBS (Whole-Genome Bisulfite Sequencing):** Used to monitor the **epigenomic specificity** of dCas9-fused modifiers, such as DNA methyltransferases or demethylases  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/mazhar_2018_the_crispr_tool_kit_for_genome_editing_and.pdf#Passage 2|[14]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/congting_2023_off_target_effects_in_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 4|[15]]]. WGBS can detect if these editors leave a **"global epigenetic footprint"** or background methylation changes independent of the guide RNA  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/hilton_2015.pdf#Passage 6|[16]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/hilton_2015.pdf#Passage 4|[17]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/congting_2023_off_target_effects_in_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 5|[18]]].
*   **ChIP-seq for Histone Marks:** Beyond mapping dCas9 binding, ChIP-seq is also used to evaluate unintended changes in the **epigenomic landscape** induced by activators, such as assessing local and distal changes in **H3K27ac** levels following dCas9-p300 treatment  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/congting_2023_off_target_effects_in_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 6|[19]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/congting_2023_off_target_effects_in_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 7|[20]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/congting_2023_off_target_effects_in_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 8|[21]]].

### **Whole Genome Sequencing (WGS)**
*   **WGS** is used to sequence the entire genome before and after treatment to uncover both desired and unintended editing events  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/congting_2023_off_target_effects_in_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 2|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/ashot_2019_site_specific_manipulation_of_arabidopsis_loci_using_crispr.pdf#Passage 1|[4]]]. While comprehensive, the sources note that WGS can be expensive and its ability to detect low-frequency events is limited by sequencing depth  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/congting_2023_off_target_effects_in_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 3|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/ashot_2019_site_specific_manipulation_of_arabidopsis_loci_using_crispr.pdf#Passage 1|[4]]].

### **Methods for Cleavage-Based Specificity (Cas9 Nuclease)**
While your query focuses on dCas9 and CRISPRa, the sources detail several methods specifically designed to detect **double-strand breaks (DSBs)** induced by active Cas9 nucleases. These methods identify the genomic "scars" that dCas9-p300 is designed to avoid:
*   **GUIDE-seq:** Captures DSBs in cells via the integration of double-stranded oligonucleotides  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/congting_2023_off_target_effects_in_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 3|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/congting_2023_off_target_effects_in_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 9|[22]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/congting_2023_off_target_effects_in_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 10|[23]]].
*   **Digenome-seq / DIG-seq:** Cell-free methods that utilize purified DNA or chromatin to identify Cas9-mediated cleavages  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/congting_2023_off_target_effects_in_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 11|[24]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/congting_2023_off_target_effects_in_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 12|[25]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/congting_2023_off_target_effects_in_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 13|[26]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/congting_2023_off_target_effects_in_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 14|[27]]].
*   **BLESS / BLISS:** Label DNA breaks **in situ** within fixed cells to map off-target activity  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/congting_2023_off_target_effects_in_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 3|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/congting_2023_off_target_effects_in_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 15|[28]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/congting_2023_off_target_effects_in_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 16|[29]]].
*   **Discover-seq:** Leverages the endogenous repair protein **MRE11** to identify Cas off-target sites via ChIP-seq  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/congting_2023_off_target_effects_in_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 15|[28]]].

**Note on CUT&RUN:** While you mentioned **CUT&RUN** as an example, the provided sources **do not explicitly describe** this method for assessing dCas9 or CRISPRa specificity; they rely almost exclusively on ChIP-seq and RNA-seq for these purposes.

> Another more popular method to detect off-target sites in cells is called GUIDE-seq (Tsai et al., 2015). This technique relies on the delivery of double-stranded oligonucleotides (dsODNs) with known sequences, which can integrate into DSBs during NHEJ (nonhomologous end joining). The integrated dsODNs provide templates for targeted PCR amplification and sequencing of the tagged DNA fragments (Tsai et al., 2015; Malinin et al., 2021; Yaish et al., 2022) (Figure 1B). GUIDE-seq can detect off-target sites with indel frequencies as low as 0.03% (Tsai et al., 2015). GUIDE-seq is more sensitive than the IDLV method because dsODNs integrate more efficiently and precisely into DSBs, while the integration events of IDLV is low in number and can distribute as far as 500bp away from the actual DSB sites (Tsai et al., 2015; Cromer et al., 2023). A primary limitation of GUIDE-seq is relevant to the low delivery efficiency of dsODNs into cells, which results in detection of only 30%–50% of all the DSBs (Tsai et al., 2015; Pan et al., 2022).

> Digenome-seq is a highly sensitive method that can identify indels with 0.1% frequency or lower (Kim et al., 2015). > To enhance the sensitivity of BLESS and reduce its requirement on cell number, BLISS (breaks labeling in situ and sequencing) technology was developed (Yan et al., 2017). BLISS ligates DSB ends with adapters containing the T7 promoter, so the tagged DNA fragments can be linearly amplified via T7-mediated transcription before sequencing (Yan et al., 2017; Ballarino et al., 2021). Compared to BLESS, BLISS demands only a few thousand cells and demonstrates a higher sensitivity. For example, Winston et al. performed side-by-side comparison between BLISS and BLESS to detect the off-target sites of validated sgRNAs targeting EMX1 or VEGFA (Yan et al., 2017). For the sgRNA targeting EMX1, BLESS uncovered 6 off-target sites, all of which are included in the 10 genuine off-target sites that BLISS discovered. Similarly, for the sgRNA targeting VEGFA, besides the 16 off-target sites that were detected by both methods, BLISS identified 27 additional offtarget sites that are not found by BLESS (Yan et al., 2017). Thus the sensitivity of BLISS is more than two folds higher than BLESS.
