# Q04 - How does the specificity profile of dCas9 fusion-based CRISPR activators compare to the off-target profile of catalytically active Cas9 nuclease editing, according to these sources?

According to the sources, the specificity profile of **dCas9 fusion-based CRISPR activators (CRISPRa)** differs significantly from **catalytically active Cas9 nuclease editing** in terms of the nature of the genomic modification, the functional consequences of off-target activity, and the specific toxicity risks.

### **Permanent vs. Reversible Modifications**
*   **Active Cas9:** Catalytically active Cas9 creates **permanent, irreversible modifications** to the genome by inducing double-strand breaks (DSBs) that are repaired via error-prone mechanisms like non-homologous end joining (NHEJ)  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/hongyi_2020_applications_of_genome_editing_technology_in_the_targeted.pdf#Passage 4|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/hongyi_2020_applications_of_genome_editing_technology_in_the_targeted.pdf#Passage 5|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/tianxiang_2023_crispr_cas9_therapeutics_progress_and_prospects.pdf#Passage 6|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/tianxiang_2023_crispr_cas9_therapeutics_progress_and_prospects.pdf#Passage 7|[4]]]. This can result in unintended mutations (indels) or chromosomal translocations at off-target sites  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/tianxiang_2023_crispr_cas9_therapeutics_progress_and_prospects.pdf#Passage 8|[5]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/tianxiang_2023_crispr_cas9_therapeutics_progress_and_prospects.pdf#Passage 9|[6]]].
*   **dCas9 Activators:** CRISPRa tools utilize nuclease-dead Cas9 (dCas9), which binds to DNA without cutting it  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/fatemeh_2019_crispr_cpf1_proteins_structure_function_and_implications_for.pdf#Passage 4|[7]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/mazhar_2018_the_crispr_tool_kit_for_genome_editing_and.pdf#Passage 5|[8]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/tianxiang_2023_crispr_cas9_therapeutics_progress_and_prospects.pdf#Passage 1|[9]]]. Consequently, their effects are **reversible and transient**, altering gene expression without introducing permanent changes to the underlying DNA sequence  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/tianxiang_2023_crispr_cas9_therapeutics_progress_and_prospects.pdf#Passage 1|[9]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/tianxiang_2023_crispr_cas9_therapeutics_progress_and_prospects.pdf#Passage 2|[10]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/kendall_2018_optimized_libraries_for_crispr_cas9_genetic_screens_with.pdf#Passage 5|[11]]].

### **Cutting-Related Toxicity and Copy Number Variation**
*   **Active Cas9:** A distinct risk of nuclease-active Cas9 is **"cutting-related toxicity,"** which is particularly pronounced at copy-number amplified genomic loci  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/kendall_2018_optimized_libraries_for_crispr_cas9_genetic_screens_with.pdf#Passage 1|[12]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/kendall_2018_optimized_libraries_for_crispr_cas9_genetic_screens_with.pdf#Passage 2|[13]]]. Multiple DSBs at these sites can lead to significant cell growth defects or death  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/kendall_2018_optimized_libraries_for_crispr_cas9_genetic_screens_with.pdf#Passage 2|[13]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/congting_2023_off_target_effects_in_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 1|[14]]].
*   **dCas9 Activators:** CRISPRa (and CRISPRi) systems **mitigate cutting-related toxicity** because they do not introduce DSBs  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/kendall_2018_optimized_libraries_for_crispr_cas9_genetic_screens_with.pdf#Passage 2|[13]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/congting_2023_off_target_effects_in_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 1|[14]]]. In cell viability screens, dCas9-based systems show little to no difference in growth between non-targeting guides and those targeting non-essential genes, whereas active Cas9 shows a clear "cutting effect"  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/kendall_2018_optimized_libraries_for_crispr_cas9_genetic_screens_with.pdf#Passage 1|[12]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/kendall_2018_optimized_libraries_for_crispr_cas9_genetic_screens_with.pdf#Passage 2|[13]]].

### **Binding Specificity vs. Functional Specificity**
*   **Stringency of Requirements:** The sources note that the requirements for dCas9-DNA binding are **less stringent** than for wild-type Cas9 nuclease cleavage  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/ashot_2019_site_specific_manipulation_of_arabidopsis_loci_using_crispr.pdf#Passage 1|[15]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/congting_2023_off_target_effects_in_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 13|[16]]]. While dCas9 can physically associate with numerous off-target sites—often enriched in **open chromatin regions**—only a limited number of these binding events result in functional consequences like transcriptional activation  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/ashot_2019_site_specific_manipulation_of_arabidopsis_loci_using_crispr.pdf#Passage 1|[15]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/congting_2023_off_target_effects_in_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 13|[16]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/ashot_2019_site_specific_manipulation_of_arabidopsis_loci_using_crispr.pdf#Passage 3|[17]]].
*   **High Functional Specificity:** Despite potential off-target binding, dCas9 activators (such as the SunTag-VP64 system) have been reported as **"remarkably specific"** in their functional outcomes  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/ashot_2019_site_specific_manipulation_of_arabidopsis_loci_using_crispr.pdf#Passage 3|[17]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/congting_2023_off_target_effects_in_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 2|[18]]]. Genome-wide transcriptomic profiling (RNA-seq) typically reveals very few differentially expressed genes (DEGs) outside of the intended target  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/ashot_2019_site_specific_manipulation_of_arabidopsis_loci_using_crispr.pdf#Passage 3|[17]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/fatemeh_2019_crispr_cpf1_proteins_structure_function_and_implications_for.pdf#Passage 1|[19]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/tianxiang_2023_crispr_cas9_therapeutics_progress_and_prospects.pdf#Passage 4|[20]]].

### **Mismatch Tolerance and Regional Constraints**
*   **Sequence Tolerance:** Wild-type Cas9 can tolerate up to **3 to 5 mismatches** between the sgRNA and the genomic DNA  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/kendall_2018_optimized_libraries_for_crispr_cas9_genetic_screens_with.pdf#Passage 7|[21]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/mazhar_2018_the_crispr_tool_kit_for_genome_editing_and.pdf#Passage 2|[22]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/congting_2023_off_target_effects_in_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 5|[23]]]. For both systems, mismatches in the **PAM-proximal "seed region"** (the first 10–12 base pairs) are poorly tolerated and generally abolish activity  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/ashot_2019_site_specific_manipulation_of_arabidopsis_loci_using_crispr.pdf#Passage 3|[17]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/mazhar_2018_the_crispr_tool_kit_for_genome_editing_and.pdf#Passage 2|[22]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/congting_2023_off_target_effects_in_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 5|[23]]].
*   **Positional Constraint:** CRISPRa activity is highly constrained to a specific window near the **transcription start site (TSS)** (typically 150 to 75 nucleotides upstream), which adds a layer of spatial specificity that is not required for nuclease-based gene disruption [24, 25].

### **Potential for Epigenomic Off-Targets**
*   While avoiding permanent mutations, dCas9 activators fused to enzymatic domains (like **dCas9-p300**) could theoretically leave a **low-level global epigenetic footprint** across the genome [26, 27]. For example, other epigenetic editors like dCas9-DNMT3A have been found to leave methylation marks independent of the sgRNA used, though it is yet to be determined if dCas9-p300 leaves a similarly pervasive global footprint [26, 28].

The off-target sites are often sgRNA-dependent, since Cas9 is known to tolerate up to 3 mismatches between sgRNA and genomic DNA (Fu et al., 2013; Hsu et al., 2013; Wang et al., 2016a). Nevertheless, accumulative studies have proved that sgRNAindependent off-target effects also exist, urging unbiased experimental detection and validation (Richter et al., 2020; O’Geen et al., 2015). In this review, we summarize available methods for the assessment of off-target effects, indicating their advantages versus limitations. Some of these detection methods for off-targets prediction is applicable for other family of Cas nucleases, such as Cas12a (Cpf1), which also create DSBs on off-target sites (Kim et al., 2019). Furthermore, we discuss strategies to improve CRISPR/Cas9 specificity and to reduce undesired mutagenesis, which is crucial for their future application in gene therapy.
