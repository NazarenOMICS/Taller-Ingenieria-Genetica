# Q01 - According to these sources, what is known specifically about dCas9-p300 (nuclease-dead CRISPRa fusion) off-target binding or off-target transcriptional activation in human cells, as distinct from wild-type Cas9 nuclease off-target cutting?

Based on the provided sources, **dCas9-p300** (a fusion of nuclease-dead Cas9 and the catalytic core of the human p300 acetyltransferase) exhibits a distinct specificity profile compared to wild-type (WT) Cas9, characterized by its focus on **epigenetic modification rather than permanent sequence alteration.**

### **High Functional Specificity in Human Cells**
*   **Minimal Transcriptomic Off-Targets:** Genome-wide transcriptome profiling (RNA-seq) of dCas9-p300 targeting the *IL1RN* promoter in human **HEK293T cells** revealed "limited" off-target gene induction  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/hilton_2015.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/hilton_2015.pdf#Passage 2|[2]]]. Only two unintended transcripts (*KDR* and *FAM49A*) were significantly induced above background at a false discovery rate (FDR) of less than 5%  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/hilton_2015.pdf#Passage 2|[2]]].
*   **Robust On-Target Performance:** In contrast to dCas9-VP64, which often requires multiple guide RNAs (gRNAs) to be effective, dCas9-p300 can achieve robust transcriptional activation using only a **single gRNA**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/yu_2015_making_sense_of_gwas_using_epigenomics_and_genome.pdf#Passage 1|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/hilton_2015.pdf#Passage 3|[4]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/hilton_2015.pdf#Passage 4|[5]]].
*   **Specific Histone Acetylation:** The fusion protein catalyzes the **acetylation of histone H3 lysine 27 (H3K27ac)** specifically at its target sites  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/yu_2015_making_sense_of_gwas_using_epigenomics_and_genome.pdf#Passage 1|[3]]]. In human cells, this modification was shown to be sufficient to activate gene expression from both promoters and distal enhancers, such as the human β-globin locus control region  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/hilton_2015.pdf#Passage 5|[6]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/tianxiang_2023_crispr_cas9_therapeutics_progress_and_prospects.pdf#Passage 1|[7]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/tianxiang_2023_crispr_cas9_therapeutics_progress_and_prospects.pdf#Passage 2|[8]]].

### **Distinctions from Wild-Type Cas9 Nuclease**
*   **Reversibility vs. Permanence:** WT Cas9 induces double-strand breaks (DSBs) that lead to **permanent, irreversible mutations** (indels) through error-prone repair pathways like NHEJ  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/tianxiang_2023_crispr_cas9_therapeutics_progress_and_prospects.pdf#Passage 3|[9]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/kendall_2018_optimized_libraries_for_crispr_cas9_genetic_screens_with.pdf#Passage 1|[10]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/kendall_2018_optimized_libraries_for_crispr_cas9_genetic_screens_with.pdf#Passage 2|[11]]]. Because dCas9-p300 does not cut DNA, its regulatory effects are **reversible and non-mutagenic**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/kendall_2018_optimized_libraries_for_crispr_cas9_genetic_screens_with.pdf#Passage 2|[11]]].
*   **Avoidance of Cutting-Related Toxicity:** WT Cas9 can cause "cutting-related toxicity," particularly at copy-number amplified loci where multiple DSBs lead to cell growth defects or death  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/kendall_2018_optimized_libraries_for_crispr_cas9_genetic_screens_with.pdf#Passage 3|[12]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/masahiro_2017_stabilization_of_foxp3_expression_by_crispr_dcas9_based.pdf#Passage 1|[13]]]. dCas9-based systems, including p300 fusions, mitigate this risk because they do not introduce genomic breaks  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/masahiro_2017_stabilization_of_foxp3_expression_by_crispr_dcas9_based.pdf#Passage 1|[13]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/mazhar_2018_the_crispr_tool_kit_for_genome_editing_and.pdf#Passage 1|[14]]].
*   **Spatial Specificity:** While WT Cas9 can be targeted anywhere to disrupt a gene, CRISPR activators like dCas9-p300 are highly constrained by position, typically requiring targeting within a specific window (e.g., 60–150 bp upstream of a transcription start site) to function effectively  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/hilton_2015.pdf#Passage 3|[4]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/tianxiang_2023_crispr_cas9_therapeutics_progress_and_prospects.pdf#Passage 4|[15]]].

### **Binding vs. Functional Activation**
*   **Less Stringent Binding Requirements:** Sources note that dCas9 DNA-binding has a **"less stringent requirement"** than the catalytic cleavage of WT Cas9  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/mazhar_2018_the_crispr_tool_kit_for_genome_editing_and.pdf#Passage 2|[16]]]. Genome-wide mapping (ChIP-seq) indicates that dCas9 associates with many off-target sites, particularly those enriched in **open chromatin regions**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/mazhar_2018_the_crispr_tool_kit_for_genome_editing_and.pdf#Passage 2|[16]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/mazhar_2018_the_crispr_tool_kit_for_genome_editing_and.pdf#Passage 3|[17]]].
*   **Limited Functional Consequence:** Despite widespread physical binding, only a "limited number" of these off-target associations result in actual gene activation or cleavage in vivo  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/mazhar_2018_the_crispr_tool_kit_for_genome_editing_and.pdf#Passage 2|[16]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/masahiro_2017_stabilization_of_foxp3_expression_by_crispr_dcas9_based.pdf#Passage 2|[18]]].

### **Remaining Uncertainties and Gaps**
*   **Theoretical Global Footprints:** There is an ongoing research gap regarding whether overexpressing dCas9-p300 might leave a **"low-level but global epigenetic footprint"** across the genome [19]. This concern is extrapolated from other editors, such as dCas9-DNMT3A, which was found to leave methylation footprints independent of the sgRNA sequence, but this has not yet been definitively determined for dCas9-p300 [19].
*   **Physical Presence vs. Mark Induction:** It remains unclear if the continued presence of the dCas9-p300 complex is required for sustained activation or if the **induced H3K27ac mark itself** establishes a functional "epigenetic memory" [20, 21].
