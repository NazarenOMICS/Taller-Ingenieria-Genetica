# Q02 - What guide RNA mismatch tolerance or sequence features have been reported in these sources that predispose dCas9-based systems to bind or activate unintended genomic loci?

Based on the provided sources, dCas9-based systems are predisposed to bind or activate unintended genomic loci due to specific guide RNA (sgRNA) mismatch tolerances, sequence-specific regional sensitivities, and broader genomic features like chromatin accessibility.

### **Guide RNA Mismatch Tolerance**
*   **Threshold for Mismatches:** Wild-type Cas9 (the basis for dCas9) is reported to tolerate up to **3 to 5 mismatches** between the sgRNA and the genomic DNA  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/congting_2023_off_target_effects_in_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 2|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/fatemeh_2019_crispr_cpf1_proteins_structure_function_and_implications_for.pdf#Passage 1|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/hongyi_2020_applications_of_genome_editing_technology_in_the_targeted.pdf#Passage 1|[3]]]. Because dCas9 binding has **less stringent requirements** than the nuclease cleavage activity of wild-type Cas9, dCas9 may bind to many more off-target sites than a standard Cas9 would cut  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/ashot_2019_site_specific_manipulation_of_arabidopsis_loci_using_crispr.pdf#Passage 1|[4]]].
*   **Positional Sensitivity:** The tolerance for mismatches is heavily dependent on their position relative to the Protospacer Adjacent Motif (PAM):
    *   **Seed Region:** The "seed sequence" consists of the **10–12 base pairs proximal to the PAM**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/tianxiang_2023_crispr_cas9_therapeutics_progress_and_prospects.pdf#Passage 3|[5]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/fatemeh_2019_crispr_cpf1_proteins_structure_function_and_implications_for.pdf#Passage 2|[6]]]. Mismatches in this region are generally poorly tolerated and significantly reduce or abolish binding  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/tianxiang_2023_crispr_cas9_therapeutics_progress_and_prospects.pdf#Passage 3|[5]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/fatemeh_2019_crispr_cpf1_proteins_structure_function_and_implications_for.pdf#Passage 2|[6]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/tianxiang_2023_crispr_cas9_therapeutics_progress_and_prospects.pdf#Passage 4|[7]]].
    *   **PAM-Distal Region:** Mismatches at the distal end of the gRNA (farthest from the PAM) are tolerated much more readily  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/fatemeh_2019_crispr_cpf1_proteins_structure_function_and_implications_for.pdf#Passage 1|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/ashot_2019_site_specific_manipulation_of_arabidopsis_loci_using_crispr.pdf#Passage 1|[4]]]. Mismatches at the **18th to 20th base positions** can form an "unusual duplex conformation" or a "Ruv loop structure" that stabilizes the off-target binding rather than causing the complex to detach  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/tianxiang_2023_crispr_cas9_therapeutics_progress_and_prospects.pdf#Passage 5|[8]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/hongyi_2020_applications_of_genome_editing_technology_in_the_targeted.pdf#Passage 2|[9]]].
    *   **"Core" Sequence:** One source identifies a specific "core" sequence (4 nucleotides located at the **+4 to +7 positions upstream of the PAM**) that appears to be particularly sensitive to mismatches  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/fatemeh_2019_crispr_cpf1_proteins_structure_function_and_implications_for.pdf#Passage 1|[2]]].

### **Sequence and Structural Features**
*   **Targeted Sequence Homology:** Off-target effects typically occur at genomic sites that are **highly homologous** to the intended target sequence  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/hongyi_2020_applications_of_genome_editing_technology_in_the_targeted.pdf#Passage 1|[3]]]. 
*   **SgRNA Length:** Truncating sgRNAs (shortening the 5' end by 2–3 nucleotides) can actually **increase specificity** by making the complex more sensitive to mismatches, thereby reducing unintended binding  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/mazhar_2018_the_crispr_tool_kit_for_genome_editing_and.pdf#Passage 4|[10]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/kendall_2018_optimized_libraries_for_crispr_cas9_genetic_screens_with.pdf#Passage 3|[11]]]. Conversely, extending the sgRNA length can also enhance specificity in some contexts  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/kendall_2018_optimized_libraries_for_crispr_cas9_genetic_screens_with.pdf#Passage 3|[11]]].
*   **TracrRNA Modifications:** Modifications to the tracrRNA, such as extending the tetraloop (tracr-v2) to improve on-target potency, have been shown to **increase off-target activity** with mismatched sgRNAs  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/kendall_2018_optimized_libraries_for_crispr_cas9_genetic_screens_with.pdf#Passage 4|[12]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/masahiro_2017_stabilization_of_foxp3_expression_by_crispr_dcas9_based.pdf#Passage 1|[13]]].

### **Genomic Predispositions**
*   **Chromatin Accessibility:** Mapping of dCas9 binding sites genome-wide indicates that off-target binding is heavily **enriched in open chromatin regions** (as identified by DNase-seq)  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/ashot_2019_site_specific_manipulation_of_arabidopsis_loci_using_crispr.pdf#Passage 1|[4]]]. Integrating chromatin accessibility data into prediction algorithms significantly improves the identification of these unintended binding sites  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/ashot_2019_site_specific_manipulation_of_arabidopsis_loci_using_crispr.pdf#Passage 1|[4]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/masahiro_2017_stabilization_of_foxp3_expression_by_crispr_dcas9_based.pdf#Passage 2|[14]]].
*   **Locus Context:** The effectiveness and specificity of dCas9-based systems can be influenced by the **epigenetic state** of the target. For instance, dCas9-p300 is more effective at activating genes in certain cell contexts than others, and its binding can be influenced by the presence of existing histone marks like H3K27me3  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/kendall_2018_optimized_libraries_for_crispr_cas9_genetic_screens_with.pdf#Passage 5|[15]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/kendall_2018_optimized_libraries_for_crispr_cas9_genetic_screens_with.pdf#Passage 6|[16]]].
*   **Copy Number Variation:** At loci that are **copy-number amplified**, even low-level binding by dCas9-based systems can lead to magnified functional consequences  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/fatemeh_2019_crispr_cpf1_proteins_structure_function_and_implications_for.pdf#Passage 3|[17]]].

### **Comparison with Cpf1 (Cas12a)**
While the query focuses on dCas9, the sources note that **Cpf1-based systems** generally exhibit **higher specificity** and a lower threshold for mismatches than Cas9  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/fatemeh_2019_crispr_cpf1_proteins_structure_function_and_implications_for.pdf#Passage 1|[2]]]. Mismatches between any of the first 18 nucleotides in the target DNA for Cpf1 are reported to impede its activity more effectively than in Cas9 systems  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q03-especificidad-off-target/Sources/fatemeh_2019_crispr_cpf1_proteins_structure_function_and_implications_for.pdf#Passage 1|[2]]].
