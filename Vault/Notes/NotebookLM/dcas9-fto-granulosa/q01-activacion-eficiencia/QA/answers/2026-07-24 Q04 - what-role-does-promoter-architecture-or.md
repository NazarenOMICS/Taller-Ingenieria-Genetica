# Q04 - What role does promoter architecture or local chromatin accessibility play in determining dCas9-p300 CRISPR activation efficiency, according to these sources?

According to the sources, **promoter architecture and local chromatin accessibility** are fundamental determinants of dCas9-p300 activation efficiency, as the system functions by directly modifying the epigenetic state to overcome physical barriers to transcription.

### Chromatin Accessibility and DNA Relaxation
*   **Accessibility as a Prerequisite:** The ability of the dCas9–sgRNA complex to localize and bind at a target sequence is constrained by the local chromatin environment  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q01-activacion-eficiencia/Sources/daan_2017_dcas9_a_versatile_tool_for_epigenome_editing.pdf#Passage 2|[1]]]. Highly chromatinized or "closed" regions may be inaccessible to the CRISPR machinery  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q01-activacion-eficiencia/Sources/tianxiang_2023_crispr_cas9_therapeutics_progress_and_prospects.pdf#Passage 1|[2]]].
*   **Mechanism of DNA Relaxation:** The p300 core domain functions as a histone acetyltransferase (HAT) that catalyzes **H3K27ac** (histone H3 lysine 27 acetylation)  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q01-activacion-eficiencia/Sources/hilton_2015.pdf#Passage 1|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q01-activacion-eficiencia/Sources/hilton_2015.pdf#Passage 10|[4]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q01-activacion-eficiencia/Sources/tianxiang_2023_crispr_cas9_therapeutics_progress_and_prospects.pdf#Passage 2|[5]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q01-activacion-eficiencia/Sources/joaquin_2019_improved_drought_stress_tolerance_in_arabidopsis_by_crispr.pdf#Passage 4|[6]]]. This modification triggers **DNA relaxation** by switching chromatin from a compacted state to a "relaxed" or "unwound" state, which exposes the DNA to the transcriptional machinery  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q01-activacion-eficiencia/Sources/joaquin_2019_improved_drought_stress_tolerance_in_arabidopsis_by_crispr.pdf#Passage 3|[7]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q01-activacion-eficiencia/Sources/joaquin_2019_improved_drought_stress_tolerance_in_arabidopsis_by_crispr.pdf#Passage 5|[8]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q01-activacion-eficiencia/Sources/jere_2018_human_pluripotent_reprogramming_with_crispr_activators.pdf#Passage 4|[9]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q01-activacion-eficiencia/Sources/jere_2018_human_pluripotent_reprogramming_with_crispr_activators.pdf#Passage 5|[10]]].
*   **Pioneer-like Activity:** The act of dCas9 binding itself has been demonstrated to **functionally open chromatin** near its target site, which can facilitate the activation process  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q01-activacion-eficiencia/Sources/hilton_2015.pdf#Passage 11|[11]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q01-activacion-eficiencia/Sources/mazhar_2018_the_crispr_tool_kit_for_genome_editing_and.pdf#Passage 1|[12]]].

### Flexibility in Promoter and Enhancer Architecture
*   **Broad Targeting Range:** A defining feature of dCas9-p300 is its unique ability to activate gene expression from both **proximal promoters and distal enhancers**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q01-activacion-eficiencia/Sources/hilton_2015.pdf#Passage 1|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q01-activacion-eficiencia/Sources/hilton_2015.pdf#Passage 3|[13]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q01-activacion-eficiencia/Sources/hilton_2015.pdf#Passage 12|[14]]]. While many other activators (like dCas9-VP64) are primarily effective when targeted to promoters, dCas9-p300 can leverage the 3D architecture of the genome to induce expression from distant regulatory elements  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q01-activacion-eficiencia/Sources/hilton_2015.pdf#Passage 1|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q01-activacion-eficiencia/Sources/kohei_2022_comparative_analysis_of_dcas9_vp64_variants_and_multiplexed.pdf#Passage 9|[15]]].
*   **Enhancer-Promoter Communication:** Targeting p300 to an enhancer catalyzes H3K27ac enrichment at both the enhancer and the **distal promoters** it regulates, leading to robust activation where standard systems typically fail  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q01-activacion-eficiencia/Sources/hilton_2015.pdf#Passage 1|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q01-activacion-eficiencia/Sources/kohei_2022_comparative_analysis_of_dcas9_vp64_variants_and_multiplexed.pdf#Passage 9|[15]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q01-activacion-eficiencia/Sources/kohei_2022_comparative_analysis_of_dcas9_vp64_variants_and_multiplexed.pdf#Passage 15|[16]]].
*   **Cellular Context:** The efficiency of this activation often depends on the pre-existing state of the locus. For example, HAT-mediated activation was found to be **stronger when the drought stress response was already activated**, suggesting that the existing "folding" or state of the chromatin at the locus dictates how much additional activation the system can achieve  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q01-activacion-eficiencia/Sources/jere_2018_human_pluripotent_reprogramming_with_crispr_activators.pdf#Passage 4|[9]]].

### Spatial and Structural Constraints
*   **Steric Hindrance:** Because the p300 core domain is a **relatively large protein**, it is sensitive to the physical spacing of binding sites  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q01-activacion-eficiencia/Sources/kohei_2022_comparative_analysis_of_dcas9_vp64_variants_and_multiplexed.pdf#Passage 11|[17]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q01-activacion-eficiencia/Sources/joaquin_2019_improved_drought_stress_tolerance_in_arabidopsis_by_crispr.pdf#Passage 2|[18]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q01-activacion-eficiencia/Sources/kohei_2022_comparative_analysis_of_dcas9_vp64_variants_and_multiplexed.pdf#Passage 10|[19]]]. If multiple guide RNAs are targeted to sites that are too close together (e.g., within 118 bp), the resulting **steric hindrance** can destabilize the protein complexes on the DNA, potentially resulting in *lower* activation than a single guide would provide  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q01-activacion-eficiencia/Sources/hilton_2015.pdf#Passage 9|[20]]].
*   **Limited Sensitivity on Repetitive Sites:** In experiments using promoters with multiple repetitive binding sites (such as the TRE3G promoter with seven sites), dCas9-p300 showed **limited increases in activity** compared to smaller activators like VP64, a finding attributed to these physical and spatial limitations  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q01-activacion-eficiencia/Sources/kohei_2022_comparative_analysis_of_dcas9_vp64_variants_and_multiplexed.pdf#Passage 11|[17]]].
*   **Responsiveness Factors:** Differences in gene responsiveness do not always correlate perfectly with initial accessibility (as measured by DNase-seq) but may instead be dictated by **transcription factor occupancy**, competition, or the underlying local epigenetic circuitry [22].

Studies on target specificity have mainly been done on Cas9 (Fu et al., 2014; Slaymaker et al., 2016; Kleinstiver et al., 2016); these findings can be extrapolated to dCas9 and dCas9-based effectors. The ability of the Cas9–sgRNA complex to localize and bind at a target sequence could be altered by genome accessibility as a consequence of local chromatin environment (Knight et al., 2015) or binding of endogenous proteins such as transcrip- tion factors (Hilton et al., 2015). Taking that notion into account target sites can be selected based on information regarding genome-bound proteins (e.g. using DNase-seq) (Maeder et al., 2013b; Kearns et al., 2014; Thakore et al., 2015). Alternatively, DNA accessibility and off-target binding are determined ad hoc for every application. Off-target binding is related to flexibility in PAM sequence recognition and permitted mismatches in the PAM-distal part of the sgRNA (Kim et al., 2015; Ran et al., 2015; Tsai et al., 2015; Wang et al., 2015b; Leenay et al., 2016). Efforts to reduce off-target binding include rational redesign of Cas9 for higher PAM specificity (Kleinstiver et al., 2015), reduction of non-specific charge interactions between Cas9 and non-target DNA strand (Slaymaker et al., 2016) or hydrogen bonding between Cas9 and the backbones of the target DNA strand and the sgRNA (Kleinstiver et al., 2016), as well as use of truncated sgRNAs (Fu et al., 2014). Although many of these strategies lead to a reduction of the DNA binding affinity of Cas9 or its activity, these efforts have contributed significantly to overcoming Cas9 off-target binding.

> Targeted plant genome editing using CRISPR nucleases has become a promising approach to create new plant varieties17–19. Beyond genome editing, the CRISPR mechanism has been remodeled to accomplish CRISPR activation (CRISPRa) and CRISPR interference (CRISPRi)20. The catalytically inactive form of Cas9 (dead Cas9, abbreviated dCas9) has been fused with transcription activators and inhibitors to modify transcription through specific gene promoters and with chromatin modulator domains to facilitate targeted epigenome editing21–23. Histone acetyltransferase (HAT) catalyzes the acetylation of core histones through the addition of an acetyl group to the lysine residue on the terminal tail of the histones24. Histone acetylation triggers DNA relaxation and leads to exposure of DNA to the transcriptional machinery25. Thus, HAT activity is correlated with gene expression activation. In this way, the use of dCas9 in fusion with HAT (dCas9HAT), combined with the directed targeting of sgRNAs, appears promising for positively regulating the activity of a targeted promoter26,27. CRISPR/dCas9 epigenome editing of specific drought stress response genes therefore emerges as an encouraging strategy for improving stress tolerance in plants.
