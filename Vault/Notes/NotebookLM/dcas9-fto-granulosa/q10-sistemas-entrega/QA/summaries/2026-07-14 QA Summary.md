## Resumen por QA

## Q01
- Pregunta: According to these sources, how do chemical transfection, electroporation, and lentiviral transduction compare in efficiency and cellular perturbation for delivering large CRISPR/dCas9 constructs?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q10-sistemas-entrega/QA/answers/2026-07-14 Q01 - according-to-these-sources-how-do.md]]

For delivering large CRISPR/dCas9 constructs, the sources compare chemical transfection, electroporation, and lentiviral transduction based on their ability to handle large cargo, their delivery efficiency, and the level of cellular perturbation (toxicity, immunogenicity, and genomic risk).
## Q02
- Pregunta: According to these sources, what delivery platforms (lipid nanoparticles, viral vectors, RNP) have been used specifically for dCas9-based (nuclease-dead) constructs, and what are their reported efficiencies?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q10-sistemas-entrega/QA/answers/2026-07-14 Q02 - according-to-these-sources-what-delivery.md]]

Based on the provided sources, **dCas9 (nuclease-dead Cas9)** constructs have been delivered using a variety of platforms including **magnetofection, viral vectors, lipid-based systems, and engineered exosomes**. These constructs are primarily utilized for gene regulation (CRISPRi/a), epigenome editing, and imaging  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q10-sistemas-entrega/Sources/johanna_1970_lipid_nanoparticle_mediated_delivery_of_crispr_cas9_for.pdf#Passage 10|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q10-sistemas-entrega/Sources/mazhar_2018_the_crispr_tool_kit_for_genome_editing_and.pdf#Passage 2|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q10-sistemas-entrega/Sources/mazhar_2018_the_crispr_tool_kit_for_genome_editing_and.pdf#Passage 3|[3]]].
## Q03
- Pregunta: What challenges do these sources describe in delivering large Cas9/dCas9-fusion payloads (e.g., dCas9-p300, ~5-6 kb) via lentivirus, nanoparticles, or mRNA/RNP compared to smaller CRISPR cargos?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q10-sistemas-entrega/QA/answers/2026-07-14 Q03 - what-challenges-do-these-sources-describe.md]]

The sources describe several unique challenges associated with delivering large **Cas9/dCas9-fusion payloads** (such as dCas9-p300 or base editors, which can reach 5–6 kb) compared to standard CRISPR cargos. These challenges primarily revolve around **packaging constraints**, **colloidal stability**, and **cellular barriers** to entry and escape.
## Q04
- Pregunta: According to these sources, what transfection or transduction approaches have been used or would be expected to work in granulosa-derived cell lines (KGN, COV434) or hard-to-transfect primary/stem-like cells, and what perturbation or toxicity concerns are noted?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q10-sistemas-entrega/QA/answers/2026-07-14 Q04 - according-to-these-sources-what-transfection.md]]

For granulosa-derived cells and other hard-to-transfect primary or stem-like populations, the sources identify **lentiviral transduction**, **magnetofection**, **electroporation**, and **exosome-based delivery** as primary approaches, each with distinct trade-offs regarding efficiency and cellular stress.
## Q05
- Pregunta: Based on these sources, which delivery method (transient transfection, lentivirus, nanoparticle, mRNA, or RNP) offers the best balance of efficiency versus minimal cellular perturbation/immunogenicity for a large CRISPRa effector like dCas9-p300?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q10-sistemas-entrega/QA/answers/2026-07-14 Q05 - based-on-these-sources-which-delivery.md]]

Based on the provided sources, the delivery of **mRNA encapsulated in lipid nanoparticles (LNPs)** offers the best overall balance of high efficiency and minimal cellular perturbation for a large CRISPRa effector like dCas9-p300.
## Q06
- Pregunta: What gaps remain in these sources regarding delivery of dCas9-p300 specifically (as opposed to Cas9 nuclease or smaller dCas9 fusions) into KGN, COV434, or primary human granulosa cells?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q10-sistemas-entrega/QA/answers/2026-07-14 Q06 - what-gaps-remain-these-sources-regarding.md]]

The provided sources reveal several significant gaps regarding the delivery of the **dCas9-p300** effector specifically into **KGN, COV434**, or **primary human granulosa cells**. While the sources discuss the general mechanics of large CRISPR effectors and experiments in rat granulosa cells, they lack specific data on these human models.
