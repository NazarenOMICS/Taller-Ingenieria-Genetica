# Q04 - According to these sources, which of these senescence and DNA damage markers have been specifically applied in granulosa cell or ovarian aging models?

In the provided sources, several senescence and DNA damage markers have been specifically applied to study **mouse granulosa cells**, **fetal oocytes**, and **ovarian aging** models. 

### Markers Applied in Granulosa Cell Models
Research investigating oxidative stress in immature mouse granulosa cells employed the following markers:
*   **$\gamma$H2AX (histone H2AX phosphorylation):** Used as a sensitive marker for **DNA double-strand breaks (DSBs)**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q09-marcadores-senescencia/Sources/manabu_2014_melatonin_protects_the_integrity_of_granulosa_cells_by.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q09-marcadores-senescencia/Sources/manabu_2014_melatonin_protects_the_integrity_of_granulosa_cells_by.pdf#Passage 4|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q09-marcadores-senescencia/Sources/manabu_2014_melatonin_protects_the_integrity_of_granulosa_cells_by.pdf#Passage 2|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q09-marcadores-senescencia/Sources/manabu_2014_melatonin_protects_the_integrity_of_granulosa_cells_by.pdf#Passage 5|[4]]].
*   **8-OHdG (8-hydroxydeoxyguanosine):** Specifically used in granulosa cells to indicate **oxidative guanine base damage** in nuclear DNA  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q09-marcadores-senescencia/Sources/manabu_2014_melatonin_protects_the_integrity_of_granulosa_cells_by.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q09-marcadores-senescencia/Sources/manabu_2014_melatonin_protects_the_integrity_of_granulosa_cells_by.pdf#Passage 4|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q09-marcadores-senescencia/Sources/manabu_2014_melatonin_protects_the_integrity_of_granulosa_cells_by.pdf#Passage 3|[5]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q09-marcadores-senescencia/Sources/manabu_2014_melatonin_protects_the_integrity_of_granulosa_cells_by.pdf#Passage 6|[6]]].
*   **MitoTracker Red:** Utilized to evaluate **mitochondrial function** by measuring membrane potentials; a reduction in staining intensity in granulosa cells indicates stress-induced dysfunction  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q09-marcadores-senescencia/Sources/manabu_2014_melatonin_protects_the_integrity_of_granulosa_cells_by.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q09-marcadores-senescencia/Sources/manabu_2014_melatonin_protects_the_integrity_of_granulosa_cells_by.pdf#Passage 7|[7]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q09-marcadores-senescencia/Sources/manabu_2014_melatonin_protects_the_integrity_of_granulosa_cells_by.pdf#Passage 8|[8]]].
*   **Hexanoyl-lysine (HEL):** Applied as an oxidative stress marker to measure **lipid peroxidation** of the granulosa cell plasma membranes  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q09-marcadores-senescencia/Sources/manabu_2014_melatonin_protects_the_integrity_of_granulosa_cells_by.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q09-marcadores-senescencia/Sources/manabu_2014_melatonin_protects_the_integrity_of_granulosa_cells_by.pdf#Passage 9|[9]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q09-marcadores-senescencia/Sources/manabu_2014_melatonin_protects_the_integrity_of_granulosa_cells_by.pdf#Passage 10|[10]]].
*   **Caspase-3/7 Activity:** Used to quantify apoptotic activity in response to oxidative stress  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q09-marcadores-senescencia/Sources/manabu_2014_melatonin_protects_the_integrity_of_granulosa_cells_by.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q09-marcadores-senescencia/Sources/manabu_2014_melatonin_protects_the_integrity_of_granulosa_cells_by.pdf#Passage 11|[11]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q09-marcadores-senescencia/Sources/xia_2017_mir_22_suppresses_tumorigenesis_and_improves_radiosensitivity_of.pdf#Passage 4|[12]]].
*   **Sirt1 (Silent information regulator 1):** Identified as a direct target of miR-22 in mouse ovarian granulosa cells, where it regulates apoptosis and radiosensitivity  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q09-marcadores-senescencia/Sources/xia_2017_mir_22_suppresses_tumorigenesis_and_improves_radiosensitivity_of.pdf#Passage 5|[13]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q09-marcadores-senescencia/Sources/xia_2017_mir_22_suppresses_tumorigenesis_and_improves_radiosensitivity_of.pdf#Passage 6|[14]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q09-marcadores-senescencia/Sources/emanuela_2017_overactive_type_2_cannabinoid_receptor_induces_meiosis_in.pdf#Passage 3|[15]]].

### Markers Applied in Ovarian and Fetal Oocyte Models
In studies of fetal gonads and the depletion of the ovarian reserve, the following markers were applied:
*   **$\gamma$H2AX:** Used in fetal oocytes to identify regions of **chromosome asynapsis**; an increase in these foci at late meiotic stages (pachytene and diplotene) indicates a failure to repair DSBs, leading to apoptosis  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q09-marcadores-senescencia/Sources/emanuela_2017_overactive_type_2_cannabinoid_receptor_induces_meiosis_in.pdf#Passage 5|[16]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q09-marcadores-senescencia/Sources/emanuela_2017_overactive_type_2_cannabinoid_receptor_induces_meiosis_in.pdf#Passage 2|[17]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q09-marcadores-senescencia/Sources/emanuela_2017_overactive_type_2_cannabinoid_receptor_induces_meiosis_in.pdf#Passage 6|[18]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q09-marcadores-senescencia/Sources/emanuela_2017_overactive_type_2_cannabinoid_receptor_induces_meiosis_in.pdf#Passage 4|[19]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q09-marcadores-senescencia/Sources/emanuela_2017_overactive_type_2_cannabinoid_receptor_induces_meiosis_in.pdf#Passage 7|[20]]].
*   **TUNEL Assay:** Specifically applied to fetal oocytes alongside SCP3 to detect **apoptotic nuclei** resulting from accelerated meiosis without proper DNA repair  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q09-marcadores-senescencia/Sources/emanuela_2017_overactive_type_2_cannabinoid_receptor_induces_meiosis_in.pdf#Passage 5|[16]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q09-marcadores-senescencia/Sources/emanuela_2017_overactive_type_2_cannabinoid_receptor_induces_meiosis_in.pdf#Passage 6|[18]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q09-marcadores-senescencia/Sources/emanuela_2017_overactive_type_2_cannabinoid_receptor_induces_meiosis_in.pdf#Passage 8|[21]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q09-marcadores-senescencia/Sources/emanuela_2017_overactive_type_2_cannabinoid_receptor_induces_meiosis_in.pdf#Passage 9|[22]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q09-marcadores-senescencia/Sources/emanuela_2017_overactive_type_2_cannabinoid_receptor_induces_meiosis_in.pdf#Passage 10|[23]]].
*   **SCP3 (Synaptonemal Complex Protein 3):** Used as a meiotic marker to identify chromosomal axial elements and track the **temporal progression of oocytes** through various stages of prophase I  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q09-marcadores-senescencia/Sources/emanuela_2017_overactive_type_2_cannabinoid_receptor_induces_meiosis_in.pdf#Passage 5|[16]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q09-marcadores-senescencia/Sources/emanuela_2017_overactive_type_2_cannabinoid_receptor_induces_meiosis_in.pdf#Passage 6|[18]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q09-marcadores-senescencia/Sources/emanuela_2017_overactive_type_2_cannabinoid_receptor_induces_meiosis_in.pdf#Passage 8|[21]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q09-marcadores-senescencia/Sources/emanuela_2017_overactive_type_2_cannabinoid_receptor_induces_meiosis_in.pdf#Passage 11|[24]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q09-marcadores-senescencia/Sources/emanuela_2017_overactive_type_2_cannabinoid_receptor_induces_meiosis_in.pdf#Passage 12|[25]]].
*   **VASA:** Used as a specific **germ cell marker** in fetal ovarian tissue analysis  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q09-marcadores-senescencia/Sources/emanuela_2017_overactive_type_2_cannabinoid_receptor_induces_meiosis_in.pdf#Passage 8|[21]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q09-marcadores-senescencia/Sources/emanuela_2017_overactive_type_2_cannabinoid_receptor_induces_meiosis_in.pdf#Passage 12|[25]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q09-marcadores-senescencia/Sources/xia_2023_inflammation_and_aging_signaling_pathways_and_intervention_therapies.pdf#Passage 4|[26]]].

### Primate Ovarian Aging
A single-cell transcriptomic atlas of **primate ovarian aging** was established to identify markers of decline. This model specifically identified the **disruption of antioxidant signaling** in early-stage oocytes and granulosa cells as a critical factor in age-related ovarian deterioration  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q09-marcadores-senescencia/Sources/xia_2023_inflammation_and_aging_signaling_pathways_and_intervention_therapies.pdf#Passage 5|[27]]]. While p16 and p21 are noted as consensus markers for senescence in other organs, the ovarian-specific analysis in these sources emphasizes the role of **oxidative damage** as the primary driver of functional decline  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q09-marcadores-senescencia/Sources/xia_2023_inflammation_and_aging_signaling_pathways_and_intervention_therapies.pdf#Passage 5|[27]]].
