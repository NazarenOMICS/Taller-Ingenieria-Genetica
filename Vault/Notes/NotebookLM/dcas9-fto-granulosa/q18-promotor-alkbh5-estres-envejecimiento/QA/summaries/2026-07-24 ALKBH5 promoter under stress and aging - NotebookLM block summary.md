## Hilo central del bloque

- Directly reported promoter-level mechanisms and transcriptional regulators of **ALKBH5** include the following:
- Based on the provided sources, the regulation of the **ALKBH5** promoter is altered by metabolic and hypoxic stress, although for **oxidative stress, aging, and cellular senescence**, the sources primarily focus on post-translational modifications and protein localization rather than direct promoter-level DNA modifications.
- Specific mechanisms at the **ALKBH5 promoter** reported in the sources include regulation by several transcription factors and conceptual models for metabolic chromatin remodeling, though direct evidence for DNA methylation or specific histone acetylation (H3K27ac) at this promoter is more limited compared to other genes.
- Evidence for the **promoter-level regulation of ALKBH5** is specifically documented in several female reproductive contexts, most notably in **epithelial ovarian cancer (EOC)** and the **endometrium**, involving direct transcription factor binding and signaling axes.
- Based on the sources, several **promoter-level mechanisms** for ALKBH5 and FTO exhibit functional analogies or specific differences in their response to stress and metabolic cues.
- A strong mechanistic extrapolation from **ALKBH5 promoter regulation** to **FTO downregulation in granulosa cell (GC) aging** is hindered by several critical evidence gaps. While both erasers are redox-sensitive and crucial for ovarian homeostasis, the sources suggest they often respond to aging and stress through fundamentally different or even inverse pathways.
