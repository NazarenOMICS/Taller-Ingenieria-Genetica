# Q02 - Is ALKBH5 promoter regulation altered by oxidative stress, cellular senescence, or aging in any cell type?

Based on the provided sources, the regulation of the **ALKBH5** promoter is altered by metabolic and hypoxic stress, although for **oxidative stress, aging, and cellular senescence**, the sources primarily focus on post-translational modifications and protein localization rather than direct promoter-level DNA modifications.

### 1. Regulation Under Oxidative and Metabolic Stress
*   **Histone Lactylation and Chromatin Accessibility:** In the context of **sepsis**—a state characterized by profound metabolic stress and excessive lactate accumulation—conceptual models suggest that **histone lactylation** may drive chromatin remodeling  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/chen_2026_m_lt_sup_gt_6_lt_sup_gt.pdf#Passage 4|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/chen_2026_m_lt_sup_gt_6_lt_sup_gt.pdf#Passage 3|[2]]]. This remodeling is proposed to alter the **transcriptional accessibility** of the *ALKBH5* locus, thereby modulating its abundance  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/chen_2026_m_lt_sup_gt_6_lt_sup_gt.pdf#Passage 3|[2]]].
*   **Hypoxia-Induced Transcription:** Hypoxia (often coupled with redox shifts) induces *ALKBH5* transcription through **Hypoxia-Inducible Factors (HIF-1$\alpha$ and HIF-2$\alpha$)**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/chen_2026_m_lt_sup_gt_6_lt_sup_gt.pdf#Passage 1|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/yuanyuan_2022_the_role_of_m6a_rna_methylation_in_cancer.pdf#Passage 1|[4]]]. These factors directly activate the *ALKBH5* gene to promote phenotypes such as breast cancer stem cell maintenance  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/yuanyuan_2022_the_role_of_m6a_rna_methylation_in_cancer.pdf#Passage 1|[4]]].
*   **Post-Translational Suppression by ROS:** While the *ALKBH5* promoter responds to transcriptional activators, **reactive oxygen species (ROS)** are directly reported to suppress ALKBH5 activity through **post-translational SUMOylation** via the ERK/JNK pathways  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/chen_2026_m_lt_sup_gt_6_lt_sup_gt.pdf#Passage 1|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/chen_2026_m_lt_sup_gt_6_lt_sup_gt.pdf#Passage 5|[5]]]. This modification sterically limits substrate accessibility rather than altering the promoter itself  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/chen_2026_m_lt_sup_gt_6_lt_sup_gt.pdf#Passage 1|[3]]].

### 2. Regulation Under Inflammatory Stress
*   **NF-$\kappa$B Axis:** In the tumor microenvironment (e.g., ovarian cancer), stress-induced inflammatory signaling through the **TLR4/NF-$\kappa$B** axis upregulates the expression of *ALKBH5*  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/yuanyuan_2022_the_role_of_m6a_rna_methylation_in_cancer.pdf#Passage 3|[6]]]. High levels of TLR4 activate NF-$\kappa$B, which then acts to increase *ALKBH5* levels, contributing to carcinogenesis  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/yuanyuan_2022_the_role_of_m6a_rna_methylation_in_cancer.pdf#Passage 3|[6]]].

### 3. Regulation in Cellular Senescence and Aging
*   **Protein Localization Changes:** In **cellular senescence**, the sources focus on a **reversible cytosolic aggregation** of the ALKBH5 protein  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/li_2026_the_role_of_n_lt_sup_gt_6.pdf#Passage 3|[7]]]. This aggregation accelerates the senescent phenotype by reducing the nuclear availability of the demethylase, leading to m6A hypermethylation of target transcripts like *Cdk2*  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/li_2026_the_role_of_n_lt_sup_gt_6.pdf#Passage 3|[7]]]. Restoring nuclear translocation of ALKBH5 can alleviate this senescent phenotype  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/li_2026_the_role_of_n_lt_sup_gt_6.pdf#Passage 3|[7]]].
*   **General Epigenetic Shifts:** The sources note that **aging** can induce genome-wide hypermethylation and silence tumor suppressor genes  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/yan_2026_the_role_of_epigenetic_modifications_in_cancer_associated.pdf#Passage 1|[8]]]. However, unlike the **LEDGF** gene (which is explicitly reported to undergo **aberrant promoter hypermethylation** during aging and oxidative stress in lens epithelial cells  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/biju_2024_dna_methylation_as_an_epigenetic_mechanism_in_the.pdf#Passage 1|[9]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/biju_2024_dna_methylation_as_an_epigenetic_mechanism_in_the.pdf#Passage 2|[10]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/fang_2024_astrocytic_alkbh5_in_stress_response_contributes_to_depressive.pdf#Passage 1|[11]]]), there is **no direct report** in these sources of specific *ALKBH5* promoter methylation changes associated with aging.

### 4. Cell-Type Specificity
*   **Astrocytes:** ALKBH5 is reported to be **more sensitive to stress** in astrocytes compared to neurons or endothelial cells  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/zhou_2020_circular_rna_metabolism_functions_and_interactions_with_proteins.pdf#Passage 1|[12]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/chen_2026_m_lt_sup_gt_6_lt_sup_gt.pdf#Passage 6|[13]]]. In response to chronic stress, its localization can be altered by circular RNAs (like **circSTAG1**), which capture it in the cytoplasm to affect downstream m6A levels  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/chen_2026_m_lt_sup_gt_6_lt_sup_gt.pdf#Passage 7|[14]]].
*   **Cardiomyocytes:** Under septic and redox stress, the balance of m6A erasers, including ALKBH5, is perturbed, leading to maladaptive remodeling of mitochondrial function  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/chen_2026_m_lt_sup_gt_6_lt_sup_gt.pdf#Passage 8|[15]]].

> Fig. 1. m6A–ROS–mitochondrial quality control axis orchestrates cell fate decisions in sepsis-induced myocardial dysfunction Sepsis triggers a systemic inflammatory response characterized by excessive cytokine release and oxidative stress, which converges on the heart and contributes to myocardial dysfunction. Under septic conditions, sustained ROS production is associated with epitranscriptomic remodeling mediated by dysregulated m6A machinery, including altered activity of m6A writers (METTL3/14), erasers (FTO, ALKBH5), and readers (YTHDF1/2/3, IGF2BP family). m6A-dependent post- transcriptional reprogramming is proposed to influence MQC by modulating mitochondrial dynamics (fusion and fission), mitophagy, and mitochondrial integrity.
