# Q01 - According to these sources, what promoter-level mechanisms are directly reported to regulate ALKBH5 transcription?

Directly reported promoter-level mechanisms and transcriptional regulators of **ALKBH5** include the following:

*   **HOXA10 Binding:** In epithelial ovarian cancer (EOC), **HOXA10** acts as a direct upstream transcription factor for *ALKBH5*  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/sipei_2021_alkbh5_hoxa10_loop_mediated_jak2_m6a_demethylation_and.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/sipei_2021_alkbh5_hoxa10_loop_mediated_jak2_m6a_demethylation_and.pdf#Passage 2|[2]]]. HOXA10 interacts specifically with the **"TAAA" region** of the *ALKBH5* promoter to enhance its transcription  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/sipei_2021_alkbh5_hoxa10_loop_mediated_jak2_m6a_demethylation_and.pdf#Passage 2|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/sipei_2021_alkbh5_hoxa10_loop_mediated_jak2_m6a_demethylation_and.pdf#Passage 3|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/sipei_2021_alkbh5_hoxa10_loop_mediated_jak2_m6a_demethylation_and.pdf#Passage 4|[4]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/chen_2026_m_lt_sup_gt_6_lt_sup_gt.pdf#Passage 1|[5]]]. This interaction is part of a **positive feedback loop**, as ALKBH5 also stabilizes *HOXA10* mRNA  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/sipei_2021_alkbh5_hoxa10_loop_mediated_jak2_m6a_demethylation_and.pdf#Passage 2|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/sipei_2021_alkbh5_hoxa10_loop_mediated_jak2_m6a_demethylation_and.pdf#Passage 4|[4]]].
*   **Hypoxia-Inducible Factors (HIFs):** Under hypoxic conditions, *ALKBH5* transcription is directly induced by **HIF-1$\alpha$**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/chen_2026_m_lt_sup_gt_6_lt_sup_gt.pdf#Passage 2|[6]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/yuanyuan_2022_the_role_of_m6a_rna_methylation_in_cancer.pdf#Passage 1|[7]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/yuanyuan_2022_the_role_of_m6a_rna_methylation_in_cancer.pdf#Passage 2|[8]]]. Research in breast cancer indicates that both **HIF-1$\alpha$ and HIF-2$\alpha$** can activate *ALKBH5* gene transcription  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/yuanyuan_2022_the_role_of_m6a_rna_methylation_in_cancer.pdf#Passage 2|[8]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/li_2026_the_role_of_n_lt_sup_gt_6.pdf#Passage 1|[9]]]. Furthermore, a positive feedback loop exists where stabilized HIF-1$\alpha$ transactivates *ALKBH5*, while ALKBH5 subsequently stabilizes HIF-1$\alpha$ by removing m6A marks from *HDAC4* transcripts  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/li_2026_the_role_of_n_lt_sup_gt_6.pdf#Passage 2|[10]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/kuai_2026_unravelling_multilayered_rna_modification_networks_in_female_reproduction.pdf#Passage 1|[11]]].
*   **TLR4/NF-$\kappa$B Signaling:** In the ovarian cancer tumor microenvironment, the **TLR4 and NF-$\kappa$B** signaling axis regulates *ALKBH5* expression  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/kuai_2026_unravelling_multilayered_rna_modification_networks_in_female_reproduction.pdf#Passage 2|[12]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/yuanyuan_2022_the_role_of_m6a_rna_methylation_in_cancer.pdf#Passage 3|[13]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/yuanyuan_2022_the_role_of_m6a_rna_methylation_in_cancer.pdf#Passage 4|[14]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/yuanyuan_2022_the_role_of_m6a_rna_methylation_in_cancer.pdf#Passage 5|[15]]]. Specifically, high expression of TLR4 activates NF-$\kappa$B, which then upregulates the expression of *ALKBH5*  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/yuanyuan_2022_the_role_of_m6a_rna_methylation_in_cancer.pdf#Passage 4|[14]]].
*   **DDX3 Regulation:** In oral squamous cell carcinoma, the DEAD-box helicase **DDX3** is reported to directly regulate the expression of *ALKBH5*  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/chen_2026_m_lt_sup_gt_6_lt_sup_gt.pdf#Passage 3|[16]]].
*   **Epigenetic Crosstalk (Histone Lactylation):** While direct evidence is still emerging, conceptual models suggest that **histone lactylation** may modulate the transcriptional accessibility of the *ALKBH5* locus [17]. In the context of sepsis, lactylation-driven chromatin remodeling may alter the openness of promoters for core m6A regulators, including *ALKBH5* [17].
