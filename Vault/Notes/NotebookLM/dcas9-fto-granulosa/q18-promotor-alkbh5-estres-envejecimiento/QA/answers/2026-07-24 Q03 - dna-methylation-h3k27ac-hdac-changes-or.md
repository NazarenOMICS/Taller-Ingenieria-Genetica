# Q03 - Are DNA methylation, H3K27ac/HDAC changes, or specific transcription factors implicated at the ALKBH5 promoter?

Specific mechanisms at the **ALKBH5 promoter** reported in the sources include regulation by several transcription factors and conceptual models for metabolic chromatin remodeling, though direct evidence for DNA methylation or specific histone acetylation (H3K27ac) at this promoter is more limited compared to other genes.

### **1. Specific Transcription Factors**
The sources directly implicate several transcription factors in the regulation of *ALKBH5* gene transcription:
*   **HOXA10:** In epithelial ovarian cancer, **HOXA10** acts as a direct upstream transcription factor for *ALKBH5*  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/biju_2024_dna_methylation_as_an_epigenetic_mechanism_in_the.pdf#Passage 3|[1]]]. It specifically interacts with the **"TAAA" region** of the *ALKBH5* promoter to enhance its transcription  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/biju_2024_dna_methylation_as_an_epigenetic_mechanism_in_the.pdf#Passage 3|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/sipei_2021_alkbh5_hoxa10_loop_mediated_jak2_m6a_demethylation_and.pdf#Passage 2|[2]]]. This is part of a positive feedback loop where ALKBH5 also stabilizes *HOXA10* mRNA  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/biju_2024_dna_methylation_as_an_epigenetic_mechanism_in_the.pdf#Passage 3|[1]]].
*   **Hypoxia-Inducible Factors (HIFs):** Under hypoxic conditions, *ALKBH5* transcription is directly activated by **HIF-1$\alpha$ and HIF-2$\alpha$**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/biju_2024_dna_methylation_as_an_epigenetic_mechanism_in_the.pdf#Passage 4|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/yuanyuan_2022_the_role_of_m6a_rna_methylation_in_cancer.pdf#Passage 1|[4]]]. Research indicates that a positive feedback loop exists where stabilized HIF-1$\alpha$ transactivates *ALKBH5*, while ALKBH5 subsequently stabilizes HIF-1$\alpha$ by removing m6A marks from its regulators (e.g., *HDAC4*)  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/biju_2024_dna_methylation_as_an_epigenetic_mechanism_in_the.pdf#Passage 5|[5]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/li_2026_the_role_of_n_lt_sup_gt_6.pdf#Passage 1|[6]]].
*   **NF-$\kappa$B:** High expression of **TLR4** activates **NF-$\kappa$B**, which then upregulates the expression of *ALKBH5* in the ovarian cancer tumor microenvironment  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/biju_2024_dna_methylation_as_an_epigenetic_mechanism_in_the.pdf#Passage 4|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/yuanyuan_2022_the_role_of_m6a_rna_methylation_in_cancer.pdf#Passage 3|[7]]].
*   **DDX3:** The DEAD-box helicase **DDX3** is reported to directly regulate the expression of *ALKBH5* in oral squamous cell carcinoma  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/biju_2024_dna_methylation_as_an_epigenetic_mechanism_in_the.pdf#Passage 4|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/yuanyuan_2022_the_role_of_m6a_rna_methylation_in_cancer.pdf#Passage 5|[8]]].

### **2. Histone Modification and Chromatin Accessibility**
While specific H3K27ac/HDAC status at the *ALKBH5* promoter is not detailed for all contexts, the sources describe emerging epigenetic crosstalk:
*   **Histone Lactylation:** In conceptual models of **sepsis**, it is proposed that **histone lactylation** (a metabolite-driven modification) mediates chromatin remodeling  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/biju_2024_dna_methylation_as_an_epigenetic_mechanism_in_the.pdf#Passage 6|[9]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/chen_2026_m_lt_sup_gt_6_lt_sup_gt.pdf#Passage 3|[10]]]. This process may alter the **transcriptional accessibility** of core m6A regulators, including the *ALKBH5* locus, thereby modulating its abundance in response to metabolic stress  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/biju_2024_dna_methylation_as_an_epigenetic_mechanism_in_the.pdf#Passage 6|[9]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/chen_2026_m_lt_sup_gt_6_lt_sup_gt.pdf#Passage 3|[10]]].
*   **Indirect HDAC Involvement:** ALKBH5 regulates the stability of **HDAC4** transcripts  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/biju_2024_dna_methylation_as_an_epigenetic_mechanism_in_the.pdf#Passage 5|[5]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/li_2026_the_role_of_n_lt_sup_gt_6.pdf#Passage 1|[6]]]. While this primarily affects the stabilization of transcription factors like HIF-1$\alpha$, it creates an indirect pathway where HDAC activity facilitates the transactivation of the *ALKBH5* promoter  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/biju_2024_dna_methylation_as_an_epigenetic_mechanism_in_the.pdf#Passage 5|[5]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/li_2026_the_role_of_n_lt_sup_gt_6.pdf#Passage 1|[6]]].

### **3. DNA Methylation**
The sources **do not directly report** specific DNA methylation (hypermethylation or hypomethylation) at the *ALKBH5* promoter itself. They contrast this with other stress-inducible genes like **LEDGF**, which is explicitly shown to undergo aberrant **promoter hypermethylation** at Sp1 binding sites during aging and oxidative stress  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/biju_2024_dna_methylation_as_an_epigenetic_mechanism_in_the.pdf#Passage 1|[11]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/chen_2026_m_lt_sup_gt_6_lt_sup_gt.pdf#Passage 1|[12]]]. Instead, for *ALKBH5*, the sources focus on its role as a regulator of other genes' methylation status or its own post-translational suppression by reactive oxygen species (ROS)  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q18-promotor-alkbh5-estres-envejecimiento/Sources/biju_2024_dna_methylation_as_an_epigenetic_mechanism_in_the.pdf#Passage 6|[9]]].
