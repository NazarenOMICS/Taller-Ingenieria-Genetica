### Passage 1

Fig. 4. The occupancy of H3K27ac at the P4 region regulates the transcription and translation of ZFP42 by dCas9-P300. (A) Schematic diagram of the promoter region sections of pig ZFP42 gene. (B) ChIP-qPCR analysis of ZFP42 promoter showing relative enrichment of H3K27ac in GCs. (C) Landscape of chromatin accessibility in ZFP42 promoter was quantitated by qPCR. (D) The protein levels of ZFP42 and H3K27ac with dCas9-P300 and gRNA (P4) transfection. (E) The mRNA and protein levels of ZFP42 in GCs with dCas9-P300 and gRNA (P4) transfection alone or plus HDAC2 overexpression rescue, untreated cells as control. (F) The occupancy of H3K27ac on the ZFP42 region with dCas9-P300 and gRNA (P4) transfection was detected by ChIP-qPCR. Data are presented as mean ± SD from 3 independent biological replicates (n = 3). Statistical significance was determined by paired two-tailed Student's t-test. *P < 0.05.
