## Q01
- Pregunta: According to these sources, what magnitude of H3K27ac enrichment (relative to baseline) does dCas9-p300 deposit at targeted loci, and by which method (e.g., ChIP-seq, ChIP-qPCR) was it measured?

## Q02
- Pregunta: How far does the H3K27ac deposited by dCas9-p300 spread from the targeted site, according to these sources?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/QA/answers/2026-07-14 Q02 - how-far-does-h3k27ac-deposited-by.md]]

Based on the sources, the H3K27ac deposited by **dCas9-p300** is primarily described as being localized to the **targeted genomic sites**, though its regulatory reach can extend significantly through the three-dimensional organization of the genome.
## Q03
- Pregunta: According to these sources, once the dCas9-p300 effector is withdrawn or inactivated (e.g., via inducible/degron systems), how long does the induced gene activation persist before returning to baseline?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/QA/answers/2026-07-14 Q03 - according-to-these-sources-once-dcas9.md]]

Based on the sources, the duration of gene activation following the withdrawal or inactivation of **dCas9-p300** is determined by the speed at which the effector is cleared from the cell and the relative lack of self-sustaining "epigenetic memory" associated with histone acetylation compared to other marks.
## Q04
- Pregunta: What systems (e.g., drug-inducible, degron, optogenetic) have been used in these sources to achieve temporal control of dCas9-p300 activity, and how precisely can activation be turned on and off?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/QA/answers/2026-07-14 Q04 - what-systems-e-g-drug-inducible.md]]

The sources describe several sophisticated systems for achieving temporal control over CRISPR-based effectors like **dCas9-p300**, ranging from chemical switches to light-activated modules. These systems allow researchers to "gate" activity to specific time windows, though they vary in their speed and levels of background "leakiness."
## Q05
- Pregunta: According to these sources, does the H3K27ac mark itself decay at the same rate as the transcriptional activation once dCas9-p300 is removed, or can epigenetic memory persist after expression returns to baseline?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/QA/answers/2026-07-14 Q05 - according-to-these-sources-does-h3k27ac.md]]

Based on the sources, the **H3K27ac mark is generally characterized as a transient and "flexible" modification** that decays relatively quickly once the synthetic writer (dCas9-p300) is removed, with the resulting transcriptional activation typically returning to baseline  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/minhee_2016_the_epigenome_the_next_substrate_for_engineering.pdf#Passage 8|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/minhee_2016_the_epigenome_the_next_substrate_for_engineering.pdf#Passage 5|[2]]].
## Q06
- Pregunta: What gaps or limitations remain in these sources regarding quantitative kinetics of H3K27ac persistence and decay after dCas9-p300 withdrawal?
- Nota completa: [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/QA/answers/2026-07-14 Q06 - what-gaps-or-limitations-remain-these.md]]

While the sources describe sophisticated tools for controlling the timing of **dCas9-p300** activity, they highlight several significant gaps and limitations regarding the specific **quantitative kinetics of H3K27ac persistence and decay** once the effector is withdrawn.
