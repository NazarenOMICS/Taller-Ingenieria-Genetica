# Q06 - What gaps or limitations remain in these sources regarding quantitative kinetics of H3K27ac persistence and decay after dCas9-p300 withdrawal?

While the sources provide advanced tools for achieving temporal control over **dCas9-p300** activity, they reveal several critical gaps and limitations regarding the quantitative kinetics of **H3K27ac persistence and decay** following effector withdrawal.

### 1. Absence of High-Resolution Decay Curves for the Mark
The most significant limitation is the **lack of granular, time-course data** specifically for the H3K27ac mark itself  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/minhee_2016_the_epigenome_the_next_substrate_for_engineering.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/minhee_2016_the_epigenome_the_next_substrate_for_engineering.pdf#Passage 6|[2]]]. While researchers have established a numerical half-life for the **dCas9 protein** (less than 15 minutes, with total clearance in 1–2 hours using AID systems), a corresponding **half-life for the histone mark** it deposits is missing  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/minhee_2016_the_epigenome_the_next_substrate_for_engineering.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]]. Existing measurements of transcriptional effects often use broad intervals, such as 48 hours post-withdrawal, which is too coarse to define the exact rate at which the acetyl mark returns to baseline  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/minhee_2016_the_epigenome_the_next_substrate_for_engineering.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]].

### 2. The "Complex vs. Mark" Causality Dilemma
The sources identify a fundamental experimental challenge in distinguishing whether sustained gene expression is driven by the **epigenetic mark** or the physical occupancy of the **dCas9-fusion complex**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/minhee_2016_the_epigenome_the_next_substrate_for_engineering.pdf#Passage 6|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/mazhar_2018_the_crispr_tool_kit_for_genome_editing_and.pdf#Passage 2|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/matthew_2019_sufficiency_analysis_of_estrogen_responsive_enhancers_using_synthetic.pdf#Passage 5|[4]]]. Because the synthetic "writer" remains associated with the target site during the activation window, it is "unclear whether the regulatory activity is due to the induced epigenetic mark or the complex"  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/minhee_2016_the_epigenome_the_next_substrate_for_engineering.pdf#Passage 6|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/dirk_2017_drug_tunable_multidimensional_synthetic_gene_control_using_inducible.pdf#Passage 8|[5]]]. This ambiguity prevents researchers from definitively modeling the functional kinetics of the mark in isolation  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/matthew_2019_sufficiency_analysis_of_estrogen_responsive_enhancers_using_synthetic.pdf#Passage 5|[4]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/dirk_2017_drug_tunable_multidimensional_synthetic_gene_control_using_inducible.pdf#Passage 8|[5]]].

### 3. Lack of Comparative mRNA and Histone Mark Kinetics
There is a notable lack of quantitative data comparing the **rate of transcript decay** (mRNA half-life) to the **rate of H3K27ac decay** at the same genomic locus  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/minhee_2016_the_epigenome_the_next_substrate_for_engineering.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/minhee_2016_the_epigenome_the_next_substrate_for_engineering.pdf#Passage 3|[6]]]. Research indicates that H3K27ac levels at distal enhancers are **not fully predictive** of target gene activation levels, further complicating the ability to model mark decay based purely on transcriptional readouts  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/minhee_2016_the_epigenome_the_next_substrate_for_engineering.pdf#Passage 3|[6]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/minhee_2016_the_epigenome_the_next_substrate_for_engineering.pdf#Passage 7|[7]]]. The sources do not establish if there is a temporal lag between the removal of the acetyl mark and the cessation of transcription  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/minhee_2016_the_epigenome_the_next_substrate_for_engineering.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]].

### 4. Contextual Variability and "Eraser" Dynamics
The sources acknowledge that decay rates likely depend on the **local chromatin environment** and the concentration of endogenous "eraser" enzymes (HDACs), but these variables are not fully quantified  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/matthew_2019_sufficiency_analysis_of_estrogen_responsive_enhancers_using_synthetic.pdf#Passage 5|[4]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/daan_2017_dcas9_a_versatile_tool_for_epigenome_editing.pdf#Passage 1|[8]]].
*   **Cell-Type Specificity:** Significant differences in degradation efficiency have been observed between cell lines (e.g., HEK293 vs. CHO cells), suggesting that the "rules" for epigenetic decay may vary across biological contexts in ways that are not yet predictable  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/daan_2017_dcas9_a_versatile_tool_for_epigenome_editing.pdf#Passage 1|[8]]].
*   **Passive vs. Active Decay:** The relative contribution of **passive dilution** (via cell division) versus **active erasure** (via endogenous HDACs) in removing synthetic acetylation marks remains largely untested and unquantified [9, 10].

### 5. Identification of "Short-Term" Memory Thresholds
While histone deacetylation is categorized as a mechanism with **"no memory,"** the sources note that many histone modifications have yet to be rigorously tested for their specific "temporal epigenetic memory"  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/matthew_2019_sufficiency_analysis_of_estrogen_responsive_enhancers_using_synthetic.pdf#Passage 5|[4]]]. Identifying the specific duration that defines "flexible" versus "permanent" memory is cited as a primary goal for future research using these rapid-degradation tools  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/matthew_2019_sufficiency_analysis_of_estrogen_responsive_enhancers_using_synthetic.pdf#Passage 5|[4]]]. Without numerical definitions for these terms, the "short-lived" nature of acetylation remains a qualitative rather than a quantitative observation [9].
