# Q05 - According to these sources, does the H3K27ac mark itself decay at the same rate as the transcriptional activation once dCas9-p300 is removed, or can epigenetic memory persist after expression returns to baseline?

Based on the sources, the **H3K27ac mark is generally characterized as a transient and "flexible" modification** that decays relatively quickly once the synthetic writer (dCas9-p300) is removed, with the resulting transcriptional activation typically returning to baseline  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/minhee_2016_the_epigenome_the_next_substrate_for_engineering.pdf#Passage 8|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/minhee_2016_the_epigenome_the_next_substrate_for_engineering.pdf#Passage 5|[2]]].

### Decay Rates and Reversibility
While the sources do not provide a direct side-by-side numerical comparison of the exact decay rates of the H3K27ac mark versus transcript levels, they offer several insights into the kinetics of this process:
*   **Rapid Abrogation of Transcription:** In systems using the Auxin-Inducible Degron (AID) to clear the dCas9-effector pool, the protein is cleared within **1 to 2 hours**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/dirk_2017_drug_tunable_multidimensional_synthetic_gene_control_using_inducible.pdf#Passage 9|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/dirk_2017_drug_tunable_multidimensional_synthetic_gene_control_using_inducible.pdf#Passage 2|[4]]]. Following this clearance, studies observed that transcriptional upregulation of endogenous target genes was **"largely abrogated"** when measured 48 hours after the start of degradation  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/minhee_2016_the_epigenome_the_next_substrate_for_engineering.pdf#Passage 9|[5]]].
*   **Active vs. Passive Decay:** Once the dCas9-p300 "writer" is removed, the H3K27ac mark decays through **endogenous "eraser" enzymes** (such as HDACs) or via **passive dilution** during cell division  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/minhee_2016_the_epigenome_the_next_substrate_for_engineering.pdf#Passage 5|[2]]].
*   **Minimal Epigenetic Memory:** Unlike DNA methylation or H3K9me3-mediated heterochromatin, which can maintain states for several weeks after effector withdrawal, Source 10 explicitly categories histone deacetylation (the reversal of p300 activity) as having **"no memory"**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/minhee_2016_the_epigenome_the_next_substrate_for_engineering.pdf#Passage 8|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/mazhar_2018_the_crispr_tool_kit_for_genome_editing_and.pdf#Passage 2|[6]]]. Acetylation is thus described as a **"short-term"** memory mechanism that typically reverses before a single cell cycle is completed or within very few divisions  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/minhee_2016_the_epigenome_the_next_substrate_for_engineering.pdf#Passage 5|[2]]].

### Ambiguity of Causal Link
The sources highlight a fundamental difficulty in distinguishing the persistence of the mark from the presence of the effector:
*   **Complex vs. Mark:** One source notes that since the dCas9-fusion complex remains physically associated with the target site during activation, it remains **"unclear whether the regulatory activity is due to the induced epigenetic mark or the complex"**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/jackson_2022_a_histone_deacetylase_network_regulates_epigenetic_reprogramming_and.pdf#Passage 3|[7]]].
*   **Requirement for Maintenance:** This suggests that the persistence of the H3K27ac mark—and its associated transcriptional output—likely depends on the ongoing recruitment of the writer to counteract the continuous activity of endogenous HDAC erasers  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/minhee_2016_the_epigenome_the_next_substrate_for_engineering.pdf#Passage 5|[2]]].

### Context-Dependent "Protracted" Expression
There is evidence that under specific biological conditions, the effects of altering acetylation can be more durable:
*   **Reprogramming Events:** Source 7 describes a "gateway" process in CD4 T cells where interfering with deacetylation during the transition from an active to a resting state leads to **"protracted viral gene expression"** that remains significantly elevated **even 14 days after the removal of the stimulus**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/jackson_2022_a_histone_deacetylase_network_regulates_epigenetic_reprogramming_and.pdf#Passage 4|[8]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/jackson_2022_a_histone_deacetylase_network_regulates_epigenetic_reprogramming_and.pdf#Passage 6|[9]]].
*   **Epigenetic "Locking":** In this context, the persistence is not necessarily due to the H3K27ac mark itself, but because the acetylation **blocks the establishment of repressive marks** (like H3K9me3), effectively reprogramming the locus into a state that is "suboptimal for latency"  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/jackson_2022_a_histone_deacetylase_network_regulates_epigenetic_reprogramming_and.pdf#Passage 5|[10]]]. 

In summary, for standard dCas9-p300 applications, the H3K27ac mark and the induced activation are considered **short-lived and reversible**, requiring the continuous presence of the effector to prevent the mark from being rapidly removed by endogenous cellular machinery  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/minhee_2016_the_epigenome_the_next_substrate_for_engineering.pdf#Passage 5|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/jackson_2022_a_histone_deacetylase_network_regulates_epigenetic_reprogramming_and.pdf#Passage 3|[7]]].
