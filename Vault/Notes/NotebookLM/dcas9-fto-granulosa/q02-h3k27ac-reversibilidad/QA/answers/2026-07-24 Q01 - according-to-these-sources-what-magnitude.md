# Q01 - According to these sources, what magnitude of H3K27ac enrichment (relative to baseline) does dCas9-p300 deposit at targeted loci, and by which method (e.g., ChIP-seq, ChIP-qPCR) was it measured?

Based on the sources, **dCas9-p300** (specifically the catalytic core domain of the human p300 acetyltransferase) deposits **significant and robust enrichment of H3K27ac** at targeted promoters and distal enhancers  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/benjamin_2015_strategies_for_precision_modulation_of_gene_expression_by.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/daan_2017_dcas9_a_versatile_tool_for_epigenome_editing.pdf#Passage 1|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/matthew_2019_sufficiency_analysis_of_estrogen_responsive_enhancers_using_synthetic.pdf#Passage 1|[3]]]. 

### Magnitude of Enrichment
While the sources characterize the magnitude as **"significant"** and **"robust,"** they often define this enrichment relative to the levels observed with endogenous induction or other synthetic activators:
*   **Comparable to Endogenous Induction:** In studies of estrogen-responsive enhancers (ERBSs), dCas9-p300(core) induced H3K27ac at targeted sites to a magnitude **similar to that caused by endogenous 17β-estradiol (E2) treatment**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/matthew_2019_sufficiency_analysis_of_estrogen_responsive_enhancers_using_synthetic.pdf#Passage 2|[4]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/hilton_2015.pdf#Passage 1|[5]]].
*   **Similar to Potent Synthetic Activators:** The levels of H3K27ac enrichment deposited by dCas9-p300 were found to be **statistically similar to those deposited by dCas9-VP16(10x)**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/hilton_2015.pdf#Passage 1|[5]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/gough_2023_dissection_of_a_non_coding_risk_locus_at.pdf#Passage 1|[6]]].
*   **Targeted and Distal Co-enrichment:** Targeting dCas9-p300 to the HS2 enhancer resulted in significant H3K27ac enrichment not only at the target locus but also at **distal HBE and HBG promoters**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/gough_2023_dissection_of_a_non_coding_risk_locus_at.pdf#Passage 2|[7]]].
*   **Potential "Hyper-acetylation":** One source notes that the effector can potentially **"hyper-acetylate"** a targeted region, which may drive the expression of multiple nearby genes that interact with the target site through chromatin looping  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/hilton_2015.pdf#Passage 2|[8]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/liao_2026.pdf#Passage 1|[9]]].

### Measurement Methods
The magnitude of H3K27ac enrichment was quantified using the following primary methods:
*   **ChIP-qPCR (Chromatin Immunoprecipitation followed by quantitative PCR):** This method was used to measure **locus-specific enrichment** at targeted promoters (e.g., *IL1RN*, *ZFP42*) and distal enhancers (e.g., the β-globin HS2 enhancer)  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/daan_2017_dcas9_a_versatile_tool_for_epigenome_editing.pdf#Passage 1|[2]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/gough_2023_dissection_of_a_non_coding_risk_locus_at.pdf#Passage 2|[7]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/gough_2023_dissection_of_a_non_coding_risk_locus_at.pdf#Passage 3|[10]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/gough_2023_dissection_of_a_non_coding_risk_locus_at.pdf#Passage 4|[11]]]. Results were typically expressed as a **fold-change relative to dCas9 controls** or as a percentage of recovery relative to input chromatin  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/gough_2023_dissection_of_a_non_coding_risk_locus_at.pdf#Passage 3|[10]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/matthew_2019_sufficiency_analysis_of_estrogen_responsive_enhancers_using_synthetic.pdf#Passage 3|[12]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/matthew_2019_sufficiency_analysis_of_estrogen_responsive_enhancers_using_synthetic.pdf#Passage 4|[13]]].
*   **ChIP-seq (Chromatin Immunoprecipitation followed by sequencing):** This high-throughput method was used to map the **genome-wide distribution** of H3K27ac and to compare the patterns and fold-changes of synthetic acetylation to those induced by endogenous stimuli  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/hilton_2015.pdf#Passage 1|[5]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/jackson_2022_a_histone_deacetylase_network_regulates_epigenetic_reprogramming_and.pdf#Passage 1|[14]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/jackson_2022_a_histone_deacetylase_network_regulates_epigenetic_reprogramming_and.pdf#Passage 2|[15]]].
*   **CUT&RUN (Cleavage Under Targets and Release Under Nuclease):** While used in some sources to profile general histone modification dynamics in primary cells, this technique is highlighted as a high-resolution alternative for measuring active marks like **H3K27ac and H3K9ac** [17, 18].

> dCas9 The CRISPR/Cas9 system has also been used to alter transcription [79–88]. This has been achieved by targeting with sgRNAs and a catalytically inactivated Cas9 (dCas9), which creates a RNA-based targeting system that can be fused to effector domains. Therefore, dCas9 based epigenetic editing gains the target specificity of CRISPR/Cas9 without causing a double-stranded break and while carrying out the function of the effector domain at the target site (Fig. 1f ). A dCas9 system fused to the KRAB transcription repressing domain has been used to confirm the function of LRP8-Reelin-regulated neuronal enhancers in cortical neurons and lead to the discovery of a novel synapse-to-nucleus pathway related to glutamatergic signaling [89]. However, in the context of transcriptional control the dCas9 system often achieves low effectiveness, which can be improved by the tiling of multiple sgRNAs. Yet another approach to transcription activation via dCas9 has been to fuse two activation domains per dCas9. This system was used to reprogram the cell lineage of stem cells and drive subsequent phenotypes [90]. Also, the histone acetyltransferase p300 can be used as an effector domain to achieve H3K27 acetylation and induce gene expression by targeting the mammalian β-globin locus control region, which is something that could not be achieved by a VP64 domain [91].
