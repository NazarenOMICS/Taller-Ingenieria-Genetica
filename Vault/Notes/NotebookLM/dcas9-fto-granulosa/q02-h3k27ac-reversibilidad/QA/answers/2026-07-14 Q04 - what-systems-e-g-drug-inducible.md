# Q04 - What systems (e.g., drug-inducible, degron, optogenetic) have been used in these sources to achieve temporal control of dCas9-p300 activity, and how precisely can activation be turned on and off?

The sources describe several sophisticated systems for achieving temporal control over CRISPR-based effectors like **dCas9-p300**, ranging from chemical switches to light-activated modules. These systems allow researchers to "gate" activity to specific time windows, though they vary in their speed and levels of background "leakiness."

### 1. Drug-Inducible and Small-Molecule Systems
The most common temporal control involves using small molecules as "keys" to activate or stabilize the dCas9-effector complex.
*   **Tet-on/Doxycycline Systems:** One study successfully integrated a **synNotch-Tet-Cas9:p300 system**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/hongxin_2020_cell_cell_contact_induced_gene_editing_activation_in.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]]. In this configuration, activation of the target gene (e.g., *MYOD*, *CCL19*) occurs only when two conditions are met: the cell makes contact with a specific "sender" cell (spatial control) and **Doxycycline (Dox)** is added to the medium (temporal control)  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/hongxin_2020_cell_cell_contact_induced_gene_editing_activation_in.pdf#Passage 1|[1]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/hongxin_2020_cell_cell_contact_induced_gene_editing_activation_in.pdf#Passage 2|[2]]].
*   **Chemically Induced Dimerization (CID):** These systems use "split" fragments of the dCas9 protein or its effector domain that remain inactive until a small molecule triggers their assembly  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/chenya_2021_spatiotemporal_control_of_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 2|[3]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/daan_2017_dcas9_a_versatile_tool_for_epigenome_editing.pdf#Passage 3|[4]]]. For example, **rapamycin** can be used to induce the heterodimerization of fragments fused to FKBP and FRB domains  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/daan_2017_dcas9_a_versatile_tool_for_epigenome_editing.pdf#Passage 3|[4]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/chenya_2021_spatiotemporal_control_of_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 3|[5]]].
*   **Hormone-Binding Domains:** Some systems sequester the dCas9-effector in the cytoplasm until the addition of a molecule like **4-hydroxytamoxifen (4-HT)**, which triggers its translocation into the nucleus to begin editing or activation  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/chenya_2021_spatiotemporal_control_of_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 4|[6]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/dirk_2017_drug_tunable_multidimensional_synthetic_gene_control_using_inducible.pdf#Passage 4|[7]]].

### 2. Degron-Based Systems (AID and ecDHFR)
Degron systems achieve control by regulating the **stability and degradation** of the protein pool, offering very high precision for "turning off" activation  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/dirk_2017_drug_tunable_multidimensional_synthetic_gene_control_using_inducible.pdf#Passage 5|[8]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/dirk_2017_drug_tunable_multidimensional_synthetic_gene_control_using_inducible.pdf#Passage 6|[9]]].
*   **Auxin-Inducible Degron (AID):** The dCas9-effector is tagged with an IAA17 domain. Upon the addition of the plant hormone **auxin**, the protein is rapidly poly-ubiquitinated and sent to the proteasome for degradation  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/dirk_2017_drug_tunable_multidimensional_synthetic_gene_control_using_inducible.pdf#Passage 7|[10]]]. 
    *   **Precision:** Clearance of the protein pool is extremely fast, taking **1 to 2 hours**, with a protein half-life of **less than 15 minutes**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/dirk_2017_drug_tunable_multidimensional_synthetic_gene_control_using_inducible.pdf#Passage 8|[11]]].
    *   **Reversibility:** After washing out the auxin, detectable protein reappears within **30 minutes**, returning to maximum levels in **3 to 7 hours**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/dirk_2017_drug_tunable_multidimensional_synthetic_gene_control_using_inducible.pdf#Passage 8|[11]]].
*   **ecDHFR (ecDDD) System:** This functions in the opposite direction; the protein is intrinsically unstable and degraded until it is stabilized by the addition of the drug **trimethoprim (TMP)**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/chenya_2021_spatiotemporal_control_of_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 5|[12]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/daan_2017_dcas9_a_versatile_tool_for_epigenome_editing.pdf#Passage 1|[13]]].

### 3. Optogenetic (Light-Controlled) Systems
Optogenetics offers the highest spatial and temporal resolution, using light instead of chemicals to trigger activity  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/chenya_2021_spatiotemporal_control_of_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 6|[14]]].
*   **Blue Light Recruitment:** Using light-sensitive **CRY2 and CIB1** proteins, a full-length dCas9 can be kept at the target site while the p300 effector is only recruited when blue light (approx. 450 nm) is applied  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/chenya_2021_spatiotemporal_control_of_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 7|[15]]].
*   **Magnets (pMag/nMag):** These are photoinducible dimerization domains used to reassemble split-dCas9 fragments in response to blue light  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/chenya_2021_spatiotemporal_control_of_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 7|[15]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/chenya_2021_spatiotemporal_control_of_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 8|[16]]].
*   **Precision:** These processes are described as **rapid and reversible**  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/daan_2017_dcas9_a_versatile_tool_for_epigenome_editing.pdf#Passage 4|[17]]]. Activation is turned on by light exposure and can be reversed simply by returning the cells to a dark environment  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/daan_2017_dcas9_a_versatile_tool_for_epigenome_editing.pdf#Passage 4|[17]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/hongxin_2020_cell_cell_contact_induced_gene_editing_activation_in.pdf#Passage 3|[18]]].

### Precision and Limitations
While these systems offer powerful control, the sources note several precision challenges:
*   **Leakiness (Background Activity):** Several systems, particularly the Tet and CID systems, can suffer from **"leaky" expression** or significant background activity where the gene is activated even in the absence of the inducer  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/chenya_2021_spatiotemporal_control_of_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 9|[19]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/chenya_2021_spatiotemporal_control_of_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 10|[20]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/chenya_2021_spatiotemporal_control_of_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 11|[21]]]. 
*   **Dynamic Range:** Small-molecule activators often have a **limited dynamic range**, making it difficult to achieve a true "zero" state when turned off  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/chenya_2021_spatiotemporal_control_of_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 12|[22]]] [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/chenya_2021_spatiotemporal_control_of_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 13|[23]]].
*   **Toxicity:** Inducers like rapamycin or doxycycline may induce **potential cytotoxicity** or unintended genomic effects over long treatment periods  [[Notes/NotebookLM/dcas9-fto-granulosa/q02-h3k27ac-reversibilidad/Sources/chenya_2021_spatiotemporal_control_of_crispr_cas9_gene_editing.pdf#Passage 12|[22]]].
*   **Light Penetration:** While precise, optogenetic systems are limited in vivo by the **strong absorption and scattering of light** by biological tissues [25, 26].
